### 2. Read-derived destinations

The second write uses the captured quantity for both destination and stored data. It is not based on rereading **Target** after the halfword write.

The doubled-target read follows the earlier writes. If its address overlaps one of their destinations, its result can reflect those writes rather than entry-time memory.
