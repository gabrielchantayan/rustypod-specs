## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

### Opening mask and paired-result separation

For a masked input of 0xFFFFFFFF, the corresponding argument is 0xFFC00000.

If the paired result’s lower portion is 0x00000123 and upper portion is 0x89ABCDEF, the shifted-lower argument is 0x00012300 and the unchanged-upper argument is 0x89ABCDEF, in their respective positions.

### Intermediate truncation exposed by a right shift

Under the 32-bit machine-bitvector interpretation, start with 0x00000081. A left shift by 25 retains 0x02000000. Subsequently shifting that retained value right by two, unsigned, produces 0x00800000.

An unbounded left-shift intermediate would instead be 0x102000000. Right-shifting that value by two and narrowing only afterward produces 0x40800000. That differs and does not preserve the retained 32-bit intermediate.

This example applies conditionally where the represented signed left-shift construction is interpreted as machine-bitvector arithmetic.

### Consecutive left shifts are not distinguishing

For the same starting value, two left shifts by 25 yield zero after final 32-bit narrowing whether narrowing occurs after each shift or only after both. This case cannot demonstrate an intermediate-width mismatch.

### Byte widening and overwrite order

### Terminal intervening store
