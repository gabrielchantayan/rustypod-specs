# F-9b074bc19854

Both modes produce a common header, name-related data, two common records, and three explicit trailer stores. Nonzero mode additionally updates existing fields, produces two further name-and-record groups, and executes two additional trailer stores.

Dependency-mediated output is distinguished from direct stores throughout this contract. An apparent conversion or transfer role does not establish a dependency’s implementation, write extent, ownership behavior, or preservation of other memory.
