# F-d01803c9873e

## Purpose

The established behavior is narrower than a complete object constructor: the function stages three incoming values, invokes two dependencies, and overwrites destination words at byte offsets 0, 4, 8, and 20. It does not directly initialize the intervening words at offsets 12 and 16.

The intended meaning of the destination, the source object, and the three externally stored values used during initialization is unknown. Describing this as initialization is an inference from the writes, not evidence of a particular object type, ownership model, or lifecycle.
