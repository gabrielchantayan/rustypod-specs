### Initial byte selection

If its source-byte read returns `0x80`, the destination is `0x91`, subject to platform address interpretation. F-d011 later receives `0x80` as its first explicit argument without another source-byte read.

### Initial stored word

### Wide-result operations

### Final byte extraction and reread
