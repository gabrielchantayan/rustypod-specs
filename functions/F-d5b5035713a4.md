Arithmetic halfword observations are signed 16-bit values widened for subsequent calculations. Thus `0xffff` contributes −1. Character-read signedness is unresolved; character pattern `0xff` does not establish a unique widened address contribution.

Address-bearing arithmetic is represented at 32-bit width. Doubling, multiplication, addition, and shifts precede the associated narrowing or access. Portable signed overflow, narrowing, shifting, integer-to-address conversion, and universal wraparound are not established.
