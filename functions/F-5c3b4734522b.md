**Established static behavior:** the dependency calls, their argument relationships, the direct access widths and offsets, and their sequential order are visible in the supplied material.

**Inferred intent:** the operation resembles copying a structured record with two leading regions handled by a helper. Neither a complete record-copy guarantee nor the meaning of those leading regions is established without the dependency’s contract.

This function must not be specified simply as an unrestricted 32-byte copy. Such a description would conceal dependency behavior, overlapping-storage effects, and the repeated access at offset 20.
