# F-16354eccf516

- Whether the caller-visible integer, read **after the dependency returns**, differs from the temporary integer.

The source does not establish that the integers represent lengths, capacities, offsets, counts, or any other application-specific quantity. The distinction between the integer supplied to the dependency and the integer used for the final comparison is significant. They are separate reads of caller-accessible storage, with the dependency invocation between them.
