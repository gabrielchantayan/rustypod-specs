### Width and arithmetic rules

Byte stores carry the low eight bits of their payload. Unsigned byte reads can be zero-extended to 32 bits. No direct 64-bit memory store is established.

### Initial mutations and first ambient accesses

The first mutation group begins with a word store at offset 0x14 from the final paired dependency result’s high half. Its payload is the early shifted value. A byte store to early-shifted-value offset 9 follows, carrying the high half’s low byte.

A word store at offset 0x54 from 256 times the final low half carries the scaled base. The offset-9 byte destination is then written again, now with the early shifted value’s low byte.

Next, scaled-base offset 0x14 receives a zero word, and scaled-base offset 5 is read as an unsigned byte. Small-base offset 0x14 receives a zero word before the byte is published, zero-extended, at scaled-base offset 0x34. A word is then read from scaled-base offset 0x14.

Small-base offset 0x14 receives another zero word. Two distinct byte stores follow at small-base offset 0x15, both carrying the low byte of the word just read.

Only after both byte stores does the function read ambient storage at **0x05**, converting its value to unsigned 32 bits for an address base. It then assigns the small base’s explicitly eight-bit-converted value to ambient storage at **0x01**.

The next accesses preserve these relationships:

- The ambient-derived base receives the small component’s low byte at offset 0x15.
- Scaled-base offset 0x11 receives the shifted payload’s low byte.
- The earlier scaled-base-offset-0x14 word is used as a base for a word store at offset 0x54 carrying the shifted payload.
- Scaled-base offset 0x11 is overwritten with a zero byte.
- Ambient-derived-base offset 0x54 is read, establishing the link value, and then overwritten with the earlier scaled-base-offset-0x14 word.
- Small-base offset 0x34 receives the small component as a word.
- Scaled-base offset 0x74 is read.

Neither the 0x05 read nor the link-value read is independent of the preceding writes without an additional nonaliasing contract.

### Link mutations and publication boundaries

The newly loaded scaled-base-offset-0x74 address receives a shifted dependency derivative at offset 0x54. A subsequent self-valued word store through offset 0x54 uses two separately represented reads of small-base offset 0x74. Their intra-expression evaluation order is **documented_only**.

Two pairs of writes—self-valued word stores at link-value offset 0x24 and byte stores at offset 9 of the earlier loaded address—surround another offset-0x54 word store carrying the shifted payload.

Ambient storage at **0x54** then receives the link value shifted left one position. This assignment lies immediately after a shifted dependency derivative is stored at small-base offset 0x14 and immediately before the link value is stored there.

Small-base offset 0x14 receives the link value twice. Two byte stores at small-base offset 0x15 then carry the low byte of four times the link value. Ambient storage at **0x15** subsequently receives an explicit eight-bit conversion of the small base, before the next word store through four-times-link-value offset 0x54.

The intervening region follows word fields at offsets 4, 0x64, and 0x74. It also reads bytes at offset 5 and uses their zero-extended values as bases for word reads at offset 0x74. Writes include self-valued words, scaled link values, and bytes at offsets 9, 0x0D, and 0x15.

### Ambient 0x67 publication and fresh reads

Ambient storage at **0x67** receives the saved 0xA9 value’s explicitly eight-bit-converted payload. A word read from twice-the-constant-seed offset 0x44 intervenes before ambient 0x67 is read and its result assigned to ambient storage at **0x21**.

Before the next ambient 0x67 read, the function:

- Publishes the offset-0x44 word’s low byte at publication-base offset 0x10.
- Stores that word at publication-anchor offset 0x54.

Publication-base offset 0x54 is then read as a word. Another independent ambient **0x67** read supplies a second byte store at publication-base offset 9. Intervening writes may alias ambient 0x67, so these reads cannot be replaced with its initially assigned byte.

### Completion

The word read from publication-base offset 0x54 becomes a late address base. Its offset 0x0D receives the low byte of the previously saved four-times-constant-seed-offset-0x74 word.

The word saved from **twice-the-constant-seed offset 0x14** is then stored at **twice-the-constant-seed offset 0x54**. This copies a saved word between differently offset locations. It is not established as restoration of the destination’s prior state.

A word read from publication-base offset 0x14 follows. Twice-the-constant-seed offset 0x11 receives the same saved offset-0x74 word’s low byte. Publication-base offset 9 receives the late address base’s low byte, and publication-base offset 0x44 receives that base as a word.

A word read from twice-the-constant-seed offset 4 establishes the **terminal base**. The remaining ordering is:

- A 16-bit store of 0x65 at twice the terminal base.
- A word read from terminal-base offset 0x64.
- A byte store at late-address-base offset 0x11, using the earlier publication-base-offset-0x14 read.
- A word store of decimal 100 at late-address-base offset 0x54.
- A word read from terminal-base offset 0x74.
- A word read from twice-the-constant-seed offset 0x64.
- A byte store of the terminal base’s low byte at twice-the-constant-seed offset 9.
- A word store of the terminal-base-offset-0x64 value at offset 0x54 from the latter loaded address.
