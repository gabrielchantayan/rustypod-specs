# F-80f420262fea

- Invokes an initial dependency without explicit arguments.
- Copies a 32-bit word from the beginning of an input record to an address derived from the record’s second 32-bit word.
- Passes the input record and a second argument to a dependency that produces a 64-bit result.
- Passes the upper and lower halves of that result, together with the input record, to a final dependency.

This sequencing and data flow are established by the supplied static evidence. The application-level meaning of the copied word, the addressed storage, and the two result halves is unknown. In particular, the evidence does not establish that the initial dependency initializes anything, that the middle dependency reports a status, or that the final dependency performs cleanup.
