# F-914d412075f9

## Purpose

F-914d412075f9 performs two unconditional dependency invocations associated with a caller-supplied base address, then conditionally transfers control through an indirectly selected target.

The conditional decision uses a pointer-sized field at byte offset 80 from the base and, when that field is non-null, a 32-bit value at the beginning of the referenced storage. Both values are observed after the two unconditional invocations have completed.

The established behavior is this sequence and its conditional transfer. A higher-level purpose—such as initialization, notification, cleanup, or lifecycle management—is not established by the supplied static evidence.
