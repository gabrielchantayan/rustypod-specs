## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Initial forwarding.** With first argument `1` and compatible 32-bit shift semantics, F-opaque-01 receives `128` in its fourth position. The second and third positions retain their original bits.

2. **Truncating word transformation.** A low word of `0x12345678`, shifted left by 8 under 32-bit word semantics, contributes `0x34567800`. Retaining the discarded upper byte in that argument is not equivalent.

3. **Logical high-word extraction.** A wide result whose high word is `0x80000000`, logically shifted right by 34 overall, yields `0x20000000`, not a sign-extended result.

4. **Narrowing and widening.** A byte store supplied with `0x12345678` writes `0x78`. A later unsigned byte sample of `0xFF`, widened to a word, supplies `0x000000FF`.

5. **Intermediate-state sensitivity.** Replacing the `0x20`/`0x2F`/sample/`0x20` mutation history with one final store is unsupported when intermediate accesses are observable or alias the sample’s source.

6. **Low-memory failure.** If execution reaches the literal store at inaccessible address `0x54`, successful completion is not required. Redirecting that store to an allocated record is unsupported.

7. **Shared-byte aliasing.** If an intervening computed store changes the fixed shared byte, a later read uses its changed value. Reusing the earlier captured byte would require additional non-aliasing evidence.

8. **Variable shift boundary.** If the late shift count is outside the defined range of the execution environment, this contract does not require a masked-count result or normal completion.

9. **Terminal visibility.** F-opaque-03 is reached only after the final represented direct store completes. If it returns, that does not imply preservation of memory or globals.
