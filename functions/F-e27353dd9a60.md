# F-e27353dd9a60

## Purpose

F-e27353dd9a60 observes one bit of an integer field at a fixed displacement from an address-bearing input. On normal completion, it returns **0** when bit 6 is clear and **64** when bit 6 is set. Bit numbering starts at zero at the least significant bit.

The result retains the selected bit’s numeric weight. It is not normalized to 0 or 1, and it does not indicate whether the entire field equals 64. Other bits do not contribute to the returned value.

A flag query is the apparent intent. The application meaning of the field, the meaning of bit 6, and the identity of any containing object are not established.
