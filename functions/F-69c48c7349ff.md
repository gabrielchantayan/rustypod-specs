## Behavior

These ordering relationships distinguish the contract from alternatives that capture the global earlier, reverse the writes, or omit one of them.

The ordering is established by the supplied static representation. It does not independently guarantee physical bus-transaction order, visibility to other threads, compiler barriers, or indivisible writes.
