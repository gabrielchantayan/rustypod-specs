A final dependency is annotated as non-returning, but the represented continuation would invoke its returned value as a callable target.

The available evidence does not establish an application-level purpose. Descriptions such as initialization, decoding, cleanup, fault reporting, or successful termination would add unsupported meaning.

This contract distinguishes three evidence classes:

- **Established static evidence:** behavior directly represented in the supplied material.

The requirements describe the represented continuation under execution conditions that support its arithmetic, storage accesses, and calls. They do not establish that any particular environment permits the entire operation to execute.
