# F-ccb8260361ca

## Purpose

F-ccb8260361ca reads two 32-bit words from an input memory region, subtracts the word at byte offset 0 from the word at byte offset 8, and derives quotient and remainder components using a fixed divisor of 12. It returns those components in one 64-bit value: quotient in bits 31–0 and remainder in bits 63–32.

Under the represented fixed-width arithmetic, differences whose signed interpretation is nonnegative produce ordinary division by 12. Differences whose signed interpretation is negative undergo magnitude division followed by conditional sign restoration. The initialization of the sign-control state includes a shift by the apparent operand width, so the supplied representation does not establish an unconditional signed result for that path.

Signed division with truncation toward zero is an inferred intent, not a replacement for that unresolved behavior.

The supplied body does not establish that the loaded words are addresses, that they delimit a collection, or that the quotient counts elements.
