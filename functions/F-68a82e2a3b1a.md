### Scaled addressing and halfword stores

After breakpoint continuation, the retained F-000000000019 result is scaled by four. Separately, logically shifting `0x21C48960` right by 18 and multiplying by four produces the fixed value `0x21C4`.

The word at A plus `0x44` is read and retained as B. F-000000000020 executes, followed by F-000000000021. The latter supplies a word-sized result.

The word at A plus `0x04` is then read. F-000000000022 receives the F-000000000021 result first and this newly read word second.

G2 is read next. Its captured value remains the base used after F-000000000023: a halfword store writes the low 16 bits of that base at the address formed from the base plus four times the retained F-000000000019 result. The stored value is not the scaled index and is not loaded from the destination.

After F-000000000024, the word at `0x2218` is read as C. This fixed address is `0x21C4 + 0x54`.

F-000000000025 supplies a word-sized value used as an address. Halfword `0x21C4` is stored at that address plus `0x04`.

F-000000000026 and F-000000000027 execute before the halfword at C plus `0x34` is read and zero-extended as D. F-000000000028 then supplies the final 64-bit result, whose low and high words are L and H.

The use of A as an address base and `0x2218` as a direct address is explicit. No pointer-table lookup, bounds check, or alternative address reconstruction is established.

### Final updates

The represented arithmetic derives 64 times L, four times H, and four times D. S is adjusted downward by `0xF4`. The word store at S plus `0x258` therefore precedes this adjustment and uses the pre-adjustment base.

L is written as a word at address four times D plus `0x74`. The following sequence then applies:

- F-000000000029 precedes a word store of E at B plus `0x54`.
- F-000000000030 precedes a word store of E at B plus `0x64`.
- F-000000000031 precedes a halfword store of B’s low 16 bits. Its destination combines 64 times L with an associated register-derived address.

Both stores of E are 32-bit writes of a zero-extended 16-bit value, not halfword writes.

F-000000000032 and F-000000000033 execute next. One byte is read at address five times D. After F-000000000034, that captured byte is stored at the address formed from E, the incoming additional register value, and offset `0x19`. The source byte is not reread after the call.

After F-000000000035, four times H is stored as a word at B plus `0x44`. F-000000000036 follows.

Before the final call, the link-like working value is assigned `0x08712031`. The word at address four times H plus `0x44` is read and passed to F-000000000037.
