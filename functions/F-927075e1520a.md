### Quantity calculation

The factors are:

- The signed 16-bit value obtained from descriptor +2.
- The signed 16-bit interpretation of the low half of the 32-bit value obtained from descriptor +8.

Each factor is between −32,768 and 32,767 inclusive. Their product is calculated at signed 32-bit width, then increased by 7. Both operations fit within signed 32-bit range for every possible pair of factors.

The adjusted product is divided by 8 with truncation toward zero. The supplied operations achieve this using a sign-dependent adjustment before a right shift. For negative values, this is not equivalent to simply rounding downward.

The quotient is narrowed to 16 bits. Those bits are interpreted as signed, promoted into the unsigned addition used to add 3, and masked with 0xFFFC. The resulting 16-bit quantity is stored.

An equivalent value-level description of the final stage is:

- Reduce the quotient modulo 65,536.
- Add 3 modulo 65,536.
- Clear its lowest two bits.

Consequently, the stored quantity is always a multiple of four between 0 and 65,532 inclusive. It may wrap and may have its high bit set.

For positive inputs away from narrowing boundaries, the calculation resembles rounding a bit count up to whole bytes and then to a four-byte boundary. That interpretation does not replace the exact signed division and 16-bit reduction rules.
