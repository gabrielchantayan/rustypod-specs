# F-e12b3e8576d7

## Purpose

The established behavior is orchestration: eight preliminary calls, preparation of a ten-word descriptor and a separate output-capable word, creation or acquisition of an intermediate, object-mediated processing, downstream submission, and two final calls. The supplied evidence does not establish the application-level meaning of these operations.

Terms such as “object,” “context,” “descriptor,” and “intermediate” below identify structural roles only. They do not imply a class hierarchy, ownership policy, resource type, or successful allocation.
