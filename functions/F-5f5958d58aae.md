# F-5f5958d58aae

## Purpose

The established behavior is a fixed sequence of three writes. The supplied representation does not establish the meaning of the storage, whether it belongs to a larger object, or whether clearing it constitutes initialization, reset, or disposal. 1. Write a 32-bit zero to the slot beginning at offset 0.
2. Write a 32-bit zero to the slot beginning at offset 4.
3. Write a 32-bit zero to the slot beginning at offset 8.
