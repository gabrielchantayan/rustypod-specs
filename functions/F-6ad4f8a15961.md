# F-6ad4f8a15961

## Purpose

F-6ad4f8a15961 performs an extended sequence of indirect operations, transforms returned and secondary machine-result quantities, makes ordered memory updates, and concludes with an unresolved indirect transfer.

No domain-level purpose is established. Numerical values resembling characters do not establish text processing. Repeated offsets do not establish an object type, collection, or external format.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** explicitly represented calls, transformations, accesses, and ordering.
- **[INFERENCE]:** interpretations requiring assumptions beyond that representation.
- **documented_only:** unresolved behavior without a justified executable acceptance requirement.

All described effects are conditional on execution reaching them. Successful completion and normal return are not guaranteed.

### Arithmetic interpretation

Under a **32-bit machine-bit-vector interpretation**, shifted and multiplied quantities retain only their low 32 bits. Consequently:

- Left shift by eight followed by logical right shift by eight retains the original low 24 bits.
- Successive left shifts by 28 and 28 yield zero.
- Successive left shifts by 31 and 30 yield zero.
- Selecting bits 10–19 yields a quantity from 0 through 1023.
- Selecting the low 24 bits before a left shift by six is not interchangeable with shifting an unrestricted word.
- Right shifts by 34, 36, or 44 of an apparent 64-bit result must operate on that wide result, not merely its low word.

These consequences are conditional on machine-width recovery. Signed type annotations do not independently settle signed overflow or signed left-shift semantics.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

- **Entry forwarding:** With second argument three and a compatible execution context, F-U001 receives the unchanged first argument, three, and 192.
- **Width-sensitive shift:** Under 32-bit bit-vector semantics, second-input bits 0x04000000 shifted left by six produce zero, not a widened 0x100000000.
- **Fixed-byte-derived addressing:** If the read at 0x05 observes 0xFE, the subsequent byte-derived base is 254, not −2. The write at 0x01 still stores only the low byte of the small base.
- **Calculated byte at 0x3A:** If the adjusted quantity’s low byte is 0x26, the fixed byte write receives 0x60 after multiplication, addition, and truncation.
- **Shared-word capture order:** The word read at 0x087FBBBC occurs after the final word store of 0x20 at work base plus 0x20 and before the fixed 32-bit store of 0x20 at 0x60.
- **Distinct shared-byte reads:** If ordinary storage at 0x67 receives 0xAB and remains unchanged through the copy to 0x21, that copy receives 0xAB. If an intervening computed store subsequently changes 0x67 to 0x44, the next read used for work base plus nine observes 0x44. Reusing the original 0xAB is not equivalent.
- **Ordered overwrites:** An access observer must distinguish the word stores 0x20, 0x2F, byte-derived value, and 0x20 at the constant-store destination. Final-value equivalence alone is insufficient.
- **Partial failure:** If a later access fails after earlier writes succeed, no rollback is required. The exact fault mechanism is unresolved.
- **Terminal boundary:** Reaching F-U003 establishes the four represented arguments, not a success result or normal return.
