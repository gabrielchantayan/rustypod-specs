# F-88ba56c8f65e

## Purpose

F-88ba56c8f65e is a synchronous, two-input operation that delegates preparation to one dependency and selects a subsequent call sequence according to that dependency’s integer result.

Its established behavior is a branch-and-return contract:

- A zero preparation result causes a context-like value to be obtained and passed, together with prepared data and the original inputs, to a second operation. The second operation’s result becomes the function’s result.
- A nonzero preparation result causes two other dependency invocations in order. Their results do not replace the preparation result.

“Preparation,” “context-like value,” and “second operation” describe the apparent roles of these calls, not established domain semantics. The supplied evidence does not establish whether the function creates, registers, sends, validates, or releases any resource.
