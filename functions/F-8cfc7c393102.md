# F-8cfc7c393102

## Purpose

### Opening mutations

The opening effects occur in this order: a word at final-high-result plus 0x14 receives the link word; a byte at link-word plus 0x09 receives the low byte of the high result; a word at 256 times the final low result plus 0x54 receives the scaled base; and the link-relative byte is overwritten with the low byte of the link word.

The body clears the scaled-base word at offset 0x14, captures a byte from scaled base plus 0x05, clears the index-base word at offset 0x14, and publishes the captured byte as a zero-extended word at scaled base plus 0x34.

It then rereads scaled base plus 0x14, clears index base plus 0x14 again, and performs two distinct byte stores at index base plus 0x15 using the reread word’s low byte.

The reread must not be replaced with an assumed zero. Aliasing or special-memory behavior can change its value. Repeated stores remain separate represented events.

### Saved-word propagation and fixed-address mutations

A byte from fixed address 0x05 is captured as a zero-extended address base. Fixed byte 0x01 then receives the low byte of the index base, before subsequent accesses relative to the captured byte-derived base.

The body writes the small index’s low byte at that base plus 0x15. It writes the aligned word’s low byte at scaled base plus 0x11, publishes the aligned word through the earlier reread word at offset 0x54, and overwrites the scaled-base-relative byte with zero.

The old word at byte-derived base plus 0x54 is captured as the **saved word**, before that location is replaced with the earlier scaled-base offset-0x14 capture. The saved word remains retained; its later uses are not reloads of the replaced location.

Subsequent propagation includes an index-base offset-0x34 word receiving the small index, a word loaded from scaled base plus 0x74 becoming another base, and a word store using two separate reads from index base plus 0x74. For that last store, one read supplies the destination base and the other supplies the word written at offset 0x54. Their relative evaluation order within the expression is not established; a single common capture is not represented.

Two self-relative word publications at saved word plus 0x24, interleaved with byte publications at the loaded base plus 0x09, remain distinct. The loaded base’s offset-0x54 word is also replaced.

The following ordering is established around the fixed word at 0x54:

- Index base plus 0x14 first receives the scaled-base-producing result shifted left by twenty-six bits.
- **Fixed address 0x54 receives a 32-bit word equal to the saved word shifted left by one bit.**
- Index base plus 0x14 then receives the saved word twice, as two separate word stores.
- Index base plus 0x15 receives the low byte of four times the saved word twice.

Thus the fixed 0x54 mutation precedes both saved-word stores, and the fixed 0x15 mutation follows both later byte stores. Neither fixed access may be omitted or moved on an assumption of disjoint storage.
