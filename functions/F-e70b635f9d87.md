The first word of the temporary storage is initialized to zero. The second word is not initialized by this function. Neither word is inspected after the callback returns.

The established behavior is conditional dispatch, not a particular application-level operation. Interpreting the fixed argument as an event identifier, or the temporary storage as an output structure, is inferred intent rather than an established contract.
