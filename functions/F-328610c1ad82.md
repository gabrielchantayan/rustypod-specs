## Behavior

The lower component depends only on the second argument. The order of operations matters: the right shift by nine occurs before the left shift by one. The expression does not simply preserve the argument with selected bits cleared.

**2 × floor(second argument ÷ 512).**

This produces an even, nonnegative value. Across the nonnegative signed 32-bit input range, its maximum is 8,388,606. The final left shift therefore does not overflow for those inputs.
