# F-8a4515a6add2

## Purpose

F-8a4515a6add2 coordinates two lookups, captures a byte from the second lookup result, invokes several dependencies using two local storage regions, performs two explicit cleanup calls, and returns the final contents of the first local region as a 64-bit value.

The established behavior is the ordering and dataflow of these operations. The supplied static evidence does not establish the application-level meaning of the lookups, the local values, the captured byte, or the final four-argument operation.

An interpretation involving copying and releasing managed values is plausible, but is inferred intent rather than a guaranteed ownership contract.
