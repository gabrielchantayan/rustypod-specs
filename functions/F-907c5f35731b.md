These examples are conditional consequences of static dataflow, not executed tests. Each assumes the necessary preceding operations complete and relevant accesses are valid.

By contrast, if the frame-like register has value \(S\) at the `0x26C` store and F-C10 changes it to \(T\), the subsequent `0x320` read uses \(T+\texttt{0x320}\), not \(S+\texttt{0x320}\).

### Equal constructed targets

### Halfword-derived addresses

### Breakpoint and store count

If execution stops permanently at breakpoint `0x92`, the ten post-breakpoint stores are not reached. Subject to successful preceding operations, five explicit stores have occurred.

A requirement that all **15 explicit stores** occur must establish breakpoint continuation and successful completion of the remaining sequence.

### Conditional frame recurrence

Only if preservation also holds through F-C29–F-C37 does the next traversal begin with that value. Without those assumptions, the unconditional recurrence is not established.
