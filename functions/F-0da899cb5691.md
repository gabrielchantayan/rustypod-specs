### Constants and the association-base boundary

For this interval, define descriptive mathematical quantities:

- **Displaced word:** the word saved before replacement at the earlier byte-derived base plus 0x54.
- **Adjusted base:** four times the displaced word, minus 0x96.
- **Association destination:** four times the adjusted base.
- **Payload base:** four times a word obtained through a byte-derived address.
- **Paired base:** twice that same obtained word.

These are value relationships, not object or ownership declarations.

The function writes 0x3A as a byte at the payload base, as a halfword at twice the payload base, and as a word at the payload base plus 0x20.

At the association destination plus 0x20, word stores occur in this order: 0x20, 0x2F, a zero-extended byte read from the association destination plus 4, and 0x20 again. That byte read occurs after the first two stores.

**After this sequence, the dedicated shared word supplying the association base is loaded. Only afterward is the word at fixed address 0x60 assigned 0x20.**

The association-base load therefore precedes the 0x60 store. The base is not loaded after completion of an independently movable constant-writing interval. If the dedicated shared word overlaps 0x60, the captured base reflects memory before that store, subject to earlier effects. The subsequent association writes use the captured word rather than reloading it after the 0x60 write.
