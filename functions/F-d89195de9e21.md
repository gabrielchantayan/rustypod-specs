# F-d89195de9e21

## Purpose

F-d89195de9e21 coordinates five dependency calls and then conditionally copies one 32-bit value from an input record into a selector-specific field of a destination object.

The direct assignment recognizes ten consecutive selector values, 22 through 31 inclusive. Each selects one of ten consecutive 4-byte destination fields, beginning at byte offset 124 and ending at byte offset 160. Any other selector produces no direct destination-field assignment by this function.

The dependency calls occur before the selector is read. Consequently, dispatch depends on the record contents that exist after those calls return, not necessarily the contents present at entry.

**Established behavior:** ordered dependency invocation followed by the conditional field assignment described below.

**Inferred intent:** the record appears to represent a property selector and its associated value, while the destination appears to contain corresponding property fields. The supplied evidence does not establish property names, application-level types, or the purposes of the preliminary calls.
