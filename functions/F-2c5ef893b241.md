### Arithmetic boundaries

Masks and intermediate truncations are significant. Wider calculations that preserve discarded bits are not equivalent.

A near-final result is shifted left by eight bits, restricted to its low 15 bits, and shifted right by six. The resulting **restricted index** ranges over multiples of four from 0 through 508. Its double ranges over multiples of eight from 0 through 1016.

The scaled-base offset-0x14 word is cleared. The ordering around the following captures and zero stores is specifically:

- The byte at scaled-base offset 5 is captured.
- The **first** zero-word store to the location at twice the restricted index plus 0x14 occurs **after that byte read but before** its widened value is stored at scaled-base offset 0x34.
