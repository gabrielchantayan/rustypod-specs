# F-404297869b7c

## Purpose

F-404297869b7c combines two paired numerical contributions and returns a signed, quantized result. The first two inputs form one contribution; the last two form the other. Each contribution is the mathematical product of its pair.

For inputs within the defined arithmetic domain and with a nonnegative combined contribution, the result is the number of complete 32,768-unit groups in that combined contribution. Quantization applies to the combined amount, not separately to each pair. Remainders from the two contributions can therefore accumulate into an additional group.

This is not a checked or saturating operation. Representability of intermediate arithmetic is a prerequisite even when the mathematical final result would be small.

A fixed-point application is plausible **inferred intent**. Application units, scaling conventions, and coefficient meanings are not established.

## Behavior

### Nonnegative totals

For a defined invocation with a nonnegative total, the result counts complete groups of 32,768:

| Total | Return |
|---|---:|
| 0 through 32,767 | 0 |
| 32,768 through 65,535 | 1 |
| 65,536 through 98,303 | 2 |

Only portions of these intervals that satisfy the arithmetic preconditions are applicable in a particular environment.

There is no rounding-to-nearest adjustment. A total of 65,535 returns 1.

Both contributions share one quantization boundary. Contributions of 16,384 each produce a result of 1 together, although either contribution combined with zero would produce 0.

Within one arithmetic environment, defined invocations with the same nonnegative total return the same value. Redistributing that total between the pairs can nevertheless change whether the invocation is defined, because each individual contribution must remain representable.

### Signs and cancellation

Negative inputs and negative contributions are not inherently invalid.

When the total is nonnegative, negative contributions introduce no additional negative-shift dependency. For example, a contribution of −32,768 combined with a contribution of 65,536 produces the same result as a total of 32,768 formed entirely from nonnegative contributions.

Exact cancellation returns zero when both contributions are representable and the shift width is valid. Contributions may individually exceed the narrower range permitted for the total.

For a negative total, exact results depend on the environment. Under a **sign-preserving, downward-rounding right-shift profile**, the result is the total measured in 32,768-unit groups, rounded toward negative infinity. Under that profile:

- A total of −1 returns −1.
- A total of −32,768 returns −1.
- A total of −32,769 returns −2.

These are conditional requirements, not universal promises. Truncation toward zero is not equivalent to this profile.
