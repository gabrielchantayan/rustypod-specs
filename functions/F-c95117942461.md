# F-c95117942461

## Purpose

The established behavior is the ordering of these operations and the conditions governing the field transfer. The meanings of the objects, fields, and dependency operations are not established.

The function returns the 32-bit result of the primary operation. Subsequent operations do not replace that saved result on a normal return, although their side effects may affect the surrounding system.
