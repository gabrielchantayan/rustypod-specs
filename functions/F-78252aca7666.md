# F-78252aca7666

## Purpose

The established behavior is an ordered sequence of calls, reads, and fixed-width stores. It is not a whole-object clear. Some four-byte fields receive zero, some receive `0xFFFFFFFF`, and three receive one. Several additional resets affect only one byte.

**Inferred intent:** this may be a lifecycle reset that replaces an auxiliary object and establishes an owner backlink. Field meanings, allocation behavior, destruction behavior, and ownership transfer are not established.
