# F-d0e89c240395

## Purpose

Dispatch requires both of the following observations, made after the payload write:

- The record’s first four-byte field represents a non-null pointer.
- The four-byte integer read through that pointer is nonzero.

Only when both conditions hold does the function read the target-selection location at **0x08037e14** and transfer control through its contents. The location is known; its contents and the selected target’s identity are not.

The established behavior is a store followed by conditionally enabled dispatch. Any application-level purpose, such as notification, initialization, or registration, remains inferred rather than established.
