# F-922db24c0798

## Purpose

Its established behavior is an ordered sequence: initialize a temporary 32-bit slot, obtain an intermediate value, obtain a destination address, pass the same temporary slot to a third dependency, read a global 32-bit value, and perform two destination writes.

The evidence does not establish allocation, construction, cleanup, ownership transfer, or the semantic meaning of either written field.

This contract describes the operations visible in the supplied static representation. Dependency internals and environment-specific behavior remain unresolved.
