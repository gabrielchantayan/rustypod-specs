# F-9fa1cf930086

## Purpose

For a difference whose high bit is clear, the established result is an unsigned quotient and remainder: the quotient occupies the low 32 bits, and the remainder occupies the high 32 bits.

For a difference whose high bit is set, the routine first forms its unsigned two’s-complement magnitude and performs the same division. Signed division with truncation toward zero is the inferred intent, not an unconditional guarantee of this contract.

The divisor is fixed at twelve. The routine does not accept a caller-selected divisor.
