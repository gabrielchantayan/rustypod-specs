The final dependency receives an address displaced 252 bytes from that pointer. The function returns the final dependency’s integer result after subtracting 252.

## Behavior

This ordering matters even when the two word sources commonly contain stable values: the first dependency has no established obligation to preserve the source of the later write.

The final dependency is called after the second dependency returns. There are no visible conditional branches selecting among these calls.

This contract specifies ordering within the supplied function. It does not establish cross-thread observation rules, memory barriers, or atomicity.

### Leading-word visibility

These are guarantees about the function’s preceding writes, not preservation guarantees for either call. A dependency may change that location during its execution; its implementation is unavailable.

The initial leading contents therefore have no explicit role in the visible computation. This does not mean they are irrelevant to all possible aliasing or dependency behavior.
