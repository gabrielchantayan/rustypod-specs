- **Scaled result:** four times that result.
- **Word-slot address:** four times that result, plus 20 bytes.
- **Wide-stride address:** sixteen times that result, plus 20 bytes.

These are arithmetic descriptions, not asserted object layouts. In particular, the word-slot and wide-stride addresses generally differ.

The representation mixes unsigned operations with signed-looking intermediate arithmetic. Operations explicitly on 32-bit unsigned values retain their low 32 bits. 10. **Complete visible access accounting.** If the entire prefix is reached without interruption, the explicit store sequence contains seven word-slot stores, five wide-stride stores, one fourth-input-derived-address word store, and two byte stores, regardless of address aliasing.
