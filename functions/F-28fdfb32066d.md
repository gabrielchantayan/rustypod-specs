## Side effects

Consequently:

- A later read may observe an earlier write.
- Partially overlapping writes may alter later pointer values.
- A write through a derived address may affect absolute state or another record.
- Captured snapshots retain their captured values even if the original memory subsequently changes.
- Equal repeated values do not prove repeated writes are redundant.
