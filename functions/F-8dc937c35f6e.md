## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static examples, not executed tests.

1. **Incoming bits.** With incoming unsigned value `0x00008000`, shifting right by 15 yields one. The described early arguments include `8`, `0x80000000`, and `0x00010000`. Shifting the bit-31 value left another 30 places yields zero under 32-bit truncation.

2. **Index boundary.** A small-index source of `0x7F` produces `0x1FC`, whose double is `0x3F8`. A source of `0x80` produces zero because the mask excludes its shifted set bit.

3. **Initial reread.** Acceptance preserves the scaled-call clear, byte read, doubled-index clear, byte-derived word publication, reread, second doubled-index clear, and two byte stores. Moving both doubled-index clears before the reread is not equivalent.

4. **Low-address publication.** A doubled-index base of `0x3F8` publishes `0xF8` at absolute addresses `0x01` and `0x15`. The first follows the absolute `0x05` read; the second follows the specified intervening link-derived stores.

5. **Truncated word.** A link value of `0x80000001` produces `0x00000002` at absolute address `0x54` under truncating arithmetic.

6. **Global sample.** If the four adjusted-base stores modify the publication-base global through aliasing, the subsequent sample observes the then-current word. That sample still precedes the absolute `0x60` store.

7. **Terminal read boundary.** If absolute `0x3A` overlaps a later retained word source, that read includes the modified byte according to target byte order. Earlier retained reads are not retroactively changed.

8. **Three-access interleaving.** Suppose publication-base plus `0x74` equals adjusted-base plus `0x10`, the retained adjusted `0x04` word is `0x11223344`, and the adjusted `0x40` read yields `0x55667788`. 9. **Retention across aliasing.** If the intervening publication `0x74` store overlaps adjusted offset `0x40`, the later adjusted `0x10` store still uses the word read before that publication, not a fresh read.

10. **Dispatch and faults.** An inaccessible loaded address has no visible fallback. If final dispatch is reached, its arguments are the specified constant and retained words, not inferred buffers or ownership handles.
