### Absolute 0x3A: possible and impossible overlap

A propagation word of 0x40 gives adjusted ingredient 0x6A and adjusted base 0x1A8. The absolute-0x3A byte assignment writes 0x80, the low byte of \(8 \times 0x6A + 0x30 = 0x380\).

The adjusted-base offset-4 word sample instead spans 0x1AC through 0x1AF. It does not include 0x3A. More generally, no propagation word makes this particular adjusted-base sample overlap 0x3A under the conditional model, because their address residues differ.
