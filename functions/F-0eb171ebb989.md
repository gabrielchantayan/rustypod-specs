# F-0eb171ebb989

## Purpose

F-0eb171ebb989 is a read-only predicate on an integer field at a fixed displacement from a caller-supplied, address-bearing value. Its result is true exactly when that field has the signed integer value −1.

The contract concerns the field’s value, not the validity or meaning of its containing object. Neither result certifies that the object is initialized, usable, owned by the caller, or in a particular lifecycle state.

**Established static evidence:** a fixed-displacement integer access determines a Boolean equality result.

**Inferred intent:** the field may hold a sentinel. The meaning of that sentinel is not established. Terms such as “invalid,” “empty,” “finished,” or “uninitialized” would add unsupported domain semantics.
