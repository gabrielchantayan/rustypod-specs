# F-d7460c3d4a39

## Purpose

No application-level purpose, object layout, protocol, or resource lifecycle is established. The recurring displacement of 20 bytes identifies address arithmetic, not a proven structure field. Dependency arguments and results are not established pointers or allocated objects.

This contract distinguishes:
