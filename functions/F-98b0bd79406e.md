# F-98b0bd79406e

## Purpose

The established behavior is the branch, the explicit zero write, the dependency arguments, and their ordering. The application-level meaning of the decision value, the temporary state, and the resulting output is not established.

A possible intent is to initialize, use, and finalize a temporary context when some prerequisite is available. That interpretation is inferred from the call sequence, not established by the dependencies’ contracts. In particular, the last call must not be described as a memory release or successful cleanup without additional evidence.
