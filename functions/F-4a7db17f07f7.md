## Acceptance examples

### Split-result forwarding

### Byte widening and information loss

A byte value of 255 captured at anchor plus 5 is published as word value 255, not a sign-extended negative value.

Under 32-bit truncating arithmetic, a value shifted left by 25 and then left by seven becomes zero. A widened computation retaining the discarded bits does not satisfy that condition.

### Publication capture before address 96

If execution reaches the final workspace-plus-32 store, that word store precedes the publication-base read, which precedes the word store of 32 at address 96.

If the publication-base word contains value 512 when read, subsequent publication-derived addresses use that captured 512. Reloading after the address-96 store is not an equivalent capture rule.

### Address-58 interleaving

The earlier fixed capture and link capture retain their already captured values.

### Arithmetically valid address-103 feedback

Suppose also that the initial address-103 store writes 7, the intervening effects before the second read leave address 103 unchanged, and the signed shift is supported. The first and second reads then obtain 7.
