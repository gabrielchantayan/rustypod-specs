On its first iteration, the destination starts at working offset 19. On subsequent iterations it starts at offset 0.

Let the resulting destination offset be the payload offset. The operation amount is selected as follows:

- Calculate the capacity-related value minus the payload offset and retain its low 16 bits.
- Compare that allowance with the unsigned 32-bit remaining length.
- Select the allowance unless remaining length is no greater; in that case select remaining length's low 16 bits.

Because the latter branch requires remaining length to be at most 65,535, its mask does not further reduce the selected value.

After F-U08 returns:

2. The source-address value advances by the amount.
3. Remaining length decreases by the amount.
4. F-cd7e1905512f receives the retained descriptor word, zero, retained signed descriptor byte, selected code, and working address.
5. A nonzero 32-bit submission result causes F-256e8a77aa59 to receive that result and zero.

The second iteration calls F-3a7a47316439 at offset 0 with counter 1, then F-U08 at offset 2 with source advanced by 13 and amount 7. Exactly two submissions follow those respective operations.
