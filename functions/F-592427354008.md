## Behavior

### Visible execution order

The established static sequence is:

1. A software breakpoint operation carrying operand 46.
2. If execution proceeds to the next visible operation, an indirect invocation at a target derived from the incoming link-register-associated value plus 4,094, with three explicit zero-valued arguments.
3. If that invocation returns to the visible continuation, arrival at a point represented as invalid instruction data.

This ordering is the central observable constraint. The representation contains no alternative branch that bypasses the breakpoint and directly performs the indirect invocation.

The sequence does not guarantee that all three stages occur during any particular execution. A breakpoint may transfer control elsewhere or prevent continuation. The indirect target may fail to execute or may not return. The invalid-instruction boundary prevents a reliable account of later behavior.
