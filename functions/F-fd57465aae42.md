#### Shared byte at 0x67: assignment and three separate reads

- After the assignment, an offset-0x44 word is read from the doubled linked base. The first read of 0x67 then supplies a byte store to low address 0x21.
- Later, following computed-address mutations and a word copy, the second read of 0x67 supplies a byte store at offset 0x09 of the computed publication base.
