**Established static evidence:** the supplied representation exposes argument forwarding, arithmetic relationships, explicit widths for computed-address dereferences, captured-value reuse, and relative ordering of evaluations. It does not establish an application-level purpose.

Their addresses identify evaluation sites, but their names, surrounding conversions, and receiving values do **not** establish their storage widths or signedness. Under a **32-bit modular bit-vector premise**, left shifts discard bits above bit 31, eight-bit conversions retain the low eight bits, and unsigned right shifts introduce zeros.
