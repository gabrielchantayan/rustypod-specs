# F-4508920d541a

## Purpose

The established behavior is the ordering of dependency calls, the values supplied to them, the two nonzero checks, and the returned word. The supplied material does not establish the application-level meaning of the inputs, the working value, the intermediate result, or the final operation.

Preparation, conversion, cleanup, and context access are useful descriptions of apparent roles, not verified dependency semantics. In particular, no allocation, ownership transfer, successful destruction, or preservation of global state can be guaranteed from this function alone.
