## Behavior

Conditional arithmetic conclusions below use 32-bit word and byte-address arithmetic modulo \(2^{32}\). Products, address additions, and left shifts retain their low 32 bits. Unsigned right shifts insert zeros. This model must not be confused with portable signed-overflow or signed-left-shift semantics.

Several extractions shift unsigned 64-bit results right by 34, 36, or 44 bits before narrowing. Those are not oversized shifts of 32-bit operands.

A late byte store instead uses a signed right shift with a count taken from the low eight bits of a computed address. Counts exceeding 31 are possible. Neither count masking nor a particular out-of-range result is established.
