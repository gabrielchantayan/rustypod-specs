If true, the low 16 bits of the context word are first stored at the initial pair’s low half plus 42 bytes. Only after this store does the routine compare two Boolean conditions:

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static cases, not executed tests. Each assumes all preceding accesses and calls complete.

1. **Early unrecovered path.** With zero incoming condition state, the load address is the third argument plus F-unresolved-03’s high half. The converted halfword is written at its low half plus 60 before the unrecovered boundary. Successful return is not established.

2. **Unsigned predicate boundary.** A result of zero from F-unresolved-05 makes the predicate false. A signed result of −33,280 shifts to −65 and makes it true; −33,281 shifts to −66 and makes it false.

3. **Store before initial route selection.** When the predicate is true, the halfword store at the initial pair’s low half plus 42 occurs even when the Boolean comparison immediately selects the common tail.

4. **Distinct sign and overflow operands.** With an initial pair high half of zero, subtracting 47 does not overflow. A nonnegative context word therefore selects the common tail; a negative context word selects direct loop entry.

5. **Direct initial loop target.** If initial selection enters the loop body directly, its first F-unresolved-07 call uses target `0x085737FC`. If execution later completes the common tail with nonzero continuation control, the next invocation uses `0x08573900`.

6. **First loop entry through the tail.** If initial selection enters the common tail and its continuation control is nonzero, the first F-unresolved-07 invocation uses `0x08573900`, not the direct-entry target.

7. **Loop early-return boundary.** An incremented working value of `0x00008000` produces a negative 32-bit product with 65,536 and selects the return. A value of `0x00010000` produces zero and does not. Both cases first perform the word store at the old working value plus 62.

8. **Post-call continuation.** Zero continuation control, read after F-unresolved-16, suppresses the square-derived store and selects the return. Nonzero control causes that store and another loop-body entry, subject to subsequent faults or dependency behavior.

9. **Return packing.** A selected low-half input of `0x80000001` produces low result `0x00000002`. A return-related global value of 84 produces high result zero.
