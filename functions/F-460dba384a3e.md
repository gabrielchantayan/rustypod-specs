The output is the full arithmetic difference when that difference is representable. It is not restricted to an ordering category, a Boolean, or an absolute distance. Zero and negative results are normal results, not failure indications.

Changing only the intervening value cannot affect the required result while the operands and access conditions remain unchanged.

Exchanging the operand values reverses the mathematical difference, but each invocation needs its own range check. A representable negative difference does not imply that its positive counterpart is representable. This distinction matters for signed ranges whose negative and positive bounds have different magnitudes.

No explicit widening, saturation, unsigned conversion, or overflow check appears in the supplied definition.
