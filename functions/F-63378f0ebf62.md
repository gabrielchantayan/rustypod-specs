## Behavior

### Opening publication and first shift

The opening access sequence is:

1. Read a word at the third argument plus `0x74`.
2. Store that word at Base A plus `0x54`.
3. Store its low byte at Base C plus `0x11`.
4. Read and retain the word at Base B plus `0x44`.
5. Read a signed byte at twice Base A; sign-extend it into a word store at Base B plus `0x54`.
6. Read that destination back.
7. Store Payload at offset `0x54` relative to the opening word’s address value.
8. Read the word at Base C plus `0x64`.
9. Store Base B’s low byte at offset `0x09` relative to the read-back value.

External slot **E01**, at `0x087fc4dc`, is then referenced three times: first to capture its value, again to locate a word read at offset `0x54`, and again to locate a word replacement at that offset. The replacement is the word obtained from Base C plus `0x64`. These references must not be collapsed into an immutable snapshot.

The displaced word supplies a read at offset `0x44`. The **first shift** right-shifts the 32-bit value `0x2c` by the low eight bits of the first E01 value. Its low-byte result is stored:

- At offset `0x08` relative to that newly reached address.
- Subsequently at offset `0x11` relative to the displaced word.

Between these byte stores, the earlier Base B word is stored at the displaced word’s `0x74` field.

Other opening writes include two consecutive word stores at Base C plus `0x54`: first the word from Base C plus `0x64`, then the fourth argument. Both accesses matter. The sequence also writes a byte to absolute address `0x6e`, stores word `0x65` at two destinations, and uses a zero-extended byte from offset `0x05` as an address base. A previously written `0x74` field is reloaded before further replacements.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static obligations, not runtime results.

- **Opening publication:** If the opening word is `0x123456ab`, the first two stores publish word `0x123456ab` and byte `0xab`, in that order.
- **Signed propagation:** An opening signed-byte read of `0x80` produces word `0xffffff80`.
- **First shift:** If the first E01 value has low byte `2`, the published shift byte is `0x0b`. Later E01 references need not have the same value.
- **Third shift:** With signed byte `0x80` and shift count `1`, ordinary 32-bit arithmetic right-shift semantics produce a published byte of `0xc0` at both specified destinations.
- **Distinct comparison boundaries:** Equality makes Transfer target ≤ Derived base true, but makes Transfer target < `0x46` false when the target equals `0x46`.
- **Carry condition:** Carry input `0x80000000` makes Doubling carry true regardless of Comparison bit. E18 equal to `0xffffffa7` makes the threshold alternative true and skips the conditional extra E18 reference.
- **External publication order:** E15’s `0x54` halfword store precedes the first E16 and E17 reads. Aliasing may therefore change the values those reads obtain.
- **Negative final byte:** Final byte `0xff` converts to `0xffffffff`; OR with `0x44` leaves the first transfer argument unchanged.
- **Failure:** A fault before dispatch does not imply reversal of completed stores or delivery of an explicit error result.
