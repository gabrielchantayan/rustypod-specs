# F-d33de3836df0

No application-level purpose is established. The visible operations do not establish allocation, initialization, destruction, serialization, or ownership transfer.

A **four-call run** consists of four consecutive calls with no displayed explicit arguments. Unless stated otherwise, the run’s result means the fourth call’s displayed result.

8. **Repeated absolute clears:** Every reached absolute-address clear writes a fresh zero word at `0x24`, even if dependencies changed that location after a previous clear. Later calls may change it again.
