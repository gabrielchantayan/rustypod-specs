# F-d356a9f046c3

Its direct effects include byte clears, 32-bit slot updates, copies of low bytes from words at offset `0x74`, two assignments to shared state, and a terminal 16-bit clear. No application-level purpose is established. Allocation, initialization, dispatch, and cleanup are not supported semantic classifications.

### Widths and descriptive labels

The following labels describe captured values, not implementation identifiers:

- **Linked words A, B, C, and D:** successive word reads described below.
- **Payload P:** the full word captured in phase 13.

A **slot selected by a value** is the word address obtained by multiplying that value by four and adding `0x14`.

A **byte-selected write from a base** first reads an unsigned byte at that base plus `1`, then writes a byte at the address equal to the selected byte plus `0x11`. The destination is not the original base plus `0x11`.

Unsigned right shifts are zero-filling. Some left shifts, multiplications, and additions use represented signed 32-bit values. Their overflow behavior is unresolved; formulas involving them are conditional on the applicable arithmetic semantics.
