The supplied static representation does not establish an application-level purpose, such as initialization, cleanup, decoding, or event handling. Those interpretations are not part of this contract.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** operations and ordering explicitly represented in the supplied material.
- **Inferred intent:** interpretations consistent with those operations but not independently established.
- **documented_only:** unresolved behavior that must remain unspecified until additional evidence exists.

The contract is conditional on the accuracy of the supplied representation. In particular, the final transfer was not successfully recovered as an ordinary control-flow construct.
