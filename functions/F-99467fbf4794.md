# F-99467fbf4794

## Purpose

The established behavior is structural rather than semantic: the function writes two four-byte values, invokes two direct dependencies in order, checks a pointer-valued slot, and conditionally calls a function obtained through that slot. The supplied representation does not establish what the written values mean, what the dependencies construct or transform, or why the final address adjustment is required.

An initialization or object-setup role is plausible, but remains inferred intent. This contract does not assign constructor, destructor, reference-counting, allocation, or ownership-transfer semantics.

### Second dependency does not return normally
