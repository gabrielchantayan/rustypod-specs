The byte at initial shifted address value plus 0x09 receives that high portion’s low byte. The shifted mutation base’s offset-0x14 word is cleared before its offset-0x05 byte is read. That byte is zero-extended into the offset-0x34 word. The offset-0x14 word is subsequently read and retained. Intervening computed writes may alias it, so the retained value is not unconditionally zero.
