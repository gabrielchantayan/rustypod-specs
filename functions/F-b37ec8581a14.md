The established sequence is:

1. Obtain an object and invoke the target selected at byte offset 236 within its dispatch table, with no explicit arguments shown.
2. Obtain an object again and invoke the target selected at byte offset 312, passing the first input, the newly obtained object, and the third input, in that order.
3. Obtain an object a third time and invoke the target selected at byte offset 64, passing that newly obtained object and the first input, in that order.
