# F-98b4796128a1

That sequence is the established behavior represented by the supplied static artifact. It is not evidence of a complete, ordinarily executable routine. The artifact explicitly reports problematic instruction data and truncated control flow. Accordingly, this contract describes conditional behavior rather than promising that every invocation reaches every operation.

A plausible interpretation is that the routine enters a diagnostic or exceptional execution path. That is inferred intent only. The evidence does not establish a business purpose, a successful outcome, or a recovery protocol.
