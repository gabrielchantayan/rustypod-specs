### Input transformations

### Small-component range

### Widening versus unknown object width

A read from fixed object 0x05 cannot be required to behave identically without its declaration. Likewise, an explicit one-byte conversion before assignment to 0x01 or 0x67 does not establish that neighboring storage is untouched.

### Conversion before arithmetic at 0x3a

Suppose the captured old word is 101 and the relevant arithmetic is representable. The adjusted scalar is 254.

With an unsigned 8-bit character interpretation, its converted value is 254, so the subsequent arithmetic yields 2080. With a signed 8-bit interpretation whose conversion maps 254 to −2, the subsequent arithmetic yields 32.

If the destination preserves those integer values, the assigned results differ. A destination that subsequently narrows to eight bits may produce the same low-byte representation in both cases. Neither destination behavior is established by the supplied representation.
