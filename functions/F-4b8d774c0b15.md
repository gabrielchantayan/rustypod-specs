These are conditional static examples, not executed tests.

2. **Byte truncation:** A first token of `0x43` produces `0x0c` for its initial byte publication: four times the token is truncated to 8 bits.

3. **Byte-selected destination:** A selector-byte value of `0xff` selects address `0x110`, not a sign-extended address or a pointer loaded from memory.

8. **Word addressing:** An ordinary word-slot selector of 3 selects address `0x20` and writes 32 bits. A scaled-publication stage with selector 3 instead writes 12 at address `0x44`.
