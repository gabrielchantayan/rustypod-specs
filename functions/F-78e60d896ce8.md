## Behavior

### 2. Arithmetic and field transformations

The representation contains fixed left shifts up to 31 positions, multiplication by powers of two including \(2^{25}\), logical right shifts of unsigned values, masks, and successive transformations of already transformed values.

Established relationships include:

- Selecting the low 20 bits, shifting right by ten, then left by five or six.
- Selecting the low 24 bits before left shifts.
- Selecting the low 22 bits of a high result half before shifting left by seven or eight.
- Selecting a low 19-bit field before shifting left by two.
- Extracting fields from combined unsigned results through right shifts by 34, 36, or 44 positions.
- Selecting a single bit and placing it at bit 16.

Mask placement matters. Masking before a shift is not generally interchangeable with masking afterward. Retained intermediate values also matter: a later expression may consume a prior combined result rather than the most recent result.

**[INFERENCE] Wrapping interpretation:** where execution uses wrapping 32-bit arithmetic, multiplication and left shifts discard bits above bit 31. Successive shifts therefore operate on truncated intermediates, not unlimited-precision integers.

Several represented operands are signed. Signed overflow, signed left shifts, signed right shifts, and conversion of out-of-range values require machine semantics not supplied here. A later variable right shift uses a count selected from a low byte, potentially exceeding 31. Its concrete behavior for such counts is **documented_only**.

The opening accesses have an established order. A word is first stored at offset 20 from the high-half-derived base. A byte is then stored at offset 9 from the 25-position-shifted base, followed by a word store at offset 84 from the 256-scaled low-half base. Next, a word at offset 20 from the \(2^{25}\)-scaled base is cleared, and an explicit unsigned byte is read at offset 5 from that base. A word at offset 20 from the compact doubled base is then cleared. The captured byte is zero-extended into a word store at offset 52 from the larger base.

### Undeclared-global conversion
