### Representation

The first three assignments establish all-zero values at their respective widths. The final assignment establishes the 32-bit value `0x7fffffff`, whose decimal value is 2147483647 and whose most significant bit is clear.

The evidence does not establish byte order. On a conventional little-endian target, the final four bytes would be `ff ff ff 7f`; on a conventional big-endian target, they would be `7f ff ff ff`. These are conditional representations, not a determination of the actual target’s layout.
