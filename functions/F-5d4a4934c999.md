# F-5d4a4934c999

The intermediate address is not itself returned. Nor is the second offset applied directly to the original input.

No explicit third read, retry, comparison, or validation appears in the supplied body. These are source-level logical accesses; the supplied material does not establish their physical bus transactions, instruction selection, or behavior on memory-mapped devices.
