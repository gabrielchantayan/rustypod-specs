# F-954d263cbbe1

## Purpose

F-954d263cbbe1 performs an ordered sequence of indirect invocations and memory updates. Its established behavior includes publishing a dependency result, extracting bytes from word values, propagating a captured word to two destinations, updating fields at offset `0x14`, performing byte-selected writes to low numeric addresses, and clearing storage before invoking address zero.

No application-level purpose is established. Names such as **captured word**, **configuration word**, and **link value** describe data flow only; they do not establish object types or intended use.

The contract is conditional on execution reaching each event. Every preceding dependency must return normally, and every preceding memory access must complete, for later behavior to occur. There is no visible validation, recovery, or rollback path.

The exact derivation is therefore: retain the index’s left shift by two, logically shift that retained value right by two, then shift left by sixteen. Under 32-bit bit-vector arithmetic, this produces the same low 32 bits as shifting the original retained index left by sixteen. The intermediate operations remove the original index’s top two bits, but the final left shift by sixteen would discard those bits anyway. There is no bit-vector result difference attributable to those discarded high bits.

This equivalence does not establish the behavior of the earlier signed left shift for negative or overflowing operands. That uncertainty remains separate.

5. **F-0043 bit-vector equivalence.**

Logical right shift by two gives `1`; left shift by sixteen gives `0x00010000`. Directly shifting the original index left by sixteen has the same low 32 bits. This example does not establish defined signed-shift behavior for the initial left shift.
