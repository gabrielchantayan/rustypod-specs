## Purpose

The supplied representation contains no independent application-level computation, validation, resource management, or result processing. Any useful application behavior would therefore depend on the destination selected by the dispatch location.

This contract distinguishes three levels of confidence:

- **Established from the supplied representation:** the apparent interface, indirect invocation, and absence of other explicit wrapper operations.
- **Inferred intent:** forwarding execution through an externally established dispatch destination.
- **Unknown or documented_only:** the actual machine-level transfer mechanism, destination identity, calling convention, target behavior, and environmental guarantees.

The supplied material is not sufficient to establish that the representation faithfully recovers the original control flow. In particular, its accompanying diagnostic questions whether the transfer is a call or an indirect jump. That uncertainty limits the strength of call-and-return claims throughout this contract.
