# F-7986c3f4f8a0

## Purpose

The established behavior is construction-like, but the meanings of the record and its fields are not established. In particular, the available evidence does not prove that either dependency allocates memory, that the first field owns a resource, or that the second field is a reference count.

This contract distinguishes the function’s explicit operations from effects that dependencies may produce. Its normal-return guarantees assume the required memory accesses succeed and every invoked dependency returns normally.
