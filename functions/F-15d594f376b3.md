# F-15d594f376b3

## Purpose

The established behavior has three action families:

- Map an extracted selector to one of five constants, submit that constant to an object method, and conditionally submit the initially acquired value to another method.

Interpretations such as “event decoding,” “mode selection,” “start,” “stop,” or “delay” are inferred intent, not established semantics. This contract therefore describes values, dispatch slots, and call ordering rather than assigning domain-specific meanings.
