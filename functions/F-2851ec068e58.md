## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Initial scaling:** a fourth input of 1 supplies 128 as the initial call’s second argument. A fourth input of `0x02000000` supplies zero under 32-bit truncation.

2. **Third-input truncation:** third inputs 1 and 257 both produce `0x01000000` after the represented 24-position left shift. Whole-function equivalence does not follow.

3. **Small-base boundary:** the small quantity may be 508, giving a doubled small base of 1016 and an offset-20 access at 1036. Its low byte is 248 when stored at absolute address 21.

4. **Cleared-word read-back:** with ordinary coherent memory and no external modification, the initial large-base offset-20 read-back is zero regardless of overlap with the intervening zero clear. A nonzero result requires additional environmental semantics.

5. **Absolute scaled word:** if the selected word is 10, its explicit absolute-address-84 update stores 20, subject to 32-bit arithmetic. Surrounding computed writes can subsequently change that location.

6. **Captured publication base:** if an intervening address-96 or scaled offset-84 store changes the global’s backing storage, publication still uses the value captured before those stores, not a refreshed value.

7. **Adjusted byte arithmetic:** a selected word of zero gives an adjusted value of −150 in signed notation and an address-58 byte of 128 under low-eight-bit arithmetic.

8. **Saved word versus reloaded byte:** changing address 169 after its final capture does not change that saved word. Changing address 103 through an intervening computed write can change a later offset-nine publication byte.

9. **Separate reloads:** if the first later address-103 read observes 7 and an intervening aliased write changes it to 9, the two corresponding offset-nine stores use 7 and 9 respectively.

10. **Partial failure:** inaccessible reached memory does not imply a clean error return or reversal of earlier writes. A nonreturning final destination prevents the represented normal return.
