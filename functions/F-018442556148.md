- Minimum input: `−32,767`
- Maximum input: `32,768`

- A negative input produces a nonnegative product. Its right shift by 16 produces the positive magnitude of the input.
- Zero produces zero.
- A positive input produces a negative product. If right shift of negative signed values is arithmetic, the result is the mathematical negation of the input.
