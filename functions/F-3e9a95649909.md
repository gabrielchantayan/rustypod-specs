# F-3e9a95649909

## Purpose

1. Invoke F-9b9c1c7483f4 with a fixed selector and addresses of two local, four-byte values.
2. Forward the resulting 32-bit value to an operation selected through the supplied receiver.
3. After that operation returns ordinarily, invoke F-8a5aadac202f with one of the same local addresses.

The selector’s symbolic description suggests an operation involving a caption-video layout. That is inferred intent, not an established guarantee of a visible layout change, navigation, playback, or successful completion of any external operation.

The final dependency’s position suggests cleanup intent. Its actual ownership and resource-management effects are unknown.

This contract describes the supplied static evidence. No runtime tests have been performed.

- A receiver address readable at its initial integer-word location.

## Behavior

These are value copies, not aliases to the caller’s original argument storage. The locations remain distinct even when their initial contents are identical.

The body applies no visible comparison, arithmetic, sign conversion, or semantic interpretation to either value before invoking F-9b9c1c7483f4. Relative placement of the local locations, padding, and other stack-layout details are not part of this contract.

### First dependency invocation

F-9b9c1c7483f4 receives three arguments, in this order:

1. The address of the local initialized from the fourth input.
2. A reference to a fixed selector.
3. The address of the local initialized from the third input.

The selector is fixed by this function rather than chosen by its caller. Its exact bytes, length, encoding, termination, and mutability are not established by the supplied body.

Because the dependency receives addresses, its behavior cannot be reduced to consuming copies of the initial values. It may read or modify the addressed storage according to its unknown contract. The visible body also does not establish whether it retains or publishes either address.

On ordinary return, its result is retained as a 32-bit value. There is no visible success test, sentinel comparison, or branch based on that result.

### Receiver lookup and indirect invocation

After F-9b9c1c7483f4 returns ordinarily, the function reads the receiver’s initial integer word to obtain the dispatch base. It then accesses the callable entry at the base plus 284 bytes.

The lookup is not performed before the first dependency invocation. A receiver or dispatch-table change made during that invocation can therefore affect the selected target.

The resolved operation receives:

1. The receiver address originally supplied to F-3e9a95649909.
2. The 32-bit value returned by F-9b9c1c7483f4.

The helper result is forwarded without a visible transformation or filter. The body does not reread either input-derived local to construct these two arguments.

The operation’s return value, if any, is ignored. Its ordinary return permits execution to continue to F-8a5aadac202f.

The indirect operation’s behavior is otherwise unknown. In particular, the absence of a local address among its explicit arguments does not prove that it cannot access that local. F-9b9c1c7483f4 may have retained or published an address, and the indirect operation may obtain access through shared or reachable state.

### Final dependency invocation

If the receiver-selected operation returns ordinarily and execution reaches the final call, F-8a5aadac202f receives the address of the same local location that:

- Was initialized from the fourth input.
- Was passed as the first argument to F-9b9c1c7483f4.

This establishes **storage identity**, not preservation of any particular contents.

At the final call, the dependency receives access to whatever contents exist in that location then. Those contents need not equal either the original fourth input or the contents left immediately after F-9b9c1c7483f4 returned. The intervening receiver-selected operation may have changed the local through an address retained or published by the first dependency.

The visible body performs no restoration of the original fourth-input value and no direct rewrite of that local between the two named dependency calls.

There is no corresponding direct invocation of F-8a5aadac202f on the local initialized from the third input.

After F-8a5aadac202f returns ordinarily, the outer function returns without a value.
