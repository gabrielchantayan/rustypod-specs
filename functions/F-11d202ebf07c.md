### Initial boundary

The incoming byte is then written at entry base +0x1d. The displaced byte is widened and stored at short width at entry base plus the signed displacement.

Next, the word at entry base +0x44 is retained as the first **handoff**. Only afterward does initial anchor +0x54 receive the initial anchor’s own value. Aliasing cannot make the retained handoff observe that later store.

The initially read byte also supplies the first phase’s **link base**.

### First and second phases: byte-dependent addressing

Both phases mark current anchor +0x11 with the low byte of twice that anchor.

In the first phase, the marker precedes a signed-short read at current anchor plus selected address. The signed result selects a byte relative to the incoming handoff. Only after that byte has been read is the first argument’s +0x64 word read; that full word is written at signed result +0x54.

The dependent byte, interpreted as an address-forming value, receives the handoff’s low byte at +0x0d. A word from selected address +0x64 is then retained as the **selection seed**. Its capture precedes the short-width store of the dependent byte at signed result plus selected address. The outgoing handoff is read from selected address +0x44 after that store.

The second phase reads first argument +0x64 after its marker but before its signed-short and dependent-byte reads. This argument-field capture supplies a later marker.

After the dependent byte has been obtained, the second phase’s mutations have this order:

- First argument +1 receives the selected address’s low byte.
- Selected address +0x0d receives the incoming handoff’s low byte.
- Selected address +0x44 receives the retained selection seed.
- Dependent-byte-derived address +0x0d receives the selected address’s low byte.
- Signed result plus selected address receives the dependent byte at short width.

The outgoing handoff is then freshly read from selected address +0x44. With disjoint ordinary storage and no intervening modification, it equals the selection seed just stored there.
