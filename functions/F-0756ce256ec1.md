## Behavior

### Arithmetic model

For clarity, this contract uses these derived quantities:

- **D:** A multiplied by 2.
- **H:** D shifted right by 8 positions.
- **M:** D shifted right by 12 positions.
- **N:** M shifted right by 4 more positions.
- **T:** the result of subtracting 4 from A, shifted right by 24 positions.

The recovered integer operations use signed 32-bit values. This requires two qualifications:

1. Signed multiplication or subtraction can overflow the represented type. The supplied material does not establish whether actual execution wraps, traps, or follows some other rule. No unconditional modulo-\(2^{32}\) behavior is specified.
2. Right shifts of negative signed values depend on the target semantics. Arithmetic, sign-extending shifts are a plausible interpretation, but are not independently verified.

For inputs where the relevant intermediate values are nonnegative and representable, these uncertainties do not affect the results.

N is obtained through successive shifts by 12 and 4. It must not be described as an unsigned field extraction or masked 16-bit value. Under ordinary arithmetic-right-shift semantics, it equals D shifted right by 16, but the represented operation is staged.

T is computed from A minus 4, not from D. The subtraction precedes the shift.
