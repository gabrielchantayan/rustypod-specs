# F-00d58835fcc7

## Purpose

The established behavior is the dependency invocation, the subsequent memory-based selection of an indirect target, and the two arguments supplied to that target. A higher-level interpretation—such as reversing a displacement, undoing an adjustment, or releasing a resource—is not established.

This contract distinguishes three evidence levels:

- **Established static evidence:** behavior directly represented in the supplied source representation.
- **Inferred intent:** a plausible interpretation of that behavior, not a requirement on callers or dependencies.
- **documented_only:** a question the available evidence does not resolve.

No runtime tests have been performed.
