# F-647e766e232d

The supplied static representation does not establish an application-level purpose. Formatting, serialization, object construction, and container maintenance are not justified interpretations. Character-valued constants and self-referential stores do not establish those behaviors.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** represented accesses, widths, value dependencies, and ordering between separate statements.

### Widths and arithmetic

The representation explicitly distinguishes 8-bit bytes, 16-bit halfwords, 32-bit words, and some 64-bit results. This does not resolve the width of every fixed-storage symbol.

For represented 64-bit results, the low word comprises bits 0–31 and the high word bits 32–63. Extraction after logical right shifts of 34, 36, and 44 bits corresponds to the high word shifted right by 2, 4, and 12 bits respectively.

Invocation arguments frequently include primary results shifted left by eight, high words unchanged or shifted left by six, repeated forms of the same captured result, and explicit zeros. Later transformations include other shifts through position 31 and masks of various widths, including 11–14, 16–22, and 24 low bits.

These are bit-pattern operations, not established unit conversions. In particular, shifting a 32-bit unsigned word left by eight and then right by eight discards its original upper byte.

Unsigned shifts and masks have their represented logical meaning. Signed left shifts, signed multiplication, address overflow, conversion to signed words, negative signed right shifts, and oversized shift counts require target-specific interpretation. A 32-bit modular interpretation is plausible but is not established for every represented signed expression.

Locally retained zeros remain zero through the displayed shifts and masks until reassigned. This does not imply that dependencies preserve global memory.

### Initial calculated-address effects

The first direct writes place the retained shifted base in a word at the final high word plus 0x14, place the final high word’s low byte at the retained shifted base plus 9, and place the mutation base in a word at 256 times the final low word plus 0x54. The offset-9 byte destination is then overwritten with the retained shifted base’s low byte.

Next, a word at mutation base plus 0x14 is cleared. A byte is read at mutation base plus 5. After a word at doubled compact quantity plus 0x14 is cleared, the captured byte is zero-extended into a word at mutation base plus 0x34.

The word at mutation base plus 0x14 is subsequently captured. Call this the **captured offset-0x14 word**. Another clear of doubled compact quantity plus 0x14 precedes two byte stores of that captured word’s low byte at doubled compact quantity plus 0x15.

These are ordered statement-level effects. The captured offset-0x14 word must not automatically be substituted with zero: earlier overlapping accesses can affect its value.

The declaration of that fixed-storage source is unavailable. Its access width, signedness, and any extension preceding conversion are **documented_only**. The unsigned 32-bit destination does not establish a 32-bit source read or byte zero-extension.

The mutation base’s offset 0x11 then receives the retained eight-bit-shifted value’s low byte. A word at captured offset-0x14 word plus 0x54 receives that retained value, after which mutation base plus 0x11 is cleared by a byte write.
