# F-2df155496de9

## Purpose

F-2df155496de9 obtains a single 32-bit result from a dependency and distributes its two 16-bit halves to caller-supplied destinations. The upper half is written first; the lower half is written second.

The established behavior is bit splitting, not numerical conversion into two independently meaningful quantities. The source does not establish whether the halves represent coordinates, counters, status fields, or another paired value.

The first input has no visible role in this operation. The function has no explicit return value; its direct outputs are the two destination writes.
