# F-d9c2b920b026

## Purpose

F-d9c2b920b026 coordinates creation or acquisition of a candidate object, conditionally attaches that candidate to a caller-provided context, and performs a final operation on temporary local state.

The established behavior is narrower than “construct and return an object.” The candidate is not returned directly. Instead, an eligible candidate is stored in the context, and the function returns a 64-bit value containing a fixed upper word and a Boolean lower word. That Boolean reflects a later dependency’s result, not merely whether a candidate was obtained.

The apparent roles of initialization, allocation, rejection cleanup, and finalization are inferred intent. The supplied evidence establishes calls and memory operations but does not establish the underlying resource or ownership protocols.

### Temporary representation

The temporary address does not designate durable caller storage. Whether any dependency retains it is unknown; no such retention is established or supported as safe by the visible lifetime.

### Result

- Upper 32 bits: `0x00008000`.
- Lower 32 bits: either `0x00000000` or `0x00000001`.

Thus the two established normal-return values are:

- `0x0000800000000000`
- `0x0000800000000001`

The lower word is one only when an eligible candidate was attached and F-d33f6d597e5d returned a nonzero 32-bit value. It remains zero for a null candidate, a rejected candidate, or an eligible candidate followed by a zero result.

These normal-return guarantees are conditional on all required operations returning normally.
