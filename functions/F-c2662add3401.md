### 2. Width and arithmetic constraints

The representation distinguishes 8-bit values, 16-bit stores, 32-bit arithmetic and stores, and represented 64-bit results.

- Byte stores retain the low eight bits; halfword and word stores affect two and four bytes.
- Unsigned-byte loads widened into words are zero-extended.
- Unsigned right shifts insert zeros. Mask placement relative to shifts matters.
- Extractions beginning at bit positions 32, 34, 36, and 44 operate on represented 64-bit results, not solely their low words.
- Intermediate truncation matters. Under 32-bit bit-vector semantics, successive left shifts by 28 and 28, or by 31 and 30, produce zero.
- Signed overflow, signed shifts, address conversion, and address overflow require semantics not independently established here.

A late byte store uses a signed right shift whose count is the low byte of a computed address. Counts of 32 or greater, and right shifts of negative values, have no established portable interpretation in this evidence.

All numeric offsets below are byte offsets. A “word at an offset” identifies a four-byte access, not a proven structure member.

### 4. Clearing, early globals, and saved-state publication

Scale-derived offset 0x14 is cleared before capturing the byte at scale-derived offset 0x05. Doubled-compact offset 0x14 is then cleared, and the captured byte is widened into the word at scale-derived offset 0x34. A subsequent word read captures scale-derived offset 0x14.

Doubled-compact offset 0x14 is cleared again. Two consecutive byte stores at doubled-compact offset 0x15 use the readback’s low byte.

The fixed byte at address 0x05 is read before the fixed byte at 0x01 receives the doubled compact base’s low byte. The captured 0x05 byte subsequently acts directly as an unsigned address base.

After that global publication:

- Captured-byte-base offset 0x15 receives the compact index’s low byte.
- Scale-derived offset 0x11 receives the eight-bit-shifted value’s low byte.
- Offset 0x54 through the scale-word readback receives the eight-bit-shifted value.
- Scale-derived offset 0x11 is cleared.
- Captured-byte-base offset 0x54 is read before being overwritten with the scale-word readback.
- Doubled-compact offset 0x34 receives the compact index.

Call the captured offset-0x54 word the **saved word**. Later uses retain this snapshot rather than rereading its original location.

A word read from scale-derived offset 0x74 supplies a link base. Link offset 0x54 first receives the scale seed shifted left by 26. A separate self-valued indirect store contains two reads of doubled-compact offset 0x74: one determines the destination base, the other the data. Their relative operand-evaluation order is not established; they are not proven to be one stable snapshot.

Repeated stores publish the saved word at its own offset 0x24 and its low byte at link offset 0x09. Link offset 0x54 is replaced with the eight-bit-shifted value between the repeated pairs.

Global word 0x54 receives the saved word shifted left once after doubled-compact offset 0x14 receives the scale seed shifted left by 26, but before two stores of the saved word to that indirect field.

Two byte stores at doubled-compact offset 0x15 then publish the low byte of four times the saved word. Only afterward does global byte 0x15 receive the doubled compact base’s low byte.
