### Arithmetic interpretation

Arithmetic is width-sensitive. A 32-bit left shift followed by another calculation cannot be silently replaced by an unbounded calculation.

Under a truncating 32-bit bit-vector interpretation:

- Shifting left eight discards the original upper eight bits.
- Masking before a shift may differ from masking afterward.
- Successive shifts need not preserve information.
- A value shifted left 28 and then left another 28 becomes zero.
- Address scaling by 2, 4, 8, 256, and 33,554,432 remains width-sensitive.
