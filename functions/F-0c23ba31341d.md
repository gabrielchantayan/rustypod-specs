## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static expectations, not executed tests. They assume the relevant dependencies return and all preceding accesses and arithmetic have defined, permitted behavior.

1. **Initial quotient equivalence.** Fourth arguments 8 through 15 all yield quotient 1. The word placement preceding F-U013 is therefore 524288 at address 2097172. F-U013 receives 524288 three times.

2. **Independent copy destination.** If F-U042 supplies 3 and F-U047 supplies 7, the associated word placements contain 12 at addresses 68 and 48. F-U043 receives 12, while F-U048 receives 28.

3. **Fresh byte selection.** With first payload 65, suppose the first two reads at initial quotient plus 1 observe 7 and 9. Under truncating byte conversion, the byte stores write 4 at address 24 and 0 at address 26. The retained-byte word destination is address 56, derived from 9 rather than 7.

4. **Read distinct from placement.** If F-U064 supplies 3, its self-scaled word placement is 12 at address 68. The word read feeding F-U065 is instead at address 32. If that read observes 40, F-U065 receives 5.

5. **Replacement selector.** If F-U135 supplies 5 and F-U136 supplies 9, F-U136 receives 20. The subsequent zero-word destination is address 56, not address 40. F-U137’s first three arguments are all 9.

6. **Signed-conversion boundary.** Fourth argument hexadecimal FFFFFFFF yields quotient hexadecimal 1FFFFFFF. The early unsigned address calculation yields hexadecimal FFE00014; the unsigned stored-value calculation yields hexadecimal FFF80000. Preservation of the latter bit pattern through signed conversion is an additional condition, not established portable behavior.

7. **Clearing is not persistent state.** An execution reaching every address-36 clearing boundary performs four direct zero-word stores there. A dependency that modifies address 36 between boundaries does not contradict this contract. Reaching only three boundaries does not satisfy the four-store expectation.

8. **Extraction versus narrowing.** An unsigned payload of hexadecimal FFFFFFFF has extraction 1023. A resulting stored byte of 255 is expected only under truncating byte-conversion semantics; without that premise, the narrowing result remains unresolved.
