## Behavior

1. Read the shared source value and assign it to the destination word at offset 0.
2. Assign zero to the destination word at offset 4.
3. Assign `0xFFFFFFFF` to the destination word at offset 8.
4. Assign `0xFFFFFFFF` to the destination word at offset 12.
5. Return without a result value.

The shared source is consulted for the first assignment only. The remaining three values are fixed bit patterns and do not depend on the destination’s previous contents.

No read of the destination’s previous contents is shown. Existing values in all four words are overwritten, rather than merged, compared, incremented, or conditionally retained.

The evidence shows no branches, loops, retries, or alternate successful layouts. For a valid invocation, none of the four assignments is conditional on the copied value or on preexisting destination data.
