# F-ff0ead080160

## Purpose

F-ff0ead080160 produces a 16-bit result by exchanging the byte significance of a 16-bit value obtained through a dependency-provided address.

For a source word that remains stable throughout the operation, the result’s most significant eight bits equal the source word’s least significant eight bits, and the result’s least significant eight bits equal the source word’s most significant eight bits.

This describes a transformation of a **numerical 16-bit word**, not necessarily a reversal of two independently addressed bytes in memory. The available evidence does not establish the target’s memory byte order.

The intended use may be byte-order conversion, but that purpose is an inference. The established behavior is limited to acquiring an address, extracting two byte-sized portions through that address, and returning their concatenation in exchanged significance.
