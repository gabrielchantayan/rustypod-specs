## Acceptance examples

These are conditional deductions from static evidence, not executed tests.

If the first store destination equals the following read address, the word read occurs after the write. An implementation that reads before storing does not preserve the established order.

### Selector bounds and truncation

For an unsigned payload source of 0xFFFFFFFF, a right shift by 22 followed by a byte store writes 0xFF. For a source of 0x08000000, masking to 30 bits, shifting right by 20, and narrowing writes 0x80.
