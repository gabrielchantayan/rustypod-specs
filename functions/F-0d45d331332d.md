# F-0d45d331332d

## Purpose

F-0d45d331332d performs an ordered sequence of indirect calls, address calculations, memory accesses, and global updates. The supplied static representation has no explicit conditional branch or loop. Its final call targets address zero with three zero arguments; an ordinary return follows only if that call returns.

The established behavior is a state transformation rather than a value-producing API. It initializes and propagates 32-bit fields, copies low bytes from word-sized reads, performs byte-indexed writes, updates one global word twice, and clears a final 16-bit location.

A higher-level purpose, such as constructing objects or preparing execution state, is **documented_only**. Neither valid execution on a particular target nor a conventional application-level meaning is established.

2. Its unscaled value is passed to the next dependency.

The third relationship persists across intervening calls in the static representation. It must not be replaced with a newly obtained secondary-register value.

### 2. Argument extraction and first indexed byte write

Another result is forwarded through calls. A later dependency result supplies a four-scaled address whose offset-20 word receives the third argument’s high extraction.

Before the next dependency invocation, the function reads a word using the **early secondary word** from phase 1. Its address is calculated by retaining that word’s low 29 bits, logically shifting right by 19, and adding 20.

The quantity before the addition ranges from 0 through 1023. The resulting word-read address ranges from 20 through 1043 and need not be aligned.

The scaled destination of the preceding word store is passed onward. After intervening calls, the newly read word is logically shifted right by 22. The function then reads a byte at the third argument’s high extraction plus 1 and writes the low byte of the shifted word at the byte value plus 17.

Consequently:

- The index-byte read lies at addresses 1–512.
- The byte-store destination lies at addresses 17–272.

The following four-argument dependency receives a **separately represented secondary-register word** in its first three positions and zero in its fourth. Its relationship to the early secondary word is unresolved. Equality, inequality, and a shared producer must not be assumed.

### 3. Further indexed clears and repeated arguments

## Acceptance examples

These examples are conditional consequences of static evidence, not executed tests.

1. **Third-argument extraction.** A third argument of 0x00800000 extracts to 1. If execution reaches phase 2 with valid accesses, the corresponding word store writes 1 and its index-byte read uses address 2. Arguments 0 and 0x007FFFFF extract to 0; 0xFFFFFFFF extracts to 511.

2. **Established secondary-word reuse.** If the early secondary word is 0x00080000, its masked phase-2 word-read address is 21. This uses the value also used for the phase-1 zero-word destination and unscaled call argument, even though calls intervene.

3. **Separate later secondary word.** If that early word is 0x00080000 but the later separately represented secondary word is 7, the phase-2 repeated-argument call receives 7, 7, 7, and 0. Substituting the early word would violate the represented data relationships.

4. **Byte truncation.** If the phase-2 word read yields 0xFFFFFFFF, shifting right by 22 produces 1023, but the indexed byte store writes 255.

5. **Fresh field read.** If the first phase-4 base-plus-116 read yields 0x123456AB and an intervening dependency changes that word to 0x123456CD, the two byte copies write 0xAB and 0xCD, respectively.

6. **Separate address-1 reads.** If the two phase-3 reads yield 4 and 9, their zero-byte destinations are 21 and 26.

7. **Paired-result ordering.** If the phase-4 64-bit result has low half 0x11223344 and high half 0x55667788, those values are passed in that order, followed by the separate high-extraction argument.

8. **Shift information loss.** Under a target interpretation that produces a wrapped 32-bit result, shifting 0x40000001 left by two yields 4. Logically shifting that retained word right by two yields 1, not the original value. This example does not establish signed-overflow semantics.

9. **Captured global address.** If G-B is captured as address A and a dependency changes G-B to B, the later explicit field read still uses A plus 20.

10. **Final 24-bit assembly.** If, after the pointer clear, G-C reads as 0x123456 and the returned byte is 0xAB, the G-A update writes 0x123456AB. This does not require three separately ordered byte reads.

11. **Terminal failure.** If the address-zero call does not return, ordinary completion is not established. Earlier explicit stores have already been attempted; no rollback follows from the body.
