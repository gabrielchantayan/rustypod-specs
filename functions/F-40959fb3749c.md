The established behavior is delegation, not computation. The supplied representation contains no explicit argument construction, result processing, destination validation, retry, or error translation. It does not establish what useful operation the destination performs.

Consequently, the recovered call-and-return structure is evidence about the supplied representation, not proof that the executable performs an ordinary nested call.
