# F-b2077e6b0a6a

## Purpose

The decision to replace the fields depends on whether a dependency returns a nonzero 64-bit value. A zero result skips the transformation, temporary construction, and field replacement, but does not skip destination initialization or normal-path cleanup.

This is a source-facing behavioral contract. The supplied static evidence establishes the operations and their ordering, but not the application-level meanings of the destination, token, context byte, or temporary objects. Descriptions such as “initialize,” “transform,” and “cleanup” identify their apparent roles; they do not establish undocumented ownership or lifetime guarantees.

This invocation precedes destination initialization and all local-storage preparation. Its side effects therefore occur before those later operations. No assumption is made that it preserves global state or the receiver.
