# F-6da955e43736

## Purpose

The function has no explicit result value. Its observable effects are destination writes and any effects of its dependencies. Most supported selectors initialize six sequence words and two accompanying words. Selectors 8 and 9 also replace the initial header. Selector 6 and unsupported selectors leave the selector-dependent fields unwritten by this function.

**Unknown:** configuration contents and dependency implementations are unavailable. Neither configuration stability nor dependency preservation of memory may be assumed.
