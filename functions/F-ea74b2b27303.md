# F-ea74b2b27303

## Purpose

- Replace the first 32-bit word of the supplied object with a value read from a global 32-bit storage location.
- Invoke F-81a39c9757b1 with the address of the object's third word and values read from its third and fourth words.
- After that invocation returns, capture the object's third word and calculate a 32-bit difference between its fifth word and the captured value.
- Pass that difference and the constant 12 to F-870c4fcf7295.
- Pass the captured third-word value, the result of F-870c4fcf7295, and zero to F-ff1bdc376dcd.
