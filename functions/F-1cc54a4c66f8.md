# F-1cc54a4c66f8

## Purpose

F-1cc54a4c66f8 clears three fixed-width regions relative to a caller-supplied base address, then invokes an indirectly selected dependency with two addresses derived from that base and the numeric argument 32.

The established behavior is an ordered sequence of memory writes followed by an indirect invocation. A broader role such as initialization or reset is **inferred intent**, not an established contract. In particular, the supplied evidence does not establish the object’s type, the meaning of the cleared regions, or the operation performed by the dependency.

The dependency receives access to the same storage containing two of the cleared regions. Consequently, clearing those regions before invocation does not guarantee that they remain zero afterward.

## Behavior

### Ordered direct writes

The wrapper first stores a 2-byte zero at B plus `0x16`. It next stores a 2-byte zero at B plus `0x14`. It then stores a 4-byte zero at B plus `0x2C`.

The distinction between these widths is significant. The first two operations are not represented as one combined 4-byte store, even though they clear an adjacent four-byte span. The order is the higher-addressed 2-byte region first, then the lower-addressed 2-byte region.

All three values are zero across the entire stated width. Their resulting byte contents are therefore zero regardless of byte order. The evidence does not establish atomicity, synchronization, or safe concurrent access.

No input-dependent condition guards these writes. The wrapper does not visibly inspect existing field contents, validate B, or check a capacity before beginning.

### Indirect invocation

After all three stores, the wrapper invokes a target selected through a stored function address. This contract labels that unresolved dependency **F-unresolved-01**; the label identifies the dependency role, not a recovered implementation.

The target selection is represented after the stores. If the supplied address aliases storage used to select the target, those earlier writes could affect the subsequent selection. The evidence does not establish that such aliasing is intended or supported.

The dependency is given the address at B plus `0x34`, the address at B plus `0x14`, and the value 32. There is no visible intervening operation that restores previous contents or changes the three cleared regions before the invocation.

Immediately before dependency execution, assuming the three stores succeeded and there was no external interference:

- Offsets `0x14` through `0x17` contain zero.
- Offsets `0x2C` through `0x2F` contain zero.
- The wrapper has not directly initialized storage at offset `0x34`.

These are entry-state facts for the dependency, not unconditional postconditions for the wrapper.

### Behavior after invocation

The wrapper does not visibly branch on a dependency result. Any error reporting through memory, global state, process state, or control flow belongs to the unresolved dependency and remains unspecified here.
