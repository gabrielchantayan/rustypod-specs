## Behavior

### Initial access sequence

The first visible write stores B’s low 16 bits at S plus B.

The operation then reads:

- A 32-bit word **V** at the address formed by adding J to the bitwise OR of R and A.
- An unsigned byte **Q** at S plus that same bitwise OR.

OR is not interchangeable with addition.

Three word stores follow, in order: V at D plus 8; D’s 32-bit representation at A plus 64; and V at H plus 116. Only after those stores are a 32-bit value **W** read from D plus 40 and a 32-bit value **X** read from H plus 92.

The next byte writes are ordered as follows:

1. H’s low byte at V plus 3.
2. Q at H plus 16.
3. X’s low byte at Q plus 28.

The third destination uses Q itself as the address base. Because Q is unsigned, its numeric destination is between 28 and 283 inclusive. No additional H-based offset is present.

These reads are not entry-time snapshots. Earlier stores can change W or X through aliasing.

### Derived region and sentinel effects

The following quantities describe unsigned 32-bit calculations:

- **Delta**: A minus 56.
- **Masked**: A minus 224, then masked with `0x7fffffff`.
- **Index**: Masked shifted right by one.
- **Region**: twice the wrapped result of A minus 224, again at 32-bit width.

Thus Index is between zero and `0x3fffffff`. Region is not twice A minus 224; the subtraction precedes multiplication.

A separate address, **Shifted**, is represented by shifting W left by two and then right by 32. W is used through a signed intermediate. The result is **documented_only**: signed shifting and a shift count equal to the represented operand width prevent a portable numeric interpretation.

The ordered effects in this phase are:

- A byte containing Index’s low eight bits is stored at W plus `0x0897e8b8`.
- A global receives the value `0xb8`.
- A byte containing Delta’s low eight bits is stored at A minus 50.
- A word containing Delta is stored at Shifted.
- Halfwords at Region plus 4 and Region plus 6 receive `0xffff`, in that order.
- A byte at Shifted plus 4 receives `0xb8`.
- The halfword at Region plus 2 receives `0xffff`.
- A global receives the low 16 bits of Region’s address representation.

The last assignment establishes a 16-bit extracted value, not a proven halfword-wide global destination.

Only afterward is the unsigned halfword at Region read. Its value becomes an address between zero and 65535. A word containing Delta masked with `0x897e8b80` is stored at that address.

The halfword at Region has not been directly initialized by the three sentinel stores, which target offsets 2, 4, and 6. Earlier writes may nevertheless overlap it.

### Dependency-address processing and conditional termination

**High** denotes the result represented by interpreting E as signed and shifting it right by 32. Its numeric value is unresolved. A separate 16-bit **Sign** value comes from a signed right shift of E by 31 followed by narrowing. Under arithmetic signed-right-shift semantics, Sign is zero for a clear top bit and `0xffff` for a set top bit.

A halfword containing Sign is written at High plus 4 before E is tested for signed negativity.

If E is negative under the represented 32-bit signed interpretation, control reaches an invalid-instruction termination marker. No later effects are represented on that branch. The marker does not establish an exception class, process status, or recoverable failure protocol.

On the other branch, the operation:

- Stores Sign as a halfword at High plus 18.
- Stores E as a word at address E.
- Reads a signed halfword at High plus Index.
- Repeats the halfword store at High plus 18 and the word store at E.
- Stores a word at High plus 8 obtained by sign-extending the read halfword and shifting it right by 16.

Under arithmetic right-shift semantics, the last word is zero for a nonnegative halfword and `0xffffffff` for a negative halfword. It is not the original halfword’s numeric value.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static acceptance cases, not executed tests. Each assumes execution reaches the relevant operation.

1. **Initial truncation.** For B with representation `0x12345678`, the initial indirect store writes halfword `0x5678` at S plus B, not a full word.

2. **Unsigned wraparound.** With A equal to zero, Delta is `0xffffffc8`, Masked is `0x7fffff20`, Index is `0x3fffff90`, and Region is `0xfffffe40`. No underflow rejection is represented.

3. **Byte-derived address.** With Q equal to `0xff`, X’s low byte is written at numeric address 283. Neither sign-extending Q nor adding H preserves the represented destination.

4. **Conditional termination.** If E is signed-negative, the halfword store at High plus 4 precedes termination. The later stores through E are not reached in the represented flow.

5. **Selector alternatives.** An E value with bit 2 clear gives Selector 88 and the explicit Index destination 368. With bit 2 set, those values are 92 and 384.

6. **Signed-halfword reduction.** Assuming arithmetic signed shifts, a halfword read of `0x7fff` leads to zero at High plus 8; `0x8000` leads to `0xffffffff`.

7. **Address-slot reread.** Suppose the final address slot initially represents P after receiving four times T. If the self-address store at P overlaps that slot and changes its represented value to P′, the following Index store targets P′ plus 4, not necessarily P plus 4.

8. **Fresh concatenation operand.** Suppose Z’s upper halfword is `0x1234`, but an intervening write changes the upper-part slot’s later 16-bit operand to `0xabcd`. The reset-related concatenated value is then `0xabcdea70`, not `0x1234ea70`.

9. **Global-width boundary.** Assigning the extracted low 16 bits of Region does not justify requiring a two-byte global write. That width remains **documented_only** without a declaration or stronger evidence.

10. **Unresolved full-width shift.** Neither Shifted nor High may be required to equal zero merely because the representation shifts right by 32.
