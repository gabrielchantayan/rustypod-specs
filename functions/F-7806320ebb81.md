# F-7806320ebb81

## Purpose

F-7806320ebb81 clears three consecutive 32-bit storage elements beginning at a caller-supplied address.

The established behavior is limited to writing zero to those three elements, in increasing element order, and returning without a result value. The function does not inspect their previous contents or make its actions conditional on them.

A plausible intent is initialization or reset of a small caller-owned storage region. That intent is inferred, not established: the supplied evidence does not identify a containing object, field meanings, or a lifecycle operation. In particular, clearing these elements must not be described as destroying an object, releasing resources, or restoring every invariant of an unknown larger structure.

1. It writes a 32-bit zero to the first element.
2. It writes a 32-bit zero to the immediately following element.
3. It writes a 32-bit zero to the third element.
