# F-34ada3f91385

## Purpose

No application-level purpose is established. In particular, the evidence does not establish that this fragment initializes an object, manages a device, implements a complete callable routine, or terminates normally.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** operations and ordering represented in the supplied fragment.
- **Inferred intent or execution model:** interpretations that require assumptions beyond those operations.
- **`documented_only` unknowns:** unresolved behavior, including dependency effects, entry-state requirements, and execution beyond the recovered boundary.

### First byte store

After F-U03 returns and before F-U04 is invoked:

1. Read one unsigned byte at address B plus one.
2. Interpret that byte as a numeric selector.
3. Add decimal 17 to the selector to obtain the store address.
4. Store one byte formed by shifting V right by 22 positions and retaining the low eight bits.

Because V is represented as unsigned, the represented right shift is logical. The stored byte consists of original bit positions 22 through 29 of V. Bits 30 and 31 do not survive the final byte narrowing.

If the selector is **S**, the destination is the numeric address **S + 17**. The fragment does not show an additional table lookup, a retained object base, or a pointer-width load between the selector read and the store.

With an unsigned eight-bit selector and ordinary address addition, the destination range is decimal 17 through 272 inclusive.
