# F-b1b149a39f90

## Purpose

The first dependency receives the numeric constant 236 and supplies a 32-bit value. The second receives that value and the first caller argument; its returned address determines the destination of the first write and an address passed to the third dependency. The third dependency’s result determines the destinations of the remaining three writes.

Neither allocation nor initialization is established by these calls. In particular, 236 is an established numeric argument, not an established byte count. A construction or component-setup role is plausible, but remains an explicitly labeled inference rather than part of the behavioral requirements.
