## Purpose

## Behavior

### Arithmetic and carried state

Unsigned 32-bit arithmetic wraps modulo \(2^{32}\). Signed overflow, signed left shifts outside their defined range, implementation-dependent signed right shifts, and shift counts at least 32 lack established portable semantics. Pointer arithmetic and address wrapping also require an execution model.

The contract distinguishes:

- **Current pointer:** base for repeated word accesses.
- **Carried word:** an early loaded value, later replaced by fixed values or a saved word.
- **Scratch word:** initially zero; later supplies the continuation’s captured byte.
- **Dispatch word:** used in address arithmetic and as an argument.
- **Region word:** supplies continuation-region addresses.
- **Accumulator:** initialized in the inner phase and subsequently updated.
- **Target-address state:** controls the outer and inner indirect target selections.

These are descriptive roles, not a declared structure layout.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static obligations, not observed executions.

1. **First write:** Given a valid entry base and argument 1 equal to `0xABCD`, the first access writes that halfword at base plus 10.

2. **Width distinction:** The assignment of `0xEFEC` to F-6255e949b9cc-associated storage does not justify selecting a 16-bit store. Likewise, the explicitly 16-bit zero assigned at address 48 establishes the assigned value, not destination width.

3. **Promoted multiplication:** If the address argument 4 plus 64 has signed low halfword one, the assigned value at `0x0897F07A` is 4096 before unknown destination conversion—not an independently established halfword store.

4. **Target order:** On the first outer pass, if F-U02 is selected, its target uses `0x0897F0C3 + 0x2E`; state becomes `0x0897F193` before invocation. A subsequently reached F-U03 selects from that updated state plus `0xE56`, then installs `0x0897F1D7` before invocation.

5. **Outer address:** With carried word 24 and dispatch zero, the `0xC470` destination is 39. With carried word 16, it is 38 because the conditional subtraction applies.

6. **Pointer replacement:** If the converted value read at `0x0897F216` is `0x2000`, that pointer is installed before the returned-address byte read. Subsequent current-pointer accesses use it until replacement.

7. **Product ordering:** A returned low half of one produces 32 without overflow. The self-address store at 32 precedes the larger-product branch.

8. **Backward edge:** With previous carried word `0x12345678` and saved word 64, the next captured byte is `0x78`, dispatch is eight, and carried word is 64, conditional on intervening calls permitting continuation.
