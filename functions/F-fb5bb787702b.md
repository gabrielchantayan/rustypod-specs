## Behavior

- Masking to 30 bits and shifting right by 20 produces up to ten bits.
- Masking to 22 bits and shifting right by 12 also produces up to ten bits.
- Shifting an unsigned word right by 22 produces up to ten bits.

A following byte store truncates these results; it does not preserve ten bits.

Some address calculations use signed representations, including multiplication and left shifts. Their overflow behavior is not established as a portable language contract. Consequences requiring fixed-width machine arithmetic are conditional on that interpretation.
