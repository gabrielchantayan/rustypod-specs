# F-d2c3f7647481

## Purpose

F-d2c3f7647481 coordinates three dependencies around a temporary, addressable state area. It initializes that area from three incoming 32-bit values, passes its address and a fourth incoming value to a preparation dependency, invokes an operation using the area’s then-current middle word, invokes a finalization dependency, and returns a 64-bit value assembled from two distinct observations.

The returned value combines:

- A low 32-bit word captured from the operation dependency’s return.
- A high 32-bit word read from the beginning of the temporary area **after finalization returns**.

This ordering is central to the contract. The high word is not necessarily the original input word, the value present after preparation, or the value present when the operation returns.

Preparation, operation, and finalization are descriptive labels for call positions. Their business meanings, resource-management roles, and success conventions are not established.
