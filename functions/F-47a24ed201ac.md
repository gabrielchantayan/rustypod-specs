# F-47a24ed201ac

## Purpose

F-47a24ed201ac clears one 32-bit storage location at a fixed displacement from a caller-supplied base address.

The established effect is limited to writing zero at byte offset 200 from that base. The evidence does not establish the meaning of the containing storage or the field being cleared. Interpreting this operation as initialization, a status reset, a counter reset, or an ownership change would require additional evidence.
