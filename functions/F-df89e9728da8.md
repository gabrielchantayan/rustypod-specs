The sequence is consistent with initializing a structured object and two embedded regions. That interpretation is **inferred intent**, not an established object model. Nor does it establish the purposes or extents of the interior regions.

The established contract is the ordered store, the two argument addresses, the dependency call order, and the identity of the normally returned pointer. The dependencies’ behavior remains unknown.
