# F-0049356bd073

## Purpose

The observable behavior is narrower than an object-construction guarantee. The available static evidence does not establish an object type, allocation policy, ownership transfer, or complete initialized layout. In particular, the final two stores are based on the second dependency’s returned address, not necessarily on the original destination or the first dependency’s result.

The apparent intent is to initialize a leading field and delegate additional setup before clearing two trailing fields. That interpretation is inferred intent; the contract below rests on the explicit operations and their ordering.
