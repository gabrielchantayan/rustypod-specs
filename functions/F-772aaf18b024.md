# F-772aaf18b024

## Purpose

On an ordinary return, the result is the original input address.

“Teardown,” “notification,” and “collection iteration” are inferred roles. The established contract consists of the represented accesses, arguments, branches, and call ordering. It does not establish destruction, allocation release, ownership transfer, or the internal effects of any dependency.
