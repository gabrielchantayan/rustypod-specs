### Final write and completion

Let the final address-bearing result be F-U176’s 64-bit result. Its extraction shifted right by 41 produces an unsigned value of at most 23 bits. A subsequent right shift by 11 therefore cannot encounter a negative signed value.

Equivalently, define a destination index as the high word of that result logically shifted right by 20. The destination address is:

**destination index + floor(destination index / 4).**

The index ranges from 0 through 4095. Consequently:

- The destination address ranges from 0x0000 through 0x13FE.
- Each group of four successive indices is followed by one skipped address.
- The formula does not enforce 16-bit alignment.
- Address zero is possible and is not rejected.

The final invocation order remains significant: F-U177 consumes the low word, F-U178 produces the scalar used by F-U179, F-U179 receives that scalar and zero, and F-U180 precedes the store. These intervening calls may affect memory accessibility or contents.

The attempted write has width 16 bits and numeric value **0x00CD**. No read of the destination is represented. Afterward, the representation ends at an invalid-instruction marker rather than an ordinary return.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Independent word uses.** If F-U004 returns low word 0x12345678 and high word 3, transfer 1 and F-f3c147c541d8 receive 0x12345678. F-U008 receives 0x60, while F-U012’s second argument is 0x03000000.

2. **Transformed payload versus original result.** If W(F-U013) is 0x80000001, F-U014 receives 0x40000000 and F-U018’s third argument is 0x40000000. Transfer 2 carries F-U014’s result, which cannot be inferred from those inputs without its contract.

3. **Mask boundary.** If L(F-U089) is 0xFFFFFFFF, F-U094’s third argument is 0x003FFFF0. Bits above the lowest 18 source bits do not survive.

4. **Signed-shift boundary.** If W(F-U135) is 0x04000000, its left-by-5 copy is 0x80000000. Under arithmetic right shift, F-U143’s second argument is 0xFC000000, not the original scalar.

5. **Forced zero without a zero producer.** Any W(F-U148), including 0xFFFFFFFF, yields a zero transfer-17 payload under the 32-bit interpretation.

6. **Transfer distinction.** Matching payloads do not permit collapsing events 5, 6, 7, and 16: their control triples are respectively (0,3,7), (0,0,7), (0,4,7), and (0,1,7).

7. **Destination discontinuity.** A final high word below 0x00100000 gives address zero. High words 0x00300000 and 0x00400000 give addresses 3 and 5. Address 4 is skipped by this transition; address 3 also demonstrates the absence of enforced even alignment.

8. **Maximum destination.** A final high word of 0xFFFFFFFF gives index 4095 and starting address 0x13FE. If the store completes, its numeric value is 0x00CD and its width remains 16 bits.

9. **Interruption and ordering.** If F-U179 does not return, neither F-U180 nor the final store is required to occur. No represented recovery path bypasses that dependency.
