### Difference calculations

For a recognized classification, the function derives two signed, 32-bit integer differences in the supplied representation:

- **First difference:** the context field at offset 0x2C minus the field at offset 0x24.
- **Second difference:** the context field at offset 0x28 minus the field at offset 0x20.

Their dispatch order matters: the first difference becomes argument 2, and the second becomes argument 3.

There is no explicit normalization, absolute-value operation, sorting, clipping, saturation, or range validation. Equal endpoints produce zero when the subtraction is representable. A minuend smaller than its subtrahend produces a negative result when representable.

The operands are not explicitly widened before subtraction. Consequently, two readable signed 32-bit operands can have a mathematical difference outside the signed 32-bit range. The supplied evidence does not establish a portable result for that case. A contract must not silently substitute wraparound, saturation, or a wider mathematical result.
