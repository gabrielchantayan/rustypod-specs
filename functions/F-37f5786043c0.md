### Leading-field write

- Original bit 0 is discarded.
- Original bits 1–16 become output bits 0–15.
- Original bits 17–31 do not contribute to the stored field.

Equivalently, take the low 17 bits of the post-call value and discard their least significant bit. The resulting unsigned bit pattern is between 0 and 65,535.

This is not generally equivalent to signed division by two. High bits are excluded, and negative inputs require the shift-semantics qualification described under **Uncertainties**.
