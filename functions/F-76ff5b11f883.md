### Fixed-byte ordering

### Fresh 0x67 observations

Suppose the initial 0x67 write publishes 0x12, but intervening computed writes change that byte to 0x34 before the copy to 0x21. The copied byte must come from the fresh read, not the retained 0x12.

Likewise, the two later offset-9 publications may differ if intervening computed writes change 0x67. Reusing one captured byte for all three publications is unacceptable.

### Fixed-link capture

### Unsigned publication and retained transfer value
