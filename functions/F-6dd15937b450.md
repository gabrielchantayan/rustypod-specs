### Entry into direct memory effects

The initial store order is significant. A 32-bit store at high-half-derived offset +0x14 precedes a byte store at another derived base’s +0x09. A word store through the final low-half-derived base at +0x54 follows, before the byte at +0x09 is overwritten.
