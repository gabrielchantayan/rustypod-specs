The distinction is exact:

- A zero first byte selects the zero-byte branch.
- Every nonzero first-byte value selects the nonzero-byte branch.

The decision does not depend on whether a nonzero character is printable, whitespace, a delimiter, or part of a valid encoding. Character signedness does not change whether a byte is zero.
