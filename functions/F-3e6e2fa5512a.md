7. **No rollback.** If the dependency immediately following the absolute write of 192 at address 13 does not return, that write and preceding completed writes remain part of the observable prefix. No later operation is required to occur.

8. **Terminal behavior.** If all visible calls and accesses complete, acceptance requires preserving the absence of an established normal return. Returning success would add behavior not supported by the evidence.
