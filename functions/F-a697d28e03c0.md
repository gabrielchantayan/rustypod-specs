### First write: conditional address source

The controlling dependency result is treated as unsigned for the bit test and initial shift.

The condition examines **bit 30**, using mask 0x40000000:

- When bit 30 is set, the selected word is the controlling result shifted left by two bits.
- When bit 30 is clear, the selected word is the incoming register-derived alternative address word shifted left by ten bits.

This is not a test of bit 31 or an ordinary signed-negative condition.

The representation forms the unsigned two-bit-shifted value before testing whether it must be replaced. On the clear-bit path, that initial value does not determine the store address or F-d005 arguments.

The first explicit write stores the payload word’s low eight bits at the address formed by multiplying the selected word by two. F-d005 follows the store and receives the selected word twice.

The duplicated arguments are **not** the doubled destination address.

### Second write: extracted payload

A separate dependency result supplies the second address source.

The destination is that source shifted left by three bits. The stored byte comes from the unsigned payload word shifted right by 22 bits, retaining the low eight bits. Under the 32-bit interpretation, this selects original bits 22 through 29.

After this store, F-d007 receives, in order:

1. The second address source multiplied by four.
2. The second address source shifted left by ten bits.

Neither argument is specified as the byte destination. The two arguments are not generally equal.
