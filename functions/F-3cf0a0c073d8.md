# F-3cf0a0c073d8

## Purpose

The wrapper does not establish a higher-level service such as initialization, dispatch validation, error recovery, or resource management. Any such service would depend on the selected target and its calling environment, neither of which is identified by the supplied evidence.

The principal limitation is control-transfer recovery: the supplied representation describes an indirect transfer as a call, but accompanying diagnostic material reports that its underlying control flow was not fully recovered. This contract therefore distinguishes behavior represented by the supplied body from unresolved machine-level calling and return semantics.
