### Address-58 conversion order

Suppose the adjusted base is 130, consistent with a link value of 70 under conditional arithmetic.

With an additional unsigned 8-bit plain-character interpretation, the promoted right-hand arithmetic result is 1088. With an additional signed 8-bit interpretation that converts 130 to −126, it is −960.

A sufficiently wide signed destination could distinguish those results. Only with an additional low-8-bit destination conversion do both yield 64. The unconditional contract therefore neither assigns 64 nor asserts a one-byte write at address 58.
