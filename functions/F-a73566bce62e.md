If it is nonzero, its value is used directly. The context-relative candidate field is not read on this path.

This substitution occurs once. A loaded value of zero is still passed to F-33e1c7c6ba3f; it does not cause another lookup, a retry, or an early return. No distinction is made between a positive and a negative nonzero candidate.
