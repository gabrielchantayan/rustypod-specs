That third byte is accepted as a three-byte leading candidate when its upper four bits are binary `1110`; equivalently, applying mask hexadecimal F0 yields hexadecimal E0.

- the third byte’s low four bits in result bits 12–15;
- the retained byte from P − 2, masked to six bits, in result bits 6–11; and
- the first retained observation from P − 1, masked to six bits, in result bits 0–5.
