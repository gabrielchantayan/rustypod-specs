# F-6a5e11407743

## Purpose

F-6a5e11407743 performs an initial dependency call involving an embedded slot, selects exactly one of two alternative dependencies, and conditionally invokes a final indirect dependency.

Selection depends on a gate byte, an optional object pointer, and the pointer result of an object-associated indirect call. The final invocation depends on a separate pointer and its first 32-bit word, read after the selected dependency returns.

These are established control-flow and data-flow roles. The supplied body does **not** establish that either selected dependency produces output. Their purposes, output behavior, and ownership effects remain unknown.
