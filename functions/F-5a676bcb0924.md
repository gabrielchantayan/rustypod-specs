# F-5a676bcb0924

## Purpose

F-5a676bcb0924 performs an ordered sequence of indirect calls, integer transformations, byte accesses, and 32-bit memory accesses. The supplied static representation does not establish an application-level purpose.

Visible effects include:

- Copying the low byte of fetched words to computed addresses at offset `0x01`.
- Storing 32-bit words at computed addresses with offsets `0x14`, `0x24`, and `0x34`.
- Reading four 32-bit words at offset `0x74`: from the entry base and three subsequently computed bases. No displayed store uses offset `0x74`.
- Using zero-extended byte selectors to choose absolute destinations in `0x11`–`0x110`.
- Writing absolute byte locations `0x0D` and `0x11`.
- Passing captured and transformed dependency results to further indirect calls.

The representation carries a control-flow warning and ends at an invalid-instruction boundary. This contract describes the displayed sequence conditionally on its accuracy and on execution reaching each operation. It does not establish that the complete sequence is executable or returns normally.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests. Required accesses and calls must be reachable and valid.

1. **Initial truncation and order.** If the first fetched word is `0x123456AB`, the initial byte store is `0xAB`. It precedes the first call, which receives the full word.

2. **Read-only offset `0x74`.** A conforming interpretation accounts for word reads from entry base, \(P\), \(AJ\), and \(AO\), each plus `0x74`. It does not introduce a word store at that offset.

3. **Different entry-bit transformations.** For entry bits `0xFFC00000`, \(E=\texttt{0x3FF}\). F-DEP-03 receives repeated arguments `0x3FE00000`; the subsequent word store contains `0xFF800000`.

4. **Masking is not alignment.** For \(J=\texttt{0xC0000003}\), \(M=3\) and \(N=0\). Clearing the lower two bits instead would change the contract.

5. **Selector mutation.** If the first selector read at \(AC+\texttt{0x01}\) yields `0x05` and the next yields `0x09`, the publication targets `0x16` and the clear targets `0x1A`.

6. **Captured selector width.** For \(AI=\texttt{0xFF}\), \(AJ=510\). Its byte-store destination is `0x20B`; the later word-store and word-read addresses are `0x222` and `0x272`.

7. **Pair halves remain distinct.** If F-DEP-18 returns low half `3` and high half `5`, then \(AS=12\), while the immediately following word-store destination is `0x28`. The repeated argument is derived from the low half.

8. **All three pairs matter.** If F-DEP-19 returns \(AT=7\), \(AU=40\), its following call receives \(7,40,7,5\), independently of the earlier pair values.

9. **Repeated final clears.** Different selector values at the two final reads require two ordered clears at their respective destinations, not one merged clear.

10. **Partial execution.** If the initial byte store succeeds but the first dependency never returns, that preceding store remains an effect. No later operation or rollback is promised.
