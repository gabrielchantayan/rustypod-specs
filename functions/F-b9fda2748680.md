# F-b9fda2748680

No application-level purpose is established. Constants that resemble characters and recurring address displacements do not establish text formatting, object initialization, container manipulation, or a recognizable protocol.

### Direct-memory phase: terminology and opening effects

The following descriptive bases identify retained address expressions, not recovered objects:

- **Primary scaled base:** a retained dependency result multiplied by 0x02000000.
- **Compact base:** twice an unsigned extraction formed by shifting a returned word left by eight, masking with 0x7FFF, and shifting right by six.
- **Seed word:** a word subsequently read using a zero-extended byte from fixed address 0x05 as its base.
- **Adjusted value:** four times the seed word, minus 0x96.
- **Working base:** four times the adjusted value.
- **Linked base:** twice a later memory-derived word.
- **Publication base:** a single retained global-word read, described below.

Under a 32-bit bit-vector interpretation, the compact extraction is at most 508 and is a multiple of four; its doubled base is at most 1016 and is a multiple of eight. These are low-address expressions, not evidence of heap allocation.

The opening direct effects preserve this ordering:

- A word store at displacement 0x14 from the latest result’s high word precedes a byte store at another retained base plus nine.
- A word store at displacement 0x54 from a base formed by scaling the latest low word precedes an overwrite of that byte destination.
- The primary-base word at displacement 0x14 is cleared before reading the primary-base byte at displacement five.
- The compact-base word at displacement 0x14 is cleared before the loaded byte is stored, zero-extended, at primary displacement 0x34.
- The primary-base word at displacement 0x14 is then read.
- Another compact-base clear precedes two byte stores at compact displacement 0x15 using the low byte of that loaded word.

A clear followed by a read does not independently prove a zero result: intervening accesses through other expressions may alias the cleared location.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Explicit-input shifts.** Under 32-bit bit-vector semantics, fourth input one contributes 0x10000000 to its shifted argument position; fourth input 16 contributes zero. Third input 0x01234567 contributes 0x12345670 to its shifted position. These examples do not assert portable signed-shift behavior.

2. **Full-width extraction.** A 64-bit result of 0x123456789ABCDEF0 has high word 0x12345678 and low word 0x9ABCDEF0. Its unsigned shift right by 36 is 0x01234567.

3. **Unsigned widening.** A primary-base byte read of 0xFE produces word-store value 254, not −2.

4. **Disjoint overwrite group.** With working base 0x1000 in ordinary byte-addressed memory, the byte read is at 0x1004 and word stores occupy 0x1020–0x1023. Those stores do not overlap that byte. The required order still includes all four stores and the intervening read.

5. **Global capture before 0x60.** If the global’s storage overlaps the word at 0x60 and initially contains 0x2000, the publication base is retained as 0x2000 before the fixed-address store changes that storage.

6. **0x3A interleaving.** If the 0x3A byte aliases part of the linked-displacement-0x14 word, the later word read observes the post-write memory state under the target byte order, not an earlier snapshot.

7. **Fresh 0x67 observations.** If the three represented 0x67 reads observe 0x12, 0x34, and 0x56, the corresponding stores receive 0x12 at 0x21, then 0x34 and 0x56 at working displacement nine. Reusing one retained byte is insufficient.

8. **Interrupted execution.** If F-unknown-01 never returns, later operations are not guaranteed. If execution reaches F-unknown-03, preceding reached direct accesses occur before it; normal function return depends on that transfer returning.
