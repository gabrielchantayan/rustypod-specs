# F-1570aa6a9c5b

The established behavior is a short, ordered sequence of one global-value read, two storage writes, one dependency call, and one return-value adjustment. The dependency’s purpose and the meaning of the stored values are not established.

A header-and-payload interpretation is plausible: the first eight bytes receive two fields, and the dependency receives the address immediately following those fields. That interpretation is inferred intent, not a proven object format, allocation protocol, or ownership rule.
