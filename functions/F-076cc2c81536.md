This contract describes accesses, arguments, ordering, and direct state changes established by the supplied static representation. “Coordinate,” “handle,” “descriptor,” and “record configuration” identify structural roles; they do not establish application-level meanings, units, or ownership.

A dependency that returns normally is followed by the remaining operations, even if it reports or internally experiences a failure.
