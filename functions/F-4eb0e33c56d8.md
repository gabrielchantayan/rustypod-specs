# F-4eb0e33c56d8

The established behavior is this sequence of calls, one explicit object-field read, and one explicit object-field write. The dependency implementations are unavailable. Consequently, this contract does not assign an application-level meaning to the temporary values, the address derived from the object field, or the final byte.

That interpretation is inferred intent only. In particular, neither resource release nor successful completion of an application operation is established by the visible call structure.
