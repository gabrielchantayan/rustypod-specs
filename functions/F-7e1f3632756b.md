### Fresh absolute-state reads

Suppose the saved 0xa9 word has low byte 0x12. The initial store to 0x67 writes 0x12. If a later dynamic write overlapping 0x67 changes it to 0x9a before one of the later reads, the corresponding calculated-base byte store uses the freshly read value, not unconditionally 0x12.

### Byte promotion and overlap

### Trace preservation

### Shift and final transfer
