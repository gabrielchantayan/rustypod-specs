# F-722f6fae15e2

## Purpose

F-722f6fae15e2 coordinates a sequence of operations on an object selected through a caller-supplied context. It invokes an initial virtual operation, polls until a dependency returns zero, performs preparation, collects three virtual-call results, passes those results through construction and transformation dependencies, and submits a derived value. It then calls a cleanup-like dependency on two temporary locations and returns two final 32-bit words packed into a 64-bit result.

The established contract is the ordering of observable calls, memory accesses, argument values, and result assembly. The application-level meanings of the object, temporary values, submission, and result are not established.

Waiting for readiness and constructing a request are plausible interpretations, not required semantics. In particular, zero from the polling dependency is a continuation condition; the source does not establish that it means application-level success.

- Bits 32–63: final contents of the **high-result word**.

Result assembly occurs after both cleanup-like calls return. Consequently, the returned bits are the contents present at that final assembly, not necessarily the values immediately after F-656819eb70e0. No sign extension, arithmetic addition, or interpretation as a success/error code is established.
