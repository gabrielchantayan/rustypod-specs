The supplied static representation ends at a reported invalid-instruction boundary. It does not establish a normal return, a successful completion status, or the behavior of execution beyond that boundary.

**Contract scope:** the behavior below describes the visible prefix, conditional on execution reaching each assignment and the relevant destination storage being accessible. It is not a guarantee that a real execution completes all seven writes.
