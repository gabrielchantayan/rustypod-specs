# F-9ee57664a5b8

The established behavior is invocation ordering and memory mutation. Parsing, formatting, initialization, allocation, and object construction are not established purposes. Character-like constants do not establish text output.

This contract distinguishes:

### Widths and arithmetic

The representation distinguishes 8-bit bytes, 16-bit halfwords, 32-bit words, and represented 64-bit results. Narrowing for byte stores retains the low eight bits; unsigned byte loads are zero-extended when subsequently used as words.

For a represented 64-bit result, the lower word contains bits 0–31 and the upper word bits 32–63. Whole-result logical shifts by 34, 36, or 44 bits are not shifts of an already truncated lower word.

For example, successive stored 32-bit left shifts by 28 bits produce zero under this interpretation. A word shifted left by at least eight bits has a zero low byte. Address wraparound, signed overflow, and signed right-shift behavior remain conditional on the underlying machine.

The final represented two-word result supplies an upper-word address base and a lower-word scaled address. Earlier dependency results supply other retained bases.

The opening direct effects include a word store at the final upper word plus `0x14`, a byte store at a retained shifted base plus `0x09`, and a word store at 256 times the final lower word plus `0x54`.
