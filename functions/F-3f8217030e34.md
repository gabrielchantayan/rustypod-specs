# F-3f8217030e34

## Purpose

F-3f8217030e34 invokes one operation between two calls that receive the same embedded-object address. On the ordinary returning path, it preserves the middle operation’s 32-bit result and returns that result after the final call finishes.

The established behavior is an ordered three-call wrapper. A synchronization or resource-guarding role is plausible because the outer calls bracket the middle call and receive the same address. Their actual meanings are not established. This contract therefore does not promise locking, exclusion, acquisition, release, or cleanup.
