# F-3325f1cb8258

1. Invoke a preliminary dependency without explicit arguments and obtain a 32-bit result.
2. Invoke an indirectly selected dependency with that result as its first argument and the entry point’s original 32-bit input as its second argument.

The representation shows no interpretation, validation, or transformation of either argument. It also shows no explicit result returned to the caller.
