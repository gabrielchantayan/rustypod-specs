### Ordered status inspection

After a nonzero preliminary result, the routine reads the first status field at B + 98.

If that field is nonzero, the routine returns. The second status field is not read directly on this path, and neither repeated operation is called.

If the first field is zero, the routine reads the second status field at B + 99.
