## Acceptance examples

### Initial forwarding

### Stable linked-storage case

If H is valid, all relevant accesses are stable, and the linked-field writes do not overlap A + 0x14, then the linked-storage phase leaves A + 0x14 equal to H, H + 0x04 equal to zero, and H + 0x08 and H + 0x0C equal to H.

### Distinct observations of T₂

- 0x2000 at R + 0x00;
- 0x1064 at R + 0x40.

Both results follow from their respective reads. Requiring 0x2064 at R + 0x40 would incorrectly substitute the second observation for the saved first observation.

If both reads instead produce 0x1000, the corresponding writes are 0x1000 and 0x1064. Equality of the observations is a condition of this example, not a guarantee of the routine.

### Record boundaries

The first record writes index 0 at R + 0x56 before calling F-external-02 with R + 0x57 and 0x41.

The last record writes index 31 at R + 0x854 before calling that dependency with R + 0x855 and 0x41. There is no 33rd iteration.

### Final invocation and result
