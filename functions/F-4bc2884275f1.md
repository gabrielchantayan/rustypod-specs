### Narrow-address constraint

The preceding left shift is essential to the narrow value’s domain.

Under the 32-bit machine-bit interpretation, shifting left by eight clears the low eight bits. Masking to 15 bits retains only seven potentially nonzero bits above them. The subsequent logical right shift by six therefore produces multiples of four:

- Narrow value: 0, 4, 8, …, 508.
- Narrow base: 0, 8, 16, …, 1016.

Equivalently, the narrow value is four times the original result’s low seven-bit unsigned field. Values such as 509, 510, and 511 are excluded. No allocation base is added to the doubled value.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static obligations, not executed tests.

1. **Unsigned widening.** If a designated unsigned byte read yields 255, its widened word-store payload is 255, not a sign-extended negative value.

2. **Narrow maximum.** If the original result’s low seven bits are all set, the shift-mask-shift derivation yields 508 and the narrow base is 1016. Results of 511 and 1022 are unacceptable under the machine-bit interpretation.

3. **Fixed-byte ordering.** If the byte-derived access region aliases address 1, its accesses occur after address 1 receives the narrow base’s low byte. The earlier byte read at address 5 remains the retained source of the base.

4. **Fixed-word payload.** If the saved link word is 7 and arithmetic is representable, the word store at fixed address 84 carries 14.

5. **Intermediate output-like effects.** Working base plus 32 receives 32, 47, the byte-derived value, then 32. Observing only the final 32 is insufficient.

6. **Publication-base capture.** A mutation of its fixed-storage source caused by the fourth output-like store must precede the publication-base read. The address-96 word store occurs after that read.

8. **Constrained shift count.** A count of 24 satisfies the established congruence; a count of 16 does not. A count of 40 satisfies the congruence but has **documented_only** shift behavior.

9. **Halfword width.** The store of 101 affects 16 bits. Replacing it with an eight-bit store is not equivalent.

10. **Final invocation.** Completion of preceding effects leads to arguments 114, the designated offset-100 value, the post-address-58 offset-20 capture, and the later offset-116 value. No success result is synthesized.
