Established behavior uses eight initialized traversal states, two destination bases, one shared destination stride, and coefficient locations of unknown mutability. The function reads these coefficient locations; the supplied body does not establish their writability, storage permissions, values, or initialization rules.

## Behavior

### Initial dependency and geometry

The first dependency call is F-d7be9cfb4000. Its arguments are the 32-bit value read from source-context byte offset 4 and the constant 5.

Let its 64-bit result be **R**. Two quantities are derived:

- **Extent:** the low 32 bits of R, converted to a signed 32-bit integer and multiplied by 5.
- **Initial selector:** R shifted logically right by 33 bits, then converted to an unsigned 32-bit value.

Equivalently, the initial selector is the upper 32-bit half of R with its least significant bit discarded.

The shared stride **S** is read from descriptor word 4 after F-d7be9cfb4000 returns and before F-c8a7bc15a71d is called.

F-c8a7bc15a71d receives the descriptor address. Its 32-bit result is a common destination adjustment. After that call returns:

1. Descriptor word 0 is read and combined with the adjustment to form **D₀**, the first adjusted base.
2. Descriptor word 1 is read and combined with the same adjustment to form **D₁**, the second adjusted base.
3. Descriptor words 8 and 3 supply the processed-column-count calculation.

Thus, descriptor changes made by F-c8a7bc15a71d can affect both bases and the count. A change to word 4 does not replace the stride already captured.

For a representable signed 32-bit product **P** of words 8 and 3, the processed-column count **N** is P divided by 8 with truncation toward zero. The supplied calculation adds 7 for a negative product before the signed right shift. It is not simply floor division for negative values.

The extent, adjusted bases, stride, and count are retained across all groups rather than recomputed per group.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional acceptance criteria, not executed tests. Required accesses and dependency calls are assumed valid and normally completing.

1. **Zero extent still initializes states.**  
   With a low half of R equal to zero, the function calls F-c8a7bc15a71d and all eight initializers. It makes no per-group calls and performs no destination stores.

2. **Selector subtraction wraps.**  
   With an upper half of R equal to zero, initializer selectors are `0xffffffff`, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, in order.

3. **Zero columns do not suppress group work.**  
   With a low half of R equal to 1 and N equal to zero, one group obtains eight source addresses, calls F-870c4fcf7295 with 0 and 10, performs no source-byte reads or destination stores, and advances every state.

4. **Negative products truncate toward zero.**  
   For representable descriptor products, −7 gives N equal to zero and −8 gives N equal to −1. Neither permits column processing, although a positive extent still permits group calls and advancement.

5. **One ordinary column has six ordered stores.**  
   With one group, N equal to one, distinct valid destinations, and stable coefficients, the six tabled stores occur at column zero. The first value is x₁ without scaling.

6. **Saturation thresholds are exact.**  
   Completed signed accumulators of −1, 1,048,575, 1,048,576, and `0x0ff00000` produce 0, 0, 1, and 255 respectively. A larger positive signed accumulator also produces 255.

7. **Narrow and full-width B differ.**  
   With stable B equal to `0x00010000`, its second-store contribution is zero because its signed low 16 bits are zero. Its sixth-store contribution is not inherently zero; the result still depends on the other terms, arithmetic limits, and saturation.

8. **Descriptor mutation follows capture order.**  
   If F-c8a7bc15a71d changes word 0, the changed base is used. If it changes word 4, the previously captured stride remains in use.

9. **Collapsed destinations retain store order.**  
   With zero stride and equal adjusted bases, all six stores for a column target one byte. The sixth store is the final store for that column. Earlier stores may additionally affect coefficient reads only if that byte overlaps writable, valid coefficient storage.

10. **A retained coefficient is not reread.**  
    If a valid destination store changes writable storage belonging to E after E has been captured, the fifth store still uses the captured E value for that column. This example grants writable overlap as a condition; it does not establish coefficient mutability generally.
