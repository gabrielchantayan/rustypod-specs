# F-0a6db59f399a

## Purpose

Its established behavior is to initialize three output fields, acquire a context value, obtain and read another record, invoke one dependency twice with different temporary-storage addresses, write two bytes through the second returned address, and invoke a final dependency.

The final dependency receives five arguments, including the address of a temporary word and the numeric constant `0x10`. The evidence does **not** establish that this constant is a byte count, an element count, a size, or a limit.

Terms such as “context,” “lookup,” and “construction” describe apparent roles in the call sequence. They do not establish application semantics or dependency contracts.
