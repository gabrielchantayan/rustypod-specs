A field at byte offset **3840 (0xF00)** supplies the address of an inner memory region. A field at byte offset **2864 (0xB30)** within that inner region supplies the returned value.

The second read is data-dependent on the first: its address cannot be determined from the input alone without obtaining the outer field’s value. No other explicit data-memory reads appear in the supplied body.
