### Writes after normal dependency return

1. It writes an eight-bit zero at region-base offset `0x94`.
2. It writes a 32-bit zero at region-base offset `0x8c`.

The four-byte store covers offsets `0x8c` through `0x8f`. The one-byte store covers offset `0x94` only. These two explicit destinations do not overlap. Offsets `0x90` through `0x93` are not covered by either store.
