### Arithmetic interpretation

Low-word extraction and high-word extraction are distinct. Logical shifts of unsigned 64-bit results by 34, 36, and 44 bits select the high word shifted right by two, four, and twelve bits, respectively.

A recurring selection retains the low 20 bits of a word and then shifts right by ten. It selects original bits 10–19 and ranges from 0 through 1023. Other expressions retain fields of 24, 22, 19, 17, 13, and smaller widths.

Under a **conditional 32-bit bit-vector interpretation**:

- Left shift by eight followed by logical right shift by eight retains the original low 24 bits.
- Successive valid left shifts with total displacement at least 32 discard all original bits.
- A mask after a shift acts on the already truncated intermediate.
- Scaling, addition, and subtraction wrap modulo 2³².
- Byte truncation retains the low eight bits; halfword truncation retains the low 16 bits.

These are not unconditional guarantees for expressions represented as signed arithmetic. These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

3. **Field extraction.** Selecting bits 10–19 from 0x000FFC00 produces 1023.

7. **Captured global base.** If the store at 0x60 affects the global supplying G, repeated stores still use G captured before that store.

8. **Interleaved captures.** If fixed byte 0x3A overlaps a later final-phase capture, that capture reflects the write according to the environment’s byte order. Hoisting the capture changes the contract.
