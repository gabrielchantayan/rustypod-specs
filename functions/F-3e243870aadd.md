### Width-sensitive arithmetic

Most working quantities are represented as 32 bits. Established transformations include:

- Low-24-bit and low-22-bit extraction.
- Smaller masked fields, including 20-, 19-, 18-, 17-, 14-, 13-, 12-, and 11-bit selections.
- Unsigned right shifts.
- Left shifts at the represented intermediate width.
