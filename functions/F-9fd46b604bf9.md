# F-9fd46b604bf9

The established behavior is **partial initialization followed by delegation**, not clearing an entire object. The direct writes cover selected locations within a 64-byte address span. Several intervening bytes receive no direct write. Two four-byte locations receive an all-ones value rather than zero.

The apparent nested iteration does not establish initialization of a larger collection. Under the unsigned comparison shown in the supplied representation, the outer body executes exactly once. The inner body executes twice.
