## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static examples, not executed tests.

1. **Input forwarding.** With a callable entry destination and input `0x89ABCDEF`, F-U001 receives `0x89ABCDEF` unchanged.

2. **Extraction widths.** Incoming register-derived state `0xFFFFFFFF` produces `0x1FFFF` from bits 2–18 and `0x1FFF8` from the low-fourteen-bit scaling.

3. **Restricted index.** When the relevant result’s low seven bits are all set, the late index is 508 and the compact base is 1016. An index of 511 is incompatible with the represented selection.

4. **Opening zero capture.** With valid accesses and ordinary sequential memory, scaled-base `+0x14` is captured as zero, whether the compact-base clear is disjoint or targets the same word.

5. **Intermediate store preservation.** Shifted-base `+9` receives the high-half-derived byte before its zero overwrite. A final zero alone does not establish the complete access sequence.

6. **Stored-base timing.** If the final calculated `+0x20` publication overlaps the stored-base slot, its effect precedes the capture. The capture precedes the `0x60` word store and remains retained afterward.

7. **Final capture overlap.** If twice-the-late-word `+0x14` equals `0x38`, its four-byte capture includes address `0x3A`. It therefore includes the preceding `0x3A` byte mutation according to the environment’s endianness.

8. **First `0x67` propagation.** If the retained `0xA9` capture is `0x123456AB`, the `0x67` byte store publishes `0xAB`. With ordinary memory and no external mutation, the subsequent `0x21` store also publishes `0xAB`, even if the intervening calculated word read overlaps `0x67`.

10. **Final invocation.** On reaching F-U003, its first argument is `0x72`; its third remains the earlier final-region `+0x14` capture despite subsequent overwrites.

11. **Invalid environment.** An inaccessible required address or uncallable destination has no specified successful-completion result or prescribed recovery.
