## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static acceptance criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Captured global survives later mutation.** If the entry-byte store changes G02’s storage, its capture observes that change. If the following doubled-address byte store changes G02 again, later uses still consume the captured value.

2. **Carry-global read ordering.** If the G13-derived halfword store overlaps G14 or G15, their subsequent captures observe the resulting storage. The previously captured G13 value is not implicitly refreshed.

3. **Central saved-word ordering.** G19 is captured after both branch-plus-9 byte writes and before the root-plus-`0x14` read. Aliasing must respect that order.

4. **Distinct central installations.** Hub plus `0x54` receives cursor; self base plus `0x54` receives self base; hub plus `0x44` receives root. They are not one generic link update.

5. **Pointer reread.** The second read from second doubled contribution plus lane occurs after an intervening halfword mutation. Its result, not the earlier pointer capture, supplies cursor.

6. **Halfword truncation.** A second-argument contribution of `0xffff` and target contribution of `0x0002` produce initial stored halfword `0x0001`, subject to a valid destination.

7. **Entry carry.** With third argument 3, the first doubled contribution is 6 or 7 for incoming carry zero or one respectively.

8. **Intermediate writes.** First region plus `0x1c` receives both `0x79` and `0x68`. An observer capable of seeing individual writes must not be shown only the latter.

9. **Terminal capture.** A final store overlapping anchor plus `0x44` does not change the already captured third call argument.

10. **Character and shift boundaries.** Under signed 8-bit character extension, terminal byte `0xff` converts to `0xffffffff`, unchanged by OR with `0x44`. A displayed shift count of 32 has no platform-independent expected result here.
