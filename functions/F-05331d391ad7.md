5. **Retained selector across dependencies.** If \(c=7\), the word clear after F-U015 begins at address 75 even if that dependency changes the byte at \(W+1\). The next direct word clear targets absolute address 36.

7. **Separate wide-result halves.** If F-U018 produces \(Lo=3\) and \(Hi=5\), F-U019 receives 12. After it returns, the high-half-derived word clear targets address 78.

8. **Repeated clearing is observable.** If a dependency changes the word at address 36 between two direct clears, the later clear writes zero again. A specification that preserves only the eventual value omits an observable event.
