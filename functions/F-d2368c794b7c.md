# F-d2368c794b7c

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional review criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Combined-result widths.** A combined result with low portion 1 and high portion 2 supplies 256 and 2 to an interaction requiring the low portion shifted left by eight and the unchanged high portion.

2. **Compact boundary.** Under 32-bit bitvector arithmetic, compact-index source 0xFFFFFFFF yields index 508 and compact base 1016. Memory accessibility remains a separate prerequisite.

3. **Mask before shift.** Selecting the low 24 bits of 0xFF000001 before shifting left by six yields 64. Shifting the unrestricted value is not equivalent.

4. **Publication-base timing.** A recorded access trace must place the word read at 0x087FBBBC before the word store of 0x20 at 0x60.

5. **Fresh fixed-byte input.** If the saved 0xA9 word has low byte 0x12 but the later 0x67 read returns 0x34, fixed address 0x21 receives 0x34. The later two reads can likewise supply different bytes when intervening aliases modify 0x67.

6. **Closing reloaded-byte provenance.** If the closing reloaded word is 0x123456AB, the closing selected destination plus 0x11 receives byte 0xAB. The second-argument read precedes this byte write; the decimal-100 word store at that destination plus 0x54 follows it.

7. **Byte publication affecting a later argument.** If the selected-destination-plus-0x11 byte address overlaps the later fourth-argument source, that word read observes memory after the byte publication and decimal-100 store. The already retained second argument is not re-read.

8. **Final closing-base overwrite.** If the final selected base’s low byte is 0x7D and the earlier closing-base-plus-nine byte was 0x20, the later overwrite publishes 0x7D after the fourth-argument and final-destination reads, but before the final two word stores. A later aliased store may overwrite it again.

9. **Width and failure.** Halfword 0x65 cannot be replaced with a byte store merely because its low byte matches. If the halfword access fails, graceful return and rollback are not promised.
