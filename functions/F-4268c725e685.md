### Meaning of zero

Zero suppresses an invocation at one position; it does not terminate traversal or permanently disable dependency calls.

A negative companion may advance through zero under defined arithmetic. The position associated with zero omits its invocation, while later nonzero companion values again require calls. The wrapper has no explicit rejection of negative values. Whether the dependency accepts them is unresolved.

Zero can also be returned normally. For example, one completed step from a companion of negative four produces a zero result.
