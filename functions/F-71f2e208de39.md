## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static examples, not observed tests.

1. **Input scaling.** Under 32-bit bit-vector arithmetic, third input 1 produces 8 after the initial shift and 8,192 after the subsequent ten-bit shift. Input `0x20000000` loses all bits in the initial shift.

2. **Low-address derivation.** A contributing result of `0xffffffff` produces S equal to 1,016. A contributing result of 8 produces S equal to `0x40`. Neither case establishes pointer validity.

3. **Opening overwrite.** R plus `0x09` receives H’s byte conversion before R’s byte conversion. The intervening word store remains required even when the final bytes match.

4. **Aliasing at fixed `0x54`.** Suppose S is `0x40`, A is 3, accesses succeed, and the missing global declaration specifies a 32-bit destination covering the same storage as S plus `0x14`. The ordered publications at that storage are the intermediate shifted value, 6 from the fixed assignment, 3 from the first A store, and 3 from the second A store. A trace placing 6 after either A store is unacceptable, even if a later store restores the same final contents.

5. **Unknown global width.** Without the extra declaration in example 4, the assignment’s position and value expression remain established, but a 32-bit global store and its exact overlap extent are not required.

6. **Global conversion.** If the initial global value converts to unsigned 32-bit `0x1234`, F is `0x1234`, not `0x34`. No byte-only restriction follows.

7. **Capture before assignment.** If the additional capture slot overlaps global `0x60`, G retains the value captured before assignment of `0x20`, subject to the unresolved source conversion.

8. **Fresh reads.** If the two later global `0x67` reads convert to bytes `0x12` and `0x34`, the corresponding byte stores at U plus `0x09` publish those respective values.

9. **Final widths and failure.** With valid nonoverflowing accesses, Z equal to `0x100` places 16-bit `0x65` at `0x200`. If the later doubled-E 32-bit store faults, earlier effects may remain and F-unknown-03 need not occur.
