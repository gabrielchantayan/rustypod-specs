# F-8ea912a2b37d

## Purpose

F-8ea912a2b37d performs two object-byte writes, invokes six dependencies, reads an object byte, initializes a 4-byte temporary according to that byte, and invokes three further dependencies. The middle of those final three calls receives the temporary’s address.

The selected initialization value is decimal 17 when the observed byte is zero and decimal 9 otherwise. This establishes the temporary’s assigned value **before** F-338d16f9ce72 executes. It does not establish its contents when its address subsequently reaches F-1216949dec94: the intervening dependency’s effects are unknown.

The established contract is an ordered sequence of accesses and calls. Initialization, mode switching, and protected delivery are possible interpretations, not established semantics. In particular, calls surrounding F-1216949dec94 are not established as lock acquisition and release.

This specification describes static evidence only. No runtime tests have been performed.
