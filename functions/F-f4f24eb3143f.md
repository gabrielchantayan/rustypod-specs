# F-f4f24eb3143f

## Purpose

The established behavior is orchestration, not a demonstrated application-level operation. Every invocation that progresses normally through preparation attempts to set a designated one-byte state field to one. Two subsequent zero-result checks determine whether the final consumer and callback are reached.
