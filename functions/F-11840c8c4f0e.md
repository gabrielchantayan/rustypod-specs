### Values entering direct mutation

The following are explanatory labels, not recovered identifiers or object types:

- **Returned pair:** the last combined dependency result before direct mutation.
- **Packed token:** a captured low result shifted left by 25 bits.
- **Packed base:** another captured result scaled by \(2^{25}\).
- **Retained payload:** an earlier result shifted left by eight bits.
- **Selected value:** a result first shifted left by eight bits at 32-bit width, then masked to its low 15 bits, then unsigned-shifted right by six.

The selected value has its low two bits clear. Its possible values are **0 through 508 in multiples of four**. Consequently, the compact base is **0 through 1016 in multiples of eight**.

Equivalently, under the stated word interpretation, the compact base is eight times the original result’s low seven-bit value. It is not an arbitrary even number through 1022. These arithmetic restrictions do not prove address validity.

The packed token and packed base have their low 25 bits clear.
