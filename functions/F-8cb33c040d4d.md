# F-8cb33c040d4d

## Purpose

F-8cb33c040d4d maps two consecutive signed integer values to a signed integer result using their directed numerical separation and a two-bit rightward scaling operation.

For a representable nonnegative separation, the result is the number of complete four-unit groups in that separation. It depends on the distance between the values, not on either value’s alignment to an absolute four-unit boundary.

For a representable negative separation, the exact result depends on the execution environment’s rule for rightward scaling of negative signed integers. The function does not establish division rounded toward zero, an absolute distance, or a nonnegative result.

**Inferred intent:** a quarter-resolution measurement of a directed interval. The meanings and units of the two values are unknown. Calling them “starting endpoint” and “ending endpoint” describes their numerical roles, not an established application-level data model.

## Behavior

### Nonnegative separation

When the ending endpoint is at least as large as the starting endpoint, and their full separation is representable, the result counts complete groups of four.

| Full directed separation | Required result |
|---:|---:|
| 0 through 3 | 0 |
| 4 through 7 | 1 |
| 8 through 11 | 2 |
| 12 through 15 | 3 |

The same grouping applies throughout the representable nonnegative domain. Any remainder is discarded.

This is distance-based grouping, not independent rounding of the endpoints. A movement from 7 to 8 produces zero even though it crosses an absolute multiple-of-four boundary. A movement from 7 to 11 produces one.

Endpoint signs do not introduce a separate rule. A movement from −13 to −5 has separation eight and produces two. A movement from −3 to 5 also produces two.

Equal endpoints produce zero, including equality at either signed extreme. Equality does not waive the requirement for two accessible elements.

Translating both endpoints by the same amount preserves the result when the translated endpoints and their full separation remain representable. This property does not authorize overflow while constructing the translated input.
