### Range-dependent interpretation

If both stored values are constrained externally to the range zero through three, their difference lies between negative three and positive three. Adding four then produces a value between one and seven. Under that precondition, the result is always zero through three and agrees with a forward displacement around a four-position cycle.

More generally, a difference of at least negative four makes the adjusted difference nonnegative, provided the arithmetic remains representable. Differences below that threshold can yield negative remainders. A negative adjusted difference that is exactly divisible by four still yields zero.
