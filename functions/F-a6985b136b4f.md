### Selection and dependency invocation

After capture, control depends on a candidate’s relationship to the captured reference:

- A candidate equal to the reference does not trigger a dependency invocation or return. Evaluation continues using the successor candidate.
- A candidate whose difference from the reference is one triggers an invocation of F-b4b48080e55c. The accumulator is passed as its argument. If the dependency returns, its result replaces the accumulator, and evaluation continues using the terminal candidate.

The initial candidate is considered first. The successor and terminal candidates remain the captured values; the routine does not refresh them from the shared scalar during the loop.
