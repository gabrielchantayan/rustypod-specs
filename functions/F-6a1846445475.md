## Acceptance examples

These are conditional criteria, not executed tests.

- **Packing:** An early result of 0x00F80000 yields a five-bit field of 31. Under bit-vector arithmetic, shifting it by three yields 248; shifting it by 31 yields 0x80000000; shifting that latter result by thirty yields zero.
- **Compact range:** A compact-index input of 0x7F yields index 508 and compact base 1016.
- **Fixed-derived width:** If evaluation and unsigned conversion at fixed 0x05 yield 0x180, later fixed-derived-base accesses use 0x180, not 0x80.
- **Narrow supplied value:** Compact base 0x3F8 supplies 0xF8 to fixed 0x01; this does not establish an eight-bit destination access.
- **Early alias-sensitive store:** If other-base offset 0x54 aliases compact-base offset 0x74, the scaling-input-shifted-by-26 store occurs before both following self-reference reads. With ordinary memory and no intervening modification, those reads observe the newly stored word.
- **Traversal timing:** The compact-base-offset-0x34 overwrite precedes the fresh offset-0x74 read used to derive the early doubled base. Its stores at anchor offset 0x44 and early-traversal-base offset 0x44 cannot be replaced by a value from an earlier read.
- **Character arithmetic:** Under bit-vector arithmetic, anchor 0x65 gives adjusted value 0xFE. Signed eight-bit character conversion can yield −2 and expression value 32; unsigned conversion yields 254 and expression value 2080. Both have low byte 0x20, but full destination conversion remains unresolved.
- **Fresh fixed-global evaluations:** A fixed snapshot of 0x123456AB supplies 0xAB before destination conversion at 0x67. If its second and third later evaluations have low bytes 0x44 and 0x55, their computed byte consumers receive those respective bytes.
- **Offset-0x40 snapshot:** If transformed-base offset 0x40 aliases publication-base offset 0x74, an old word there is sampled before the field snapshot overwrites it. The later transformed-base-offset-0x10 store uses that saved old word, assuming other accesses do not intervene at the sample location.
- **Argument preservation:** Later changes at doubled-traversal-base offset 0x14 do not replace the saved third dispatch argument.
- **Partial failure:** A nonreturning intermediate invocation prevents subsequent visible memory-phase effects and final dispatch; earlier effects need not be undone.
