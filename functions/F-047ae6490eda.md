### Incoming-payload phase

All 21 stores use the incoming eight-bit payload, without refreshing it from later address producers.

The first store is at twice the first explicit input. It occurs before the first external interaction, which receives that input unchanged. No direct read of the destination precedes the store.

For the ordinary later stores, a fresh signed 32-bit result supplies a destination through a three-bit left shift. A following interaction receives two equal arguments obtained from that same producer through a fourteen-bit left shift.

Three exceptions are significant:

- **Returned address producer:** One interaction receives an earlier primary result shifted left two bits. Its returned result—not its input—determines a destination at twice that result. The following equal arguments use a twelve-bit left shift of the returned result.
- **Fourth-input contribution:** One store is followed by a four-argument interaction. Its first, second, and fourth arguments are equal fourteen-bit-shifted quantities from the destination producer. The fourteen-bit-scaled quantity associated with the seventeenth incoming-payload store is retained for later address calculations. It is historical state, not the latest available primary result.
