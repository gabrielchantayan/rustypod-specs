# F-12f219a5a799

## Purpose

F-12f219a5a799 performs a dependency-driven sequence of indirect invocations, transforms dependency results, and then makes ordered byte, halfword, and word mutations before a final indirect dispatch.

The supplied representation does not establish an application-level purpose. Clearing fields, storing self-referential numeric values, and repeatedly publishing related values suggest initialization-like or link-like activity only as **[INFERENCE]**. They do not establish allocation, formatting, serialization, or a particular container operation.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** widths, accesses, arithmetic relationships, and ordering visible in the supplied representation.
- **[INFERENCE]:** interpretations not required for acceptance.
- **documented_only:** unresolved behavior for which this contract makes no concrete equivalence claim.

### Widths and arithmetic

Bytes, halfwords, words, and represented double-width results are respectively 8, 16, 32, and 64 bits.

Byte stores retain the low eight bits. Byte reads widened to words are zero-extended. Halfword and word accesses cover two and four bytes respectively. Numeric extraction of a 64-bit result’s components does not establish memory byte order.

Unsigned right shifts insert zeros. Masks select distinct fields and must not be treated as interchangeable. For example, a masked unsigned 24-bit quantity shifted left by six ranges through 1,073,741,760 without exceeding 32 bits.

Many other shifts and multiplications involve represented signed 32-bit quantities. Overflow, negative left operands, and the relationship between the representation and executable arithmetic remain **documented_only**. Where this contract gives modular results, they are conditional on confirmed 32-bit bit-vector semantics.

Under that interpretation, each intermediate result is truncated to 32 bits. Two successive left shifts by 28 therefore produce zero, not an unbounded mathematical product.

A late signed right shift uses a count derived from an address value’s low byte. Sign propagation and counts of 32 or greater require platform evidence; no count masking or clamping is prescribed.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional review criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Input extraction:** Third-argument values 0 and 1023 contribute zero to both extraction-dependent positions; 1024 contributes 2048; 1,048,575 contributes 2,095,104.

2. **Unsigned widening:** A sampled byte of 255 produces a word containing 255, not a sign-extended negative value.

3. **Compact address arithmetic:** Under confirmed 32-bit bit-vector semantics, the compact index is divisible by four and at most 508; its doubled base is divisible by eight and at most 1016.

4. **Early fixed word:** If the captured link is 7 and all relevant accesses complete, the fixed-address-84 word assignment contains 14. Later aliasing may change that storage; the example specifies the assignment, not the final contents.

5. **Publication boundary:** An observer of the represented trace sees the publication-base capture before the word assignment of 32 at address 96, and that assignment before publication stores. Re-reading the base after the address-96 write is not equivalent when they overlap.

6. **Late capture boundary:** If the address-58 byte assignment overlaps a subsequent offset-20 or offset-4 capture, that capture uses the post-assignment memory state. The word captured earlier from address 169 remains the earlier sampled value.

7. **Fresh address-103 reads:** If a derived store changes address 103 between its assignment and a later read, the corresponding publication-operand offset-9 assignment uses the newly sampled byte. Reusing the original low byte is not accepted.

8. **Intermediate writes:** The four-write destination at publication-operand offset 32 receives 32, 47, a sampled byte widened to a word, and 32. Final-state equality alone does not establish trace equivalence.

9. **Final dispatch:** With valid preceding execution, the target receives 114 and the three specified captured words. Later overlapping writes do not retroactively alter those captures.

10. **Invalid access:** If a reached address is inaccessible, no compensating mutation or recoverable error return is established.
