4. **Intermediate truncation.** A captured word shifted left by 28 twice becomes zero under that model, regardless of its initial value.

8. **Observable replacements.** Device-visible storage at U plus 0x20 can distinguish the ordered word values 0x20, 0x2f, the sampled unsigned byte, and 0x20. One final store is insufficient.
