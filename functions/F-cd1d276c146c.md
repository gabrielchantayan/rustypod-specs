# F-cd1d276c146c

On the ordinary returning path, it prepares two addressable 32-bit values, invokes a preparation dependency, passes that dependency’s 32-bit result to an indirectly selected operation on the supplied receiver, invokes a cleanup-shaped dependency, and returns the 32-bit value one.

The wrapper establishes an ordering relationship: preparation precedes receiver dispatch, and receiver dispatch precedes the final dependency call. It does not inspect the preparation result, the indirect operation’s result, or the final dependency’s result.

**Inferred intent:** the wrapper appears to adapt two incoming values into an argument suitable for a receiver operation, then finalize temporary state. This interpretation does not establish that the prepared argument is a pointer, that an allocation occurs, or that the final dependency releases memory.
