# F-3706942373da

## Purpose

The supplied static representation establishes no normal return path, success result, error result, or termination condition. Progress through any phase depends on preceding memory accesses completing and preceding dependencies returning.

An initialization or diagnostic role is **inferred intent**, not an established application-level purpose. The evidence does not identify the meanings of the dependency calls, the destination records, or the state flags.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** operations and ordering represented in the supplied material.
- **Inferred intent:** interpretations that are plausible but not required.
- **documented_only:** unresolved behavior that must not be replaced with an invented guarantee.

The first captured address and later reads of its global source must not automatically be equated. The opening writes may alias globals or other accessed memory. The same caution applies to the second global word.

### Unconditional repeating phase

The repeating phase is entered after the one-time prefix. It executes at least once if reached and contains no exit condition.

Each cycle includes these ordered semantic stages:

1. A sequence of computed calls culminates in a wide result. Its high word is reduced by four, and a subsequent dependency receives the result’s low word, high word, and that reduced value.
2. Further calls precede repeated five-word publications using an unresolved call-associated word.
3. A fresh wide result is published through a mixture of wide and shorter layouts. Shorter publications use that result’s high word in the first field.
4. Further calls produce another wide result. A subsequent dependency receives its low word, high word, and the earlier reduced value shifted right by 12 using signed interpretation.
5. Further calls precede repeated four-word publications using another unresolved call-associated word.
6. Another wide result is published through interleaved wide, four-word, and five-word layouts. The cycle finishes with wide-layout publications before repeating.

The subtraction by four is based on a newly obtained high word each cycle. It is not a persistent countdown and does not control termination.

## Acceptance examples

These examples are conditional static acceptance criteria, not executed tests.

### Split-result forwarding

If F-673ac3a35259 returns high word `0x12345678` and low word `0x000abcde`, then F-0a1488aa04cf receives those low and high words in that order. Under low-32-bit shift behavior, its third argument is `0xabcde000`.

### Shift round trip is not identity

If the earlier low word is `0x00080000`, shifting left by 12 produces `0x80000000`. Under arithmetic signed right shift, shifting that value right by 12 yields `0xfff80000`, not `0x00080000`.

A contract requiring preservation of the original low word would therefore be incorrect.

### Repeated fixed-address reads

If the first paired read observes values 7 and 9 at `0x288` and `0x28c`, its receiving call obtains 7 and 9. If intervening dependencies change those locations to 11 and 13, the later paired read may supply 11 and 13.

### Base selection

Under low-32-bit multiplication, a base-producing result of `0x12345673` yields record base `0x30000000`. It does not yield an allocation of that size or an address retaining the original upper bits.

### Upper-four-bit extraction

If the saved word at record base plus `0x228` is `0xf1234567`, the later unsigned right shift by 28 supplies decimal 15.

### Short publication preserves higher fields

After a wide publication, a four-word publication changes fields only through offset `0x8cc`. Values previously stored at `0x8d0` and `0x8d4` remain unless another actor changes them.

### Signed loop argument

If a cycle’s relevant high word is zero, subtracting four gives −4 where signed subtraction is representable. Under arithmetic signed right shift, the later third argument derived by shifting −4 right by 12 is −1.

### Dependency failure prevents later effects

If an early dependency does not return, later record publications are not reached through the represented control flow. There is no visible alternate path that completes initialization.
