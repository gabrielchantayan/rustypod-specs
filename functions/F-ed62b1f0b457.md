### Initial direct-memory effects

The last represented wide dependency result supplies separate low and high words. The first direct store writes the shifted-base value as a word at offset 20 from the high-word-derived address.

Next, the shifted base’s offset-9 byte receives the high word’s low byte. A word store at offset 84 from the low word multiplied by 256 receives the product base. The shifted base’s offset-9 byte is then written again, this time from the shifted-base value itself.

The product base’s offset-20 word is cleared before its offset-5 byte is sampled. The compact base’s offset-20 word is then cleared. Compact-base offset 21 then receives two separate byte stores from that captured offset-20 word.
