**Incremental mutation.** Both header-establishment sequences occur before the first lookup. A missing required lookup is therefore not a side-effect-free rejection.

**No explicit rollback or header release.** Before invoking F-a817d8d58c10, the function performs no explicit rollback or header-release operation. The destination’s contents at that point depend on all preceding dependency effects. In particular, the third lookup or another intervening dependency may alter or reverse changes made during earlier processing; preservation is not established.

**Writes through returned addresses.** Each header-establishment sequence writes four-byte words at relative offsets 4, 8, and 12. Those accesses require valid target ranges. The header’s first four bytes are not directly initialized through these relative writes, although aliasing or dependencies may affect them.

**Local storage exposure.** Dependencies receive addresses of four-byte temporary and scratch regions, a shared four-byte lookup-output slot, and two distinct 124-byte work regions. These passed regions are not explicitly cleared before use. The first and third processing paths reuse one temporary/scratch pair; the second uses another pair.

**Ownership remains unspecified.** The calls to F-8a5aadac202f and F-ac48cbf12373 are explicit and ordered, but their use does not prove destruction, deallocation, or reference-count adjustment. No visible header-release operation occurs in this function. Whether dependencies retain local addresses or require resources to outlive these calls is unknown.

**External effects are unrestricted by this body.** Dependencies may change reachable memory or globals, allocate resources, perform I/O, or fail to return. No preservation contract is supplied for destination fields, global words, selector values, or context state.

**Arithmetic is unchecked.** Visible address calculations use fixed offsets in a representation with four-byte address-valued words. There is no input-derived length calculation or explicit wraparound handling. A specific overflow outcome is not established; valid address calculations are a precondition.

1. **All lookups return nonzero.**
