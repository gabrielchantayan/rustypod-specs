## Behavior

### Dependency-group terminology

A **five-call group** consists of a call with the stated arguments followed by four calls without explicit arguments. Its fifth call provides the primary result when that result is subsequently used.

A **nine-call group** consists of:

- An initial call with the stated arguments.
- A no-explicit-argument call producing a result, a call forwarding that result, and another no-explicit-argument call producing a result.
- A five-call group beginning with that second result, optionally accompanied by a stated additional argument.

These are descriptions of observable call ordering, not additional helper functions. Calls without explicit arguments may still consume machine state.

The sixth call receives the retained result shifted left by two. Four further no-explicit-argument calls follow. Their final result supplies:

- The next iteration’s first argument, shifted left by two.
