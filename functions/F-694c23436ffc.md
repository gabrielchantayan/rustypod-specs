### Arithmetic constraints

Masks have independent significance. Retaining 24 bits before shifting left by six, 22 bits before shifting left by eight, or 20 bits before shifting left by ten yields at most a 30-bit unsigned result. These are not signed range checks.

Signed 32-bit left shifts and address-scaling multiplications also occur. Portable behavior for negative left operands, signed overflow, and out-of-range signed conversions is not established.
