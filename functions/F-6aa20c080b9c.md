Implementations that combine repeated reads, eliminate repeated writes, or assume separate memory regions are not justified by the supplied evidence.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests. Each assumes valid storage and admissible arithmetic through the relevant observation.

- **Opening overlap:** If the initial halfword destination overlaps source origin plus 0x44, the initial incoming word reflects the modified bytes under the applicable byte order.
- **Distinct global observations:** If a relay write overlaps its global source, captured selection remains the earlier observation. A later round must not substitute a cached global value for its own observations.
- **Position replacement:** If companion publication changes captured selection plus 0x14 in round 1, replacement position reflects that change rather than necessarily matching the incoming word.
- **Selection replacement:** Round 2 closes using its replacement selection and its original displacement. Recomputing displacement is not equivalent.
- **Character-derived address:** A character byte of 0xFF alone does not establish whether its promoted contribution is −1 or 255.
- **Displacement-only source:** With displacement −4, rounds 8 and 9 obtain replacement position from address 0x10, not entry position plus 0x10.
- **Two boundary payloads:** If round 8’s sample character-converts to 0x12 and round 9’s sample explicitly converts to 0x34, round 9 writes those payloads in that order. Round 10 uses retained 0x34 despite intervening boundary-memory changes.
- **Traversal overlap:** If the traversal-base overwrite changes previous link plus 0x14, the second product uses the changed factor.
- **Arithmetic limit:** Multiplier 32767 times multiplicand 100000 exceeds signed 32-bit range. A wrapped product address is not established.
- **Fresh late observation:** If traversal-selection writes change working base plus 0x44, its later boundary byte may differ from the character conversion of the earlier retained word.
- **Uninitialized payload:** Neither zero, an earlier boundary byte, nor the later terminal-derived byte is an established substitute for the byte lacking prior initialization.
- **Dependency mutation:** If F-indirect-01 changes the final designated global and returns, F-cdec09f5d432 receives the later observation, including any subsequent overlapping modifications.
