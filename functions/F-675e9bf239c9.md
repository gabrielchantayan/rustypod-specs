## Behavior

### Observation model

The contract distinguishes three kinds of state:

- **Retained observations:** already-read values subsequently reused.
- **Fresh observations:** later reads, even from a previously accessed address.
- **Frame-held state:** values stored in frame fields and potentially changed by overlapping writes.

An intervening mutation cannot change a retained observation. It can change a fresh observation or a later use of frame-held state.

No disjointness is established between incoming storage, selected storage, discovered addresses, globals, or the frame. Consequently, field labels do not imply independent objects. Narrow writes may affect later word reads; marker writes may affect address discovery.

The ordering requirements below concern the supplied static representation. They do not establish atomicity, volatile semantics, supported concurrency, or exact hardware transaction counts.

Where a global source is referenced separately to choose the selected destination and to supply a replacement payload, the contract does not require those references to return the same value. Additional replacements inside the chain explicitly use a retained selection instead.
