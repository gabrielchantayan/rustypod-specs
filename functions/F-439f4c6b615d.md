### Opening mutation and sampling

The opening mutations place the composite-tag base as a word at terminal upper word plus 0x14, then write the terminal upper word’s low byte at composite-tag base plus 9.

A word store containing the shifted base follows at 256 times terminal lower word plus 0x54. The next byte store returns to composite-tag base plus 9 and writes the composite-tag base’s low byte—not the eight-shift value.

The routine then clears shifted base plus 0x14, samples the unsigned byte at shifted base plus 5, clears small base plus 0x14, and stores the widened sample at shifted base plus 0x34. It next captures the word at shifted base plus 0x14 and clears small base plus 0x14 again.

The opening capture cannot universally be replaced by zero: intervening stores can overlap earlier storage, and memory-mapping semantics are unresolved.

### Converted-global linking

The routine writes the small base’s low byte to absolute address 1, then the small field’s low byte to converted-global base plus 0x15.

The eight-shift value supplies a byte store at shifted base plus 0x11 and a word store at opening capture plus 0x54. The shifted-base-plus-0x11 byte is then cleared.

Next, the routine publishes the small field at small base plus 0x34 and loads a word from shifted base plus 0x74. Relative to that loaded word, it writes the high base at offset 0x54. ## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

1. **Distinct contributors.** Composite-tag contributor 1 and eight-shift contributor 3 produce bases 0x02000000 and value 0x00000300 under 32-bit truncation. The opening byte destination is 0x02000009, not 0x00000309.

2. **Small-base derivation.** A small-field contributing scalar of 1 produces small field 4 and small base 8. A contributor of 8 produces small field 0x20 and small base 0x40.

3. **Aliasing interleaving.** Suppose small base is 0x40, high base is 0x04000000, and retained link word is 0x00001234. If this segment is reached with ordinary writable memory and the conditional arithmetic model, the four successive word values written at address 0x54 are 0x04000000, 0x00002468, 0x00001234, and 0x00001234. Ending that segment with 0x00002468, or emitting only one replacement store, does not satisfy the represented trace.

4. **Non-aliasing interleaving.** If small base plus 0x14 differs from address 0x54, the absolute-address store still occurs between the high-base store and both retained-link replacement stores.

5. **No invented global narrowing.** If the undeclared address-0x05 global converts to 0x00001234, the associated byte-store destination is 0x00001249 and retained-link read destination is 0x00001288. This does not establish that the absent declaration permits that input; it establishes that no additional byte narrowing is represented.

6. **Explicit byte widening.** A derived offset-5 byte sample of 0xFE widens to 0x000000FE. This rule does not resolve the address-0x05 global’s read width.

7. **Captured publication base.** If the address-0x60 store changes the publication-base global through aliasing, publication still uses the earlier captured value.

8. **Reload sensitivity.** If intervening stores change address 0x67, later stores receive the newly read bytes. The two adjusted-base-plus-9 reload-dependent stores may differ.

9. **Final captures.** Later mutations do not authorize reloading the third argument. The first argument remains 0x72 when the transfer is reached.
