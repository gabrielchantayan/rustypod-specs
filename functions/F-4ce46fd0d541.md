# F-4ce46fd0d541

## Purpose

If that dependency returns normally, the function clears two further locations and calls F-cdec09f5d432. If that call also returns normally, execution reaches an operation annotated as nonreturning.

The established contract is limited to preparation, interaction boundaries, and conditional continuation. It does not establish a complete object schema, resource-management purpose, successful return convention, or particular terminal outcome.

All behavioral claims below derive from the supplied static representation. No runtime tests have been performed.

### Preparation ordering and value relationships

Preparation has three ordered stages: establishing the initial shifted quantities, clearing the four preparation-clear locations, and propagating the shifted quantities to their remaining destinations.

Within the first stage, the result at 0x10 precedes the result at 0x34. Within the clearing stage, the required precedence is 0xE4 before 0x60, 0x60 before 0xA8, and 0xA8 before 0x1B8. Propagation to 0x24 precedes propagation to 0x4C, which precedes propagation to 0x188.

All preparation precedes entry to the indirect dependency. No intervening dependency interaction is displayed.

The two four-position destinations share a propagated result. The three eight-position destinations likewise share a propagated result. The evidence does not establish independent arithmetic evaluation or validation for every destination.

These are represented ordering and data-dependency constraints. They do not establish a physical instruction count, a separate memory reload for each propagation, atomic access, barriers, or visibility guarantees for other observers.

No preparation step visibly depends on the previous contents of the locations it overwrites. Preparation is not transactional: interruption may leave only a prefix of the effects, and no rollback is displayed.
