# F-029f0df8d35a

## Purpose

F-029f0df8d35a clears selected locations in caller-supplied storage. Its established behavior is five zero-valued stores affecting eleven bytes within a sixteen-byte span. It does not clear that span in its entirety.

The apparent intent is to initialize or reset selected fields of a record. That interpretation is inferred, not established: the available evidence does not identify a record type, field names, or the meaning of any cleared location. In particular, the untouched bytes must not be classified as padding without additional evidence.

This contract describes the supplied static evidence. No runtime tests have been performed.
