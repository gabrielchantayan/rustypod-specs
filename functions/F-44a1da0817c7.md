### First byte write

F-U021 returns an unsigned 32-bit word. Bit 30, selected by mask `0x40000000`, determines which value supplies the address calculation:

- **Bit 30 set:** the selected word comes from F-U021’s result shifted left by two bits.
- **Bit 30 clear:** the selected word comes from the incoming address-producing word shifted left by ten bits.

The store destination is twice the selected word. The byte written is the low eight bits of the incoming unsigned word.

F-U022 follows the write and receives two identical copies of the selected word. Its explicit arguments are not the doubled store destination.

Both branches retain the same invocation sequence and write position. The condition selects a value; it does not omit a call or the write.

### Second byte write

F-U076’s returned word unconditionally supplies the address calculation. The destination is that word shifted left by three bits.

The stored byte is bits 22 through 29 of the incoming unsigned word: the low eight bits remaining after a logical right shift by 22.

After the write, F-U077 receives, in order:

1. The F-U076 result multiplied by four.
2. The same result shifted left by ten bits.

There is no represented restoration of the overwritten byte.

### Third and fourth byte writes

F-U131 and F-U186 independently and unconditionally supply the address-producing words for the third and fourth writes.

For each:

- The destination is the returned word shifted left by three bits.
- Exactly one zero byte is written.
- The following consumer receives two identical copies of that returned word shifted left by fourteen bits.

F-U132 follows the third write; F-U187 follows the fourth. The consumer arguments are not the corresponding store addresses.

No comparison, overlap check, or repeated-write suppression is represented.
