### Conditional arithmetic model

Numerical deductions below use a **conditional 32-bit bit-vector model**:

- Left shifts discard bits beyond bit 31.
- Unsigned right shifts insert zeros.
- Addition, subtraction, and scaling wrap modulo 2³².
- Explicit eight-bit and sixteen-bit destinations retain the corresponding low bits.

This model is not an assertion that every signed-looking expression has defined wrapping semantics. Signed overflow, signed left shifts, signed right shifts, and excessive shift counts require execution semantics not supplied here.

Width matters. A word shifted left by eight and then logically right by eight retains its original low twenty-four bits. Successive left shifts totaling thirty-two can discard every bit. Shifting a 64-bit result before narrowing differs from shifting an already narrowed word.

The prefix also extracts bounded fields. Selecting twenty low bits and shifting right by ten yields at most 1023; selecting twenty-four low bits and shifting right by twenty yields at most 15. An unsigned 64-bit result shifted right by forty-four yields at most 0xfffff.
