- Two decrements by one.

The five quantity subtractions involve an unsigned 32-bit masked quantity and 32-bit destinations. Under the represented 32-bit unsigned arithmetic model, their stored results are reduced modulo **2³²**; there is no saturation or explicit borrow handling.

The two count decrements are represented with signed 32-bit arithmetic. Signed overflow behavior, including decrementing the minimum signed value, is not independently established by the excerpt. Likewise, unrestricted signed-index scaling and address calculations require target-specific interpretation when they overflow.

### Ownership and synchronization

The visible routine contains no explicit atomic read-modify-write operation. Calls surrounding the accounting sequence may provide synchronization, but their implementations are unavailable. No locking or concurrency guarantee follows from call placement alone.
