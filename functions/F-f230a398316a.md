# F-f230a398316a

## Purpose

F-f230a398316a describes a parameterless, resultless entry point whose visible behavior is an ordered sequence of indirect calls, a write to a named global destination, two zero-valued memory stores, and a final attempted call through address zero.

The supplied representation establishes the ordering and data dependencies of these operations. It does not establish a higher-level purpose such as initialization, shutdown, exception handling, or memory clearing. In particular, the zero-address call must not be silently reinterpreted as a return, reset, or deliberate trap.

This contract is source-facing and static-only. It describes the supplied representation without assuming that every represented operation is reachable or that the representation faithfully captures the executable’s calling convention.

### Ordered memory stores

1. A one-byte zero store through the unresolved register-derived pointer.
2. A two-byte zero store at the address numerically obtained by shifting the register-derived unsigned integer right by nine bits.

The second operation uses the shifted value as the destination address. It does not establish a read through the original integer value, an array index, or an offset from the byte-store destination.

The two stores are separate and ordered. Their destinations are not proven distinct. They may overlap each other, the named global destination, or state accessible to dependencies. No non-aliasing guarantee is established.

### Ownership and lifetime

No explicit allocation, release, reference-count update, or ownership transfer is established by the visible body.

- A failure at the two-byte store occurs after the byte store has been attempted.
