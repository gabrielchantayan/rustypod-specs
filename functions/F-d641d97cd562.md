# F-d641d97cd562

## Purpose

F-d641d97cd562 initializes local construction state, performs 18 ordered byte writes through a pointer held in that state, and invokes an indirect receiver operation with a bounded count and a two-field descriptor. It reports any inequality between the receiver’s 32-bit result and the offered count. On normal continuation, it invokes a finalization dependency with the original construction state.

The apparent intent is to construct and submit a fixed-format record containing constants, an 8-bit source-derived field, and a 16-bit source-derived field in most-significant-byte-first order. That intent is **inferred**, not an unconditional postcondition.

The established behavior is the sequence of accesses and calls. A complete record in one buffer requires destination-pointer stability and absence of interfering writes. Destination storage is not established to be disjoint from the source object, receiver object, limit-bearing storage, or construction state. Consequently, this function’s own writes, as well as dependency effects, may change objects used later.
