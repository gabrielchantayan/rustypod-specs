Important consequences are:

- A dependency may change the source field between those reads.
- The copied value may therefore be zero, even though the earlier gate required a nonzero value.
- The final destination is not checked for a zero field before being written.
- If the two later F-fdd8589eca2d calls return different objects, the zero test applies to one object and the write applies to another.
