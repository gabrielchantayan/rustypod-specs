### Nonzero-result path

When the indirect operation returns any nonzero integer, the function:

1. Reads the address-bearing field at byte offset 8 in the input object.
2. Reads one unsigned byte at byte offset 0x8F from that address.
3. Returns one if bit 2 of that byte is set, and zero otherwise.

Bit numbering here starts at zero for the least significant bit. Thus, bit 2 has mask 0x04. The mask isolates either zero or four, and shifting that isolated value right by two produces exactly zero or one. Other bits in the byte do not affect the result.

The magnitude and sign of the indirect operation’s nonzero result have no further effect. That result is neither returned directly nor combined arithmetically with the flag.
