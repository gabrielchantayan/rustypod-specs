## Behavior

2. A 32-bit word is read through that pointer and placed in the first word of the current-state region.
3. The field at context displacement 16 is read again, this time as a 32-bit word, and placed in the anchor word.

The second word of the current-state region is not explicitly initialized.

### Processing step

It then invokes F-11aa8eec50ad with:

- The original context argument.
- The input-snapshot address.
