The established behavior is initialization of two address-relative regions. The second region is selected by a dependency; it is not established that this region is the interior address supplied to that dependency.

**Inferred intent:** The sequence resembles initialization of a containing object and an embedded component, with shared 32-bit values installed at the beginning of each region. The repeated all-ones words may be sentinel values. Neither interpretation is required to explain the observed operations, and neither establishes a type, ownership model, or application-level meaning.

This contract describes the supplied static evidence. No runtime tests have been performed.
