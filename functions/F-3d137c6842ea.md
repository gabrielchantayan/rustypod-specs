## Behavior

### Initial record construction

Before invoking any dependency, the function initializes the three-word record from the global inputs.

The observable order of the initial value reads and corresponding local initialization is:

1. Read A and place it in the first 32-bit word.
2. Read B and place it in the second 32-bit word.
3. Read C and place it in the third 32-bit word.

These are separate reads. The supplied evidence does not establish an atomic snapshot across the three globals.

The function then performs an unsigned, one-bit right shift on each of the three local words. The processing order is the reverse of the record’s field order:

1. Third word, at offset 8.
2. Second word, at offset 4.
3. First word, at offset 0.

Each operation uses a 32-bit unsigned value. The shift therefore inserts a zero at the most significant bit and discards the least significant bit. It does not preserve a sign bit.

After those shifts, the first two words have these values:

- First word: A shifted right by one bit.
- Second word: B shifted right by one bit.

### Additional narrowing of the third word

The third word has an additional, narrower update.

The supplied representation exposes its lower 24 bits as one field and its remaining 8 bits as adjacent storage. After the 32-bit right shift, the lower 24-bit field is narrowed through an unsigned 16-bit value and written back to that 24-bit field.

Under that represented storage layout, this has the following effects:

- Bits 0 through 15 retain the corresponding bits of C shifted right by one.
- Bits 16 through 23 become zero.
- Bits 24 through 31 retain their values from the preceding 32-bit right shift.

Consequently, the third word passed onward is:

**C shifted right by one, then masked with 0xFF00FFFF.**

This is not equivalent to narrowing the whole third word to 16 bits. The top byte can remain nonzero. It is also not equivalent to simply shifting C, because the byte containing bits 16 through 23 is cleared.

The 24-bit field and adjacent-byte interpretation is supported by the supplied storage representation. Independent machine-level confirmation of that representation is outside the available evidence.
