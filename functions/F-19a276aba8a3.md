# F-19a276aba8a3

## Purpose

The established behavior is a short indirect-call-and-store sequence: invoke an unresolved callable target with four zero-valued arguments, then use its 32-bit result both as a destination address and as the value written to that address.

The supplied representation does not establish a normally returning function. After the store, control-flow recovery encounters undecodable instruction data. Consequently, this contract specifies the observable prefix that can be supported by the supplied evidence, not a complete executable lifecycle.

A possible interpretation is initialization of a self-referential word in storage returned by another routine. That interpretation is **inferred intent**, not an established allocation, object-layout, ownership, or initialization contract.
