# F-1a4445e88302

## Purpose

It ends with an unresolved indirect transfer represented as a four-argument call, followed by a return if that dependency returns normally.

No application-level purpose is established. The supplied representation does not justify interpreting this behavior as initialization, parsing, rendering, serialization, or resource management. Numeric constants do not establish text semantics.

## Behavior

### Dependency invocation phase

The first indirect target is the incoming link-related value plus 0xFFE. Subsequent targets are formed from location-derived values using the same displacement. The final target has no established origin.

Arguments draw from incoming words, primary results, combined-result halves, and implicit secondary results. Transformations include duplicated arguments, explicit zeros, left shifts, masks, and unsigned extractions. Early shifts include 24 bits for one incoming word and three, nine, or ten bits for the other.

Other transformations restrict values to low 24-bit or narrower fields. A ten-bit extraction retains the low 20 bits and shifts right by ten. Unsigned extraction of a 64-bit result by 34, 36, or 44 bits corresponds respectively to its high word shifted right by two, four, or twelve.

Some successive shifts produce zero under the conditional 32-bit interpretation. The associated invocations still occur. Zero arguments, absent represented arguments, and unused results do not establish purity.

No visible result-dependent early exit interrupts this phase. A dependency may nevertheless fault, block, transfer control elsewhere, or fail to return. Packed base is then published as a word at 256 times the final pair’s low word plus 0x54. The byte at payload plus nine is subsequently overwritten with payload’s low byte.

The selector is next published as a 32-bit word at doubled selector plus 0x34, before the word capture at packed base plus 0x74. Call that capture the **packed-selected base**.

Packed-selected base plus 0x54 initially receives packing source shifted left by 26 bits. ### Width-sensitive arithmetic

The selector cannot be 511 under this interpretation; it is a multiple of four no greater than 508.

### Converted-global destinations

If the address-0x05 global’s conversion yields 0x00000140 and selector is 0x104, fixed address 0x01 receives byte 0x08. The next selector-byte publication writes 0x04 at address 0x155, before the packed-base offset-0x11 store.
