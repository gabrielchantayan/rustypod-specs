The routine then writes the low byte of A at A plus 9. After that write, it reads a 32-bit word at A plus 52. A dependency receives, in order:

Four further calls precede the first computed word write. The routine writes V to a four-scaled slot selected by secondary result state, then reads a word at that same selector plus 20 **without scaling the selector**. The selector is subsequently passed to a dependency.

The scaled write and unscaled read are distinct accesses. An implementation must not merge them into a presumed structure-field operation.

### 2. Chained reads and replicated payloads

The routine then reads a word at 20 plus the earlier unscaled word read shifted right by 22. The primary selector multiplied by four is passed onward.

After further calls, a new primary result supplies a payload through a left shift by two. The routine:

- Uses the newly read word shifted right by one as a four-scaled slot selector.
- Writes the shifted payload there.
- Reads another word at the **unshifted** newly read word plus 20.
- Passes the right-shifted selector to a dependency.

A later primary result selects another four-scaled slot receiving the same shifted payload. G-A is read after that write and before the next call, which receives four times the later selector.

The payload derives from the saved call result. No fresh read of its origin is represented between these two writes.

### 3. High-field propagation and zero-valued writes

The 22-bit-shifted field is written to a four-scaled slot selected by secondary result state. The following call receives:

1. That selector.
2. The same selector.
3. The saved G-A value shifted right by 23.
4. The other saved word shifted right by 22.

During a subsequent obtain-and-forward sequence, the G-A-derived field is shifted right by another 23. Because the original value is unsigned and 32 bits wide, the first shift yields at most 511; the second necessarily yields zero.

Further calls separate three additional computed word writes:

- A four-scaled slot selected by unsigned secondary state receives an explicit zero.
