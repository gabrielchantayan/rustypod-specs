### Aggregate sources, grouping, and overflow

- The value read from `0x420` at aggregation time.
- The value read from `0x41C` at aggregation time.

The fourth contribution is the retained return value, not a fresh read of `0x428`. A change to the stored fourth-result field alone does not replace the contribution used for aggregation.
