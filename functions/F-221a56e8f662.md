**Address-derived content.** The single address-derived write converts its complete numerical destination to character-sized content. A following dependency also receives the complete numerical destination as an argument; the narrow store does not narrow that argument.

**Retained byte.** The seed undergoes unsigned 32-bit eightfold scaling, followed by eight-bit retention. Consequently, the retained byte depends only on the seed’s lowest five bits and has its lowest three bits clear. Unsigned scaling is modulo 4,294,967,296.

All 13 retained-byte writes and the global assignment supply this same value. This establishes logical value provenance, not a physical register-preservation rule.

**Selected field.** Seed bits 19 through 28, with the least significant bit numbered zero, form the conversion input for the selected-field write. Its range is zero through 1,023: the selection is ten bits wide.

Under low-eight-bit narrowing, the stored information corresponds to seed bits 19 through 26. The destination comes from a separate primary dependency result, not from this field.

**Final payload.** The final payload is independent of the seed. Its origin is the primary result associated with incoming-payload write 17. That result contributes to a retained signed 32-bit quantity through scaling by 16,384. Before retained-byte write 2, that retained quantity is multiplied by 32,768 using represented signed 32-bit arithmetic.

Where those calculations have an applicable interpretation, bits 29 through 31 of the resulting unsigned 32-bit pattern determine the final payload. Its range is zero through seven.
