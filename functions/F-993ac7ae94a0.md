For the signed index multiplications and additions, the stated mathematical address and argument relationships apply where every relevant intermediate is representable. Overflow cases remain **documented_only**.

Index D and Index E require a stricter condition because their arguments are formed by signed left shifts, not multiplication:

- Under portable signed-integer semantics, a negative left operand is not defined for this operation, even when four times its value would be representable.
- A nonnegative operand whose shifted result is not representable is also outside the defined range.

Consequently, negative Index D or Index E values, and nonnegative values above that range, are **documented_only** unless actual instruction semantics are independently established. Negative values are not made acceptable merely because their mathematical products fit in 32 bits.
