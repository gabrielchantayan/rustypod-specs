# F-58c4fdd75359

## Purpose

F-58c4fdd75359 performs a long, ordered series of indirect invocations, derives address-like values from implicit entry state and invocation results, performs potentially overlapping memory mutations, and reaches an unresolved terminal invocation with four represented arguments.

No application-level purpose is established. Character-like constants do not establish text output. Repeated offsets do not establish structures, object boundaries, or valid pointers.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** represented operations, explicit access widths, conversions, value dependencies, and ordering.
- **[INFERENCE]:** conclusions dependent on an additional arithmetic or memory-model premise.
- **documented_only:** behavior not resolved by the supplied material.

Intermediate observations and publications are observable behavior. Equal final memory contents alone do not establish equivalence.

## Behavior

### Indirect invocation phase

The first target is obtained by adding 0xFFE to the entry link-register value. Subsequent target expressions use embedded continuation-like values with the same displacement.

Two invocations without represented arguments precede an invocation producing a paired result. The next invocation receives:

1. The paired result’s lower word shifted left by eight.
2. Its upper word.
3. An implicit entry word shifted left by three.
4. Another implicit entry word shifted left by 25.

The extended phase contains invocations with zero through four represented arguments. Arguments depend on ordinary results, paired results, secondary register outputs, retained earlier values, and explicit zeros. Transformations include masks, unsigned right shifts, left shifts, and repeated arguments.

**Represented left-shift counts include one and two, and extend through 31.** Specifically:

- A value masked to 19 low bits is shifted left by one directly in an argument expression.
- Values masked to 19 or 24 low bits are shifted left by two before or during argument preparation.
- Larger shifts include eight, 15, 25, 30, and 31.

This does not assert that every integer count in that interval occurs.

A word-sized left shift followed by an unsigned right shift must preserve the loss of high bits under the wrapping-word premise. Unlimited-precision cancellation is not generally equivalent.

Invocations with unused primary results remain distinct interactions. Missing represented arguments do not establish a complete calling convention. Neither unused results nor repeated argument shapes establish purity, global preservation, or interchangeable targets.

No explicit loop or conditional branch is represented in this phase. The complete interaction catalogue is documented_only in this contract; the summary does not authorize replacement or reordering of invocations.

### Opening publications and clear-word capture

The opening cluster publishes shifted seed as a word at final pair’s upper-word base plus 0x14. Shifted seed plus 9 receives the upper word’s low byte. A word publication at 256 times final pair’s lower word plus 0x54 contains scaled seed. Shifted seed plus 9 is then overwritten with shifted seed’s low byte.

Scaled seed plus 0x14 is cleared with an explicit word publication. An explicit byte observation at scaled seed plus 5 is retained. Small base plus 0x14 is cleared, followed by a zero-extended word publication of the retained byte at scaled seed plus 0x34.

A fresh word observation at scaled seed plus 0x14 becomes the **retained clear value**. Small base plus 0x14 is cleared again; two distinct byte publications at small base plus 0x15 contain retained clear value’s low byte.

**[INFERENCE]** With ordinary byte-addressed memory, wrapping addresses, valid accesses, no access side effects, and no external mutation, retained clear value is zero. An overlapping zero clear cannot introduce nonzero data. The publication at offset 0x34 is 0x20 bytes from the cleared word and cannot overlap it merely through address wrapping.

The explicit observation is nevertheless retained. Device behavior, external mutation, or additional alias mappings could invalidate the ordinary-memory conclusion.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional review criteria, not executed tests.

- **Small shifts:** Masking a word to 19 low bits and shifting left by one can produce 0xFFFFE. Shifting the same maximum masked value left by two produces 0x1FFFFC. A contract excluding these shift counts is incompatible with the represented interactions.
- **Explicit widths:** An explicit unsigned byte observation of 0xE2 publishes word value 226. An explicit byte destination receiving a word ending in 0xAB stores 0xAB. Explicit halfword 0x0065 remains a 16-bit publication.
- **0x05 conversion:** If the observed global converts to unsigned word 0x1234, global-derived base is 0x1234; its offset-0x15 byte destination is 0x1249 and offset-0x54 word observation is at 0x1288, assuming supported arithmetic. No eight-bit truncation is authorized.
- **Fixed globals:** If prior link is 0x1234, the value assigned to the 0x54 global is 0x2468. If small base is 0x128, the explicit eight-bit value assigned to the 0x15 global is 0x28. Neither example establishes the destination store width or surrounding bytes affected.
- **Fresh 0x67 reads:** If a later 0x67 observation converts at an explicit byte destination to 0xA7, that destination receives 0xA7. This does not establish that the observation itself was eight bits or that the 0x21 assignment has the same footprint.
- **Clear value:** Under the ordinary-memory premises above, retained clear value is zero even when the two clear destinations overlap.
- **Retention:** A later change to the publication-destination global does not change retained publication base.
- **Temporal overlap:** If the 0x3A global mutation affects doubled link plus 0x14, restoration word reflects the subsequent word observation. Conversely, the later doubled-link offset-0x11 byte publication cannot alter an already retained adjusted-base offset-0x14 word.
