The available static representation contains no other explicit operation before this transfer and no explicit processing of its result. It depicts a return afterward. However, the accompanying recovery annotations identify the transfer as an indirect jump represented as a call because its control-flow structure could not be recovered. Consequently, ordinary call-and-return behavior is not established.

The two nonzero values must not be assigned meanings such as operation codes, sizes, offsets, or character values without additional evidence.
