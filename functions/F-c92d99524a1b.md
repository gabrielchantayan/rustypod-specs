These are conditional static criteria, not executed tests.

3. **Cached versus fresh input:** If `S[0x31c]` is `0x100` when `captured-quarter` is assigned and becomes `0x200` before F-U10, F-U10’s fourth argument remains `0x10`; its byte-read address is `0x37`, provided no intervening change occurs before that later evaluation.

6. **Repeated writes:** Reaching both stores at `(scaled >> 4) + 0x24` requires two word writes, even when their values match.
