# F-5f23d2c48d95

## Purpose

F-5f23d2c48d95 performs an ordered sequence of indirect calls, derives values from explicit input, implicit machine state, and call results, then mutates fixed and computed memory locations. It finishes with an unresolved indirect transfer represented as a call followed by return.

No application-level purpose is established. Pointer-like values, character-like constants, and repeated stores do not establish initialization, formatting, parsing, allocation, or collection management.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** operations and relationships present in the supplied representation.

## Behavior

### Indirect invocation phase

The opening phase is a long straight-line sequence of indirect invocations. The first destination derives from the incoming return link plus **0xFFE**. Later destinations derive from represented instruction-relative anchors plus the same displacement.

Represented argument lists include no listed arguments, explicit zeros, and transformed earlier results. An invocation with no listed arguments is not evidence that it has no implicit inputs.

Retained results remain associated with their producing invocation. Some arguments depend on substantially earlier results rather than the immediately preceding call. Unknown dependencies must not be assumed to preserve globals or unspecified registers.

Established transformations include:

| Transformation | Consequence under the bit-vector model |
|---|---|
| Left shift by 8 | Low eight bits are zero |
| Mask to 24 bits, then right shift by 8 | Extracts bits 8–23 |
| Mask to 24 bits, then right shift by 12 | Extracts bits 12–23 |

### Retained values entering mutation

The following descriptive terms denote values, not object types:

- **Shifted anchor:** an earlier paired result’s low word shifted left by 25.
- **Shifted payload:** a later returned word shifted left by 8.
- **Multiplication source:** the returned word whose product with 0x02000000 becomes the **initial base**.
- **Compact value:** another returned word shifted left by 8, masked to 15 bits, then right shifted by 6.
- **Final pair:** the last paired result before direct mutation.

The compact value is four times its producer’s low seven bits: 0–508 in multiples of four. Its double is 0–1016 in multiples of eight. These ranges do not establish valid memory.

The shifted anchor has 25 zero low bits. The anchor and shifted payload therefore have zero low bytes under the bit-vector model.

### Initial links, captures, and overlapping stores

The first word store places the shifted anchor at the final pair’s high word plus 0x14. A byte store places that high word’s low byte at the shifted anchor plus 9. Before that byte is overwritten with the anchor’s own low byte, a word store places the initial base at 256 times the final pair’s low word plus 0x54.

The initial base’s offset-0x14 word is cleared. Its offset-5 byte is then captured as an unsigned byte. The word at twice the compact value plus 0x14 is cleared, and the captured byte is stored, zero-extended, at the initial base plus 0x34.

A subsequent read captures the initial base’s offset-0x14 word as the **reloaded word**. It is not unconditionally zero: intervening accesses can alias it. After another clear at twice the compact value plus 0x14, two distinct byte stores place the reloaded word’s low byte at twice the compact value plus 0x15.

The routine next reads the global at **0x05** and converts its value to unsigned 32 bits. Call this converted value the **early global base**. Its underlying read width and signedness remain unresolved.

Immediately afterward:

- Fixed address **0x01** receives the low byte of twice the compact value.
- The early global base plus 0x15 receives the compact value’s low byte.
- The initial base plus 0x11 receives the shifted payload’s low byte.
- The reloaded word plus 0x54 receives the shifted payload as a word.
- The initial base plus 0x11 receives an explicit zero byte.

The word at the early global base plus 0x54 is captured as the **pivot**, then overwritten with the reloaded word. Twice the compact value plus 0x34 receives the compact value as a word.

### Early pointer-derived updates

A word loaded from the initial base plus 0x74 supplies an **initial link**. Its offset-0x54 word receives the multiplication source shifted left by 26.

A separate assignment reads twice the compact value plus 0x74 twice: once for a destination base and once for the stored word. Their operand-evaluation order is not independently established.

The pivot’s offset-0x24 word receives the pivot twice. Between those stores, the initial link’s offset-9 byte receives the pivot’s low byte and its offset-0x54 word receives the shifted payload; another low-pivot byte store follows.

The word at twice the compact value plus 0x14 then receives the multiplication source shifted left by 26. **After that store**, fixed address **0x54** receives twice the pivot as a 32-bit word. The computed offset-0x14 word is subsequently overwritten with the pivot twice.

Two byte stores at twice the compact value plus 0x15 receive the low byte of four times the pivot. **After both**, fixed address **0x15** receives the low byte of twice the compact value.

These three fixed-address stores differ in value, width, and position in the access sequence.

### Derived fields and distinct byte-selection paths

Call four times the pivot the **fourfold pivot base**, and eight times the pivot the **eightfold pivot value**.

The fourfold pivot base’s offset-0x54 word first receives the shifted payload, then receives the fourfold pivot base. Twice the compact value plus 0x34 also receives that base.

A word loaded from twice the compact value plus 0x74 is doubled to form the **doubled link value**. This value is stored at the pivot plus 0x44.

There are three relevant byte reads, with different roles:

1. An unsigned byte at the **fourfold pivot base plus 5** selects a word at that byte’s value plus 0x74. Call the resulting word the **earlier selected link**. Its offset-0x44 word receives the doubled link value.
2. Later, a word loaded from the **fourfold pivot base plus 4** supplies the **loaded byte base**. The first read of this loaded byte base’s offset-5 byte supplies a **zero-extended word store at the earlier selected link plus 0x44**. It does not select another word.
3. A second, separate read of the loaded byte base’s offset-5 byte selects a word at the captured byte’s value plus 0x74. This result is the **selected word** used in subsequent scaling.

The two reads through the loaded byte base have intervening stores and need not return equal bytes. Only the second performs the later byte-selected word load.

Surrounding mutations include:

- Alternating low-byte stores of eightfold and fourfold pivot values at the fourfold pivot base plus 0x15.
- Stores of the earlier selected link’s low byte at offset 0x0D, and self-base word stores at offset 0x14, first at the fourfold pivot base and then at a base loaded from its offset 0x64.

After the selected word is captured, its double becomes the **doubled selected base**, and its quadruple becomes the **repetition payload**. Before constant stores begin, further writes restore the fourfold pivot base’s offset-0x15 byte from the loaded byte base, its offset-0x54 word from the fourfold pivot base, its offset-9 byte from the doubled selected base, and its offset-0x14 word from the eightfold pivot value.
