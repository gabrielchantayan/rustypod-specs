These are conditional static examples, not executed tests. Each assumes prior operations complete and the described accesses are valid.

1. **Initial publication.** If the initial destination word is 3, the second input is stored as 32 bits at `0x20`. The next dependency receives 12.

2. **Byte truncation.** If the primary word is `0x3ff00000`, masking and shifting right by 20 gives `0x3ff`. The first selected-byte payload is therefore `0xff`.
