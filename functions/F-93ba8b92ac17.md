### Arithmetic interpretation

Explicit unsigned operations establish bounded extractions. For example, a 24-bit masked value shifted right by eight is at most `0xFFFF`; a 22-bit masked value shifted left by eight is at most `0x3FFFFF00`.

Other expressions use signed 32-bit operands. Signed overflow, left shifts of negative values, and arithmetic-to-address conversion prevent an unconditional portable interpretation.

Conditional examples below use a **truncating 32-bit model**: each word-width arithmetic boundary retains only the low 32 bits. This is not asserted as the universally established execution model. Intermediate truncation matters; discarded bits cannot reappear in a later right shift.
