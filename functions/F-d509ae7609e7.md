## Acceptance examples

These are conditional obligations, not executed tests. Each assumes valid reached storage and defined relevant arithmetic and conversions.

- **Distinct links:** With different round-2 first and destination links, the destination link controls the halfword destination and next payload; the first link becomes traversal.
- **Signed observation:** A halfword pattern of 0xFFFF contributes −1 to subsequent address arithmetic, not 65535.
- **Character ambiguity:** Character pattern 0x80 alone cannot establish the following routing base.
- **Saved-word identity:** If later overlap does not alter round-5 selected offset 68 before payload acquisition, that payload is the round-4 selected-offset-100 word, not an anchor snapshot.
- **Carrier-based indirection:** Different words at traversal offset 116 and carrier-base offset 116 in round 7 must resolve using the latter.
- **Snapshot lifetime:** Distinct fifth and sixth anchor observations may produce different round-9 bytes. Round 10 still consumes the sixth observation without refreshing it.
- **Fresh retained-link contents:** If retained-link offset 20 aliases the chain-region replacement, the second product observes that replacement absent intervening modification.
- **Repeated observations:** If intervening writes change third-product offset 68 through overlap, its two observations may differ; caching the first does not satisfy the contract.
- **Independent final destination:** A change visible to the late anchor-offset-20 observation may redirect both final-region bytes without redirecting saved-word-base offset 1.
- **Undefined-source boundary:** The later saved-word-derived byte establishes no required value for the earlier unresolved byte write.
- **External interference:** A nonreturning F-unknown-1 prevents subsequent interactions. If it returns after modifying the late global operand, the fresh observation can convey that modification to F-cdec09f5d432.
