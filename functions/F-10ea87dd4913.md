# F-10ea87dd4913

## Purpose

The established behavior is orchestration: call order, argument values, two reads of shared storage, and preparation of stack-backed storage. The supplied evidence does not establish the application-level meaning of the sequence. In particular, it does not establish that the function initializes a device, sends an event, changes a display, or reports successful completion.

This contract distinguishes:

- **Established static evidence:** operations and values explicitly represented in the supplied source.
- **Inferred intent:** interpretations suggested by the sequence but not required by the evidence.

All normal-path behavior below is conditional on preceding dependency calls returning normally.
