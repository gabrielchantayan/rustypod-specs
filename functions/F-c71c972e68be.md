### 2. Bounded amount calculation

- The newly observed source length minus the offset.

The supplied representation mixes signed and unsigned forms around the subtraction. The behavioral reading above concerns the recovered 32-bit value operations; it does not claim that a direct translation using signed arithmetic in another language would have identical overflow semantics.
