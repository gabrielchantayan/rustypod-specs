## Behavior

The prefix is a straight-line sequence of indirect transfers. The first target depends on incoming continuation state plus 0xFFE. Later targets derive from successive code-location values with the same displacement. Exact dependency identities and signatures are unresolved.

Visible invocations carry zero through four recovered arguments. Arguments include shifted primary results, separately transformed halves of paired results, explicit zeroes, masked fields, logical right shifts, and additional register outputs. Invocations whose results are unused remain part of the side-effect boundary.

Unsigned intermediates retain their represented 32-bit width. Successive shifts must not be evaluated at a wider width that preserves discarded bits. Extraction from a 64-bit result is distinct from shifting a 32-bit intermediate.

Visible masks retain 24, 22, 21, 20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13, 12, or 11 low bits, with a separate single-bit selection. Masking before shifting is not interchangeable with masking afterward.

For example, retaining 20 low bits, shifting right by ten, then left by six yields at most 0xFFC0. Retaining 24 low bits and shifting right by 19 yields zero through 31.

Some shifts and multiplications have signed 32-bit operands. Their overflow behavior is not established by the representation alone. Low-32-bit bit-vector arithmetic is a conditional machine interpretation, not an unconditional guarantee for those expressions. Signed right shift and out-of-range variable counts also remain unresolved.

### Opening computed-memory mutations

The following names describe retained values, not object types:

- **Scaled region:** a recent result multiplied by 0x02000000.
- **Shifted value:** another result shifted left by eight.
- **Index:** a result shifted left by eight, masked to 15 low bits, then logically shifted right by six.
- **Index region:** twice the index.
- **Packed base:** an earlier paired-result low half shifted left by 25.
- **Final pair:** the last paired result before explicit mutation.

Under 32-bit bit-vector arithmetic, the index ranges from zero through 508 in multiples of four. The index-region address ranges from zero through 1016 in multiples of eight. Neither range establishes address validity.

The final pair’s high half supplies a destination base whose offset-0x14 word receives the packed base. That high half’s low byte is stored at packed-base offset 0x09. A word at 256 times the final pair’s low half, plus 0x54, receives the scaled-region address. Packed-base offset 0x09 is then overwritten with the packed base’s low byte.

The scaled-region offset-0x14 word is cleared before its offset-0x05 unsigned byte is read. An index-region offset-0x14 clear separates that byte read from the zero-extended word store at scaled-region offset 0x34.

The scaled-region offset-0x14 word is subsequently saved as the **cleared-field snapshot**. Another index-region offset-0x14 clear precedes two byte stores of that snapshot’s low byte at index-region offset 0x15. Aliasing prevents treating the snapshot as unconditionally zero.

### Global-selected base and link transitions

Immediately afterward, global 0x05 is read and converted to 32-bit unsigned. Call the resulting value the **global-selected base**. Its original width and signedness remain **documented_only**.

The next visible memory operation assigns the index-region address’s eight-bit conversion to global 0x01. This establishes the supplied value, not an eight-bit destination access.

The index’s low byte is stored at global-selected-base offset 0x15. The shifted value’s low byte is written at scaled-region offset 0x11; its full word is written at offset 0x54 from the cleared-field snapshot; and scaled-region offset 0x11 is overwritten with zero.

Only then is the word at global-selected-base offset 0x54 retained as the **saved link**, before that field receives the cleared-field snapshot. Index-region offset 0x34 receives the index.

A word loaded from scaled-region offset 0x74 supplies another base. Its offset-0x54 word first receives the scale-source result shifted left by 26. A self-reference store also uses word reads from index-region offset 0x74. The representation contains both a destination-selecting read and a value read for that store; their intra-expression evaluation order is not independently established.

The saved link is written at its own offset 0x24, and its low byte is written at offset 0x09 of the scaled-region-loaded link. A word store of the shifted value at that link’s offset 0x54 separates this pair from a repeated pair of the same self-reference and byte stores.

The following global transition preserves this order:

- Index-region offset 0x14 receives the scale-source result shifted left by 26.
- Global 0x54 is assigned the saved link shifted left by one.
- Index-region offset 0x14 receives the saved link twice.
- Two byte stores at index-region offset 0x15 receive the low byte of four times the saved link.
- Global 0x15 is assigned the index-region address’s eight-bit conversion.

The globals’ destination widths are unresolved. The assignments to 0x01 and 0x15 do not instead receive the preceding four-times-link byte value.

### Further link-derived state

Four times the saved link defines the **fourfold region**; eight times that link is retained separately. A word read at index-region offset 0x74, doubled, supplies the **doubled link**.

The fourfold region’s offset-0x54 field receives the shifted value and is later overwritten with the fourfold-region address. Index-region offset 0x34 receives four times the saved link. Fourfold-region offset 0x15 receives successive low-byte values derived from eight times the saved link and the fourfold-region address.

Saved-link offset 0x44 receives the doubled link. An explicitly unsigned byte at fourfold-region offset 0x05 selects a base whose offset-0x74 word supplies another link. That link’s offset-0x44 word receives the doubled link, then later a zero-extended byte from a different region.

The selected link’s low byte is propagated into offset-0x0D fields of the fourfold region and a link loaded from its offset 0x64. Those regions also receive self-reference words at offset 0x14.

A word at fourfold-region offset 0x04 supplies the **intermediate base**. Its offset-0x05 unsigned byte is read twice, with intervening stores through the fourfold region, doubled link, and selected link. The first read supplies the widened word mentioned above. The second selects another offset-0x74 word load, producing the **terminal link**. These two byte reads must not be merged.

Before the constant-writing phase, additional byte and word stores restore combinations of the intermediate base, doubled link, fourfold-region address, and eight-times-saved-link value. In particular, fourfold-region offset 0x14 receives eight times the saved link, its offset 0x54 receives four times the saved link, and its offset 0x09 receives the low byte of twice the terminal link.

### Closing snapshots and global assignments

Twice the terminal link defines the **closing region**.

The closing suffix first reads global 0xA9 into a 32-bit **fixed-global snapshot**, then reads the word at offset 0x74 from four times the terminal link as the **terminal-field snapshot**.

The closing region receives the publication region’s low byte at offset 0x0D. Publication-region offset 0x08 receives byte 0x6C, and its offsets 0x0D and 0x09 receive the global-held base’s low byte. Closing-region offset 0x54 receives its own address.

Global 0x67 is then assigned the eight-bit conversion of the fixed-global snapshot. A word is read from closing-region offset 0x44 before a fresh read of global 0x67 supplies the assignment to global 0x21. Neither global assignment establishes the destination’s width.

The offset-0x40 word then overwrites publication-region offset 0x10, covering the preceding byte store. Global-held-base offset 0x44 receives the closing-region address.

## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static-review examples, not executed tests.

1. **Explicit unsigned-byte propagation.** An explicitly read scaled-region offset-0x05 byte of 0xE7 supplies word value 0x000000E7 at offset 0x34.

2. **Global 0x05 is different.** If its missing declaration is a signed eight-bit quantity holding −1, conversion to 32-bit unsigned produces 0xFFFFFFFF, not 255. If instead it is unsigned eight-bit holding 0xFF, the result is 255. Neither declaration is established.

3. **Index boundary.** Under 32-bit bit-vector arithmetic, index-source low seven bits of 0x7F produce index 508 and index-region address 1016.

4. **Assignment value versus footprint.** With index-region address 0x2F8, globals 0x01 and 0x15 are supplied eight-bit value 0xF8. Acceptance must not require one-byte writes without their declarations.

5. **Global-read ordering.** If the global-held-base source overlaps global 0x60, the saved base comes from the preceding read, not a reload after assigning 0x20.

6. **Fresh global observations.** If global 0x67 freshly yields 0x01CD before an object-relative byte copy, that copy stores 0xCD. The global-to-global assignment to 0x21 cannot be assumed to perform the same truncation.

7. **Ordered overwrite.** Publication-region offset 0x20 receives all four specified word stores. Its final value alone does not satisfy access-sensitive acceptance.

8. **Retained final argument.** A later mutation of closing-region offset 0x14 does not change the final transfer’s third argument.
