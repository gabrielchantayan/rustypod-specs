### Arithmetic contract

Let \(N\) be the unsigned interpretation of the 32-bit field at offset `0x8E8`. The calculated argument is:

\[
Q = 4 \times \left\lfloor
\frac{(N + 31)\bmod 2^{32}}{32}
\right\rfloor
\]

The important width and ordering rules are:

- The addition of 31 is unsigned 32-bit arithmetic.
- Overflow wraps modulo \(2^{32}\).
- The right shift is applied to that unsigned result, so it is a logical shift.
- Multiplication by four occurs after the division-by-32 effect of the shift.

After the right shift, the value is at most `0x07FFFFFF`. The final result is therefore at most `0x1FFFFFFC`; the final left shift does not overflow 32 bits.

For \(0 \le N \le \texttt{0xFFFFFFE0}\), this agrees with rounding \(N\) upward to a multiple of 32 units and assigning four units to each resulting group. For larger \(N\), addition wraps first. Ordinary unbounded “round up” arithmetic would not preserve this behavior.

There is no visible rejection of negative signed inputs. A field whose signed interpretation is negative still participates through its unsigned 32-bit representation.
