# F-8e9b374e50fb

## Purpose

The prepared state contains two scaled forms of the explicit input, additional locations populated from those stored values, and four cleared regions.

If that target returns with usable continuation state, the function clears two more regions and passes a subsequently acquired external datum to F-cdec09f5d432. A suitable return from that dependency leads to an operation designated non-returning.

The established contract is partial initialization and conditional continuation. It does not establish a complete object, an application-level success condition, an ownership transfer, or an ordinary return value.

| 0x060, 0x0A8, 0x0E4, and 0x1B8 | Four zero bytes at each location |

The integer entries specify numerical contents, not byte encodings.

The additional integer locations are storage-dependent. The value at 0x024 comes from the primary lower-scaled location. The values at 0x04C and 0x188 come from separate uses of the primary higher-scaled location; the latter is not established as a copy of the former.

Consequently, equality among the higher-scaled locations requires the relevant source and destination contents to remain effective through preparation. This observation does not establish a supported concurrent-access mechanism.

### Continuation after the first target

Continuation depends on F-a7d2319046bc returning with usable execution state.

Before the next invocation, the four-byte region at 0x008 is cleared, followed by the four-byte region at 0x100. The external datum is acquired after both clearings and supplied to F-cdec09f5d432.

The acquisition is late: an entry-time snapshot is not the established behavior. If the first target changes the datum and that change remains effective until acquisition, the changed value is the one supplied.

The resulting argument cannot be determined without its width, representation, and overlap extent. Partial overlap, in particular, does not justify claiming a zero-valued argument.

Continuation does not explicitly repeat preparation or restore the five integer locations. Changes made by the first target may remain effective wherever later local effects do not replace them.

The prepared base is not an explicit argument to F-cdec09f5d432. That absence does not establish that the dependency lacks access to the storage through an alias, a retained address, or shared state.
