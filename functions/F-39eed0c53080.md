## Behavior

2. Read the same scalar global again.

4. Write the first reading plus 84 into the 32-bit field at offset 40.

There is no intervening explicit call between these operations.

The two fields therefore do not necessarily share a common captured source. For example, first and second readings of 100 and 200 respectively establish writes of 184 at offset 40 and 200 at offset 0.
