# F-d29299532367

## Purpose

The established behavior is the sequence of calls, address calculations, reads, writes, and iteration-state updates described below. No higher-level purpose—such as allocation, serialization, dispatch, or device initialization—is established. - **Lₙ(x)** is the low 32 bits after shifting a 32-bit value left by *n* bits.
- **B(x)** is the low 8 bits.

- A **word access** transfers 32 bits.
