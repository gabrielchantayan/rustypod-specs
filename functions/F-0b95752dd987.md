These are conditional static acceptance criteria, not executed tests. Each assumes execution reaches the relevant event and the required accesses are permitted.

1. **Cumulative third-input shift.**

4. **Repeated stores remain observable.**  
   If K is 3, W17 writes 12 at `0x44`. F-X01 through F-X05 then occur, followed by W18 writing 48 at the same retained address.

5. **Fresh selector selection.**  
   If the first selector reads `0x20` for B1 and `0x80` for B2, their destinations are `0x31` and `0x91`. Reusing B1’s selector byte for B2 would not conform.

6. **Byte truncation.**  
   If M is `0x41`, B1 stores `0x04`. If M is `0x3FFFFFFF`, B2 stores `0xFF`, although its pre-truncation shifted quantity is `0x3FF`.

7. **Adjacent-store aliasing.**  
   Suppose B3’s destination and the first selector location are both `0x1C`, and B3 writes `0x41`. With no intervening external mutation, B4 then reads `0x41` and stores zero at `0x52`.

8. **Loop target and argument transition.**
