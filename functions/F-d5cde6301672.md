# F-d5cde6301672

It obtains an additional opaque value, selects one of two static text references, forwards the caller’s inputs to a configuration-like dependency, and makes two further calls using the acquired value.

The selector has two distinct roles:

The established behavior is this selection and call sequence. The interpretation that the function configures a database schema operation is inferred from the apparent text names, not established by the dependency implementations.
