# F-dfe7b459913f

## Purpose

Insertion depends on a nonzero record field. The insertion path computes a 32-bit value using that field and an external result, then chooses a position through an opaque comparison dependency. A common final phase follows a global holder-to-pointer chain and may invoke an indirect target.

Global values are read at their respective points of use. Their immutability is not established, and intervening dependencies may change them.
