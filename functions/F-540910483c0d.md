# F-540910483c0d

## Purpose

The established behavior is the sequence of those three calls, including the argument storage exposed to the dependencies. The semantic meanings of the processed value, the selected object operation, and the finalization operation are not established.

This interpretation is not an ownership guarantee: the supplied evidence does not establish that any dependency allocates, retains, transfers, or releases memory.

This distinction does not prevent broader effects. An opaque value may itself encode an address, and dependencies may have access to unrelated memory or shared state.

The evidence establishes separate addressable slots. It does not provide a contract for their relative addresses, adjacency, alignment beyond what the calls require, or surrounding stack layout.
