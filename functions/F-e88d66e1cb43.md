## Acceptance examples

These are conditional static-review examples, not executed tests.

1. **Extraction boundaries.** Third-input values 0–1023 produce an extracted field of zero. Value 1024 produces one. Value 0x000FFC00 produces 1023 and duplicated shifted arguments of 0x001FF800.

2. **Excluded bits.** Inputs 0x00000400 and 0xFFF00401 produce the same early field. This does not establish whole-function equivalence through unknown dependencies.

3. **Explicit byte extension.** If a represented unsigned-byte read at a derived offset 5 observes 0xFE, the corresponding zero-extended word store receives 254.

4. **Absolute location 5.** The previous rule cannot be applied to the undeclared symbolic global at location 5. Its converted base must not be restricted to 0–255 solely from the available evidence.

5. **Character conversion precedes arithmetic.** Suppose the adjusted quantity is 254. Under an unsigned eight-bit plain-character interpretation, the promoted arithmetic result for the 0x3A assignment is 2080. Under a signed eight-bit interpretation whose conversion maps 254 to −2, it is 32. The destination declaration is needed to determine the stored result and access width.

6. **Conditional low-byte equivalence.** Under those same character assumptions, explicit eight-bit truncation of either result in the preceding example yields 0x20. This equality does not prove that the actual global assignment truncates to eight bits.

7. **Separate 0x67 observations.** If the three post-assignment reads observe 0x11, 0x22, and 0x33, the first supplies the absolute-0x21 assignment and the next two supply the calculated offset-9 byte stores.

8. **Intervening alias.** If a store between two reads of 0x67 overlaps that global, the later read observes the applicable post-store state. Identical source expressions do not establish identical observations.

9. **Overwritten byte.** Under ordinary noninterfering memory, the later loaded-address-fragment store determines the calculated offset-9 byte after both 0x67-supplied writes.

10. **Width distinction.** The explicit 16-bit store of 0x003A affects two bytes. Their order depends on endianness.

11. **Partial failure.** A late inaccessible destination does not require reversal of earlier completed writes.

12. **Terminal arguments.** If the terminal transfer is reached, its first displayed argument is 0x72 and its third is the final-region saved word, not a presumed zero from an earlier clear.
