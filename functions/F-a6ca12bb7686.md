## Side effects

- One four-byte read from a fixed data location.
- Three writes to the supplied destination, in the order specified above.
- Three dependency calls.
- One final four-byte write through a derived address.

Bytes 9–15 are not directly written by this function, but they are not guaranteed unchanged across the entire call. That stronger claim would require constraints on dependency behavior and aliasing.
